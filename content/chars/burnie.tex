\subsubsection{Background}

Radburny ``Burnie'' Cinders is a human raised by dwarven parents on a farm, who went on to become a Book Burner --- one of the Church's agents tasked with hunting down and destroying heretical texts.  Unlike many of his colleagues, Burnie survived the great Book Burner purges that destroyed the organisation, though the experience left deep scars.

What few realised was that Burnie had been the de facto \emph{leader} of the Book Burners --- a position he inherited in his teens after the previous leader was killed off in an earlier purge.  He rebuilt the organisation from the ashes, spending his career carefully balancing undermining the Church while keeping them happy, and trading in secrets that earned him respect among the movers and shakers of Hope's Rest.  Pilch and Lillian were two of his favoured recruits, having risen through the ranks under his leadership.  He was romantically involved with fellow Book Burner Martha Mayfire, and her death in the purge shocked him to his core.

After the purge, Burnie was captured by Arbiter Killjoy and hung by the Church in the centre of Hope's Rest as a warning to all others.  He faked his death --- how, exactly, he has never satisfactorily explained --- and his colourful subsequent career included a stint at the University of Assassins, where he rose to a teaching position.  His personal life was equally eventful --- he fathered a child with Trayvon, a Tengu Book Burner colleague, producing a ``grossly disfigured'' half-human, half-Tengu son who grew up harbouring a deep hatred for his absent father.  The two eventually reconciled after the son attempted to kill Burnie, leading to ``a beautiful reconciliation'' where father and son ``quickly became best friends, making up for lost time''.

\subsubsection{Personality and Traits}

Burnie is a grouchy old man who is actually quite friendly underneath.  He has a talent for getting into trouble and an even greater talent for somehow surviving it.  His combat style is eclectic and unpredictable, wielding his sword Wasabi, a stone dagger capable of softening and reshaping stone on contact, and an assortment of hooks, grapples, and improvised weapons.

He has an unfortunate tendency to attract possession --- Mark O'Synne became trapped in his arm, trying to control his actions and prevent dangerous decisions.  Burnie's response to having a god lodged in his forearm was characteristically unfazed.  His missing eye was replaced with IRIS, a jive-talking AI system capable of shooting lasers and providing tactical targeting data.

In a fight Burnie is a Battlemastermind --- a defensive sword-and-board tactician who cultivates being underestimated, analysing enemies to their detriment and his allies' benefit, and hitting remarkably hard provided his target is looking the other way.

Burnie retains a deep and primal connection to his Book Burner roots.  When surrounded by forbidden texts, he is occasionally overcome by episodes of book-burning mania, entering a trance-like state of righteous destruction.

\subsubsection{Augments and Arsenal}

After being hit with a pillar of fire, Burnie lost his left arm, right leg and left eye.  Exme rebuilt him --- by the end of the campaign he was, in his own estimation, more machine than man:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Electric Circuitry} --- full-body wiring that serves as a platform for his other augments and grants minor resistance to lightning.
  \item \textbf{Shock Hook} --- a left-arm replacement salvaged from a pair of gloves made from a lightning beast.  Delivers staggering shock damage, and can also stimulate healing, cauterise wounds and stabilise the dying.
  \item \textbf{Darkvision Eye} --- sees in the dark, but is semi-blinded in daylight; hence the eyepatch.
  \item \textbf{Powered Pegleg} --- a sprung right leg housing a motion-driven generator and battery.  He needs to run about a hundred feet to fully charge the hook.
\end{itemize}

His arsenal was equally eccentric: \textbf{Dermal Armor}, a skin-tight suit equivalent to half-plate worn under his clothing, originally made for a rich noble; the \textbf{Multibow}, a pump-action rotating-chamber Church crossbow of near-ancient, pre-gunpowder design; and the \textbf{Cover-Shield}, a heater shield that stabs into the ground and extends mechanical legs to create five feet of half-cover, redeployable at a moment's notice.

\subsubsection{Relationships}

Burnie's son --- originally called Trayvon Jr, later renamed Buck McCraw --- was raised by the assassin Bones after Burnie abandoned him.  Half-human, half-Tengu, and hideously disfigured, Buck trained at the Guild of Assassins and initially tried to kill Burnie before the two reconciled over a shared understanding of loss.  Buck went on to introduce Kevin, a final-year PhD student from the University of Assassins, as Burnie's mentee --- a relationship characterised by its ``unhealthily abusive'' dynamic.

Burnie's adoptive dwarven parents Gerald and his wife still live on their farm outside Hope's Rest, and eventually joined the airship crew as babysitters for Riphard's orphaned twins.

Within the party, Burnie formed a close bond with Kolo --- Exme entrusted the Puzzle Die to Burnie after her departure, with a note reading ``I thought I could have both of you''.  Martin the construct butler became most attached to Burnie of all the party members, and Burnie's gruff mentorship of Kevin echoed the paternal instincts he had failed to exercise with his own son.

\subsubsection{Burnie's Story}

Burnie joined the group after the departure of earlier companions, bringing his Book Burner expertise and grizzled determination to the cause.  His knowledge of the Church's inner workings and his connections to the remnants of the Book Burner network proved invaluable as the party navigated the political complexities of Hope's Rest and beyond.

During the expedition to the ancient dwarven mines, Burnie fought through giant spiders, cloakers, and a horrifying four-armed abomination in the depths, nearly dying when crushed in the creature's claw before being saved by Anthony Tigerius's berserker rage.

When Kolo's corruption culminated in the devastating attack in Hope's Rest's sewers, it was Burnie who sealed the Kalimar summoning pit with his stone dagger, cutting off the source of the elder entity's power.  In the aftermath, Burnie became the reluctant custodian of Exme's Puzzle Die and the emotional anchor for the remaining group as they struggled to process their losses.

Burnie led the group to Linderdorf in search of blacksmiths to forge Excalibrum weapons, and into the Varg wilderness to rescue the missing gnome inventors.  He took Kevin under his wing, and his elderly parents joined the airship to care for Riphard's now-motherless twins.

During the assault on Seven's Spire, Burnie's Book Burner instincts fully took over.  Upon reaching the tower's library levels, he entered a trance of righteous destruction, setting fire to everything in sight and personally killing a sherry-drinking cleric with gleeful abandon.  His stone dagger proved invaluable once again, sealing doorways behind the group as they fought their way upward toward the pinnacle and the Great Stone.
