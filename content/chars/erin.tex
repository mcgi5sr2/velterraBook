\subsubsection{Background}

Sergeant Erin Devereux came to the party four years after the fall of the Seven's Spire, when the world had moved on and the old companions had drifted apart. She hailed from the western carnival city of Jecede, daughter of Calista and Michelle Devereaux, who ran the Midnight Pearl cabaret on its waterfront. A career soldier rather than a treasure hunter, she carried herself with a discipline the rest of the group had never much bothered with.

She was the sole survivor of a reconnaissance team sent north into the Varg lands, a mission that should have killed her with the rest. By her own account she lived only because her friend Reggie spent his last breath in a sacrifice that moved the distant gods to spare her, a debt she never stopped trying to repay and never quite believed she deserved. Lazarus, now ruling Hell as the Empire of the Eight, folded her into the regathered party as their first new recruit.

\subsubsection{Personality and Traits}

Erin was the straight edge against which the party's chaos broke. Where the others schemed and bickered, she wrote mission reports, kept her glaive sharp, and tried to hold a soldier's line in a company of lunatics. Episode 82 is told entirely in her dry, clipped field-report voice, a tone that says everything about how she saw her new colleagues.

Beneath the discipline she was unravelling. Survivor's guilt over Reggie sat heavy on her from the start, and somewhere in the Varg lands an intrusive inner voice took root, growing colder and more contemptuous of the party and the gods alike with every episode. By the time the party tracked Valkar north she had become convinced that everyone around her was a fool, a corruption she fought quietly and largely alone.

\subsubsection{Relationships}

As the newcomer, Erin spent much of her early time taking the measure of the group, wary of their methods and slow to trust their competence. The girls' night aboard the airship, where she finally spoke about Reggie to Exme and Martin, was one of her few moments of open feeling. Her bond was less with any one member than with the idea of the mission itself, and later with her home city, whose armies she came to command.

After Exme's erasure, when Kolo stood on deck in his new construct body mourning a sister he had only just gotten back, Erin stood listening, facing away, having a quiet cry with her ``best gal pals 4eva'' cloak whipping in the wind.  Then she turned to the tin goblin she had never met and said, ``Well zen Kolo, I don't know you.  But I knew her.  And if you're anything like 'er, then this is going to be interesting.  We'd best not let 'er down, no?''

\subsubsection{Erin's Story}

Erin earned her place in the warm-up against the lich Valklondar, then followed the party into the Varg lands to infiltrate the Chesney Hawks tribe. She survived being swallowed whole by a guardian naga, endured the steady poison of her inner voice through the long march north, and stood with the party as Kalimar fell.

When Canalon descended and the war moved west to her home, Erin came into her own. The Jecede council made her head of all its armies, the highest she had ever risen. It did not last. When a githyanki Nautiloid attacked the city, the party boarded it, and an Ulitharid mind flayer cut her down and devoured her brain. The episode that records her death is titled, with the group's usual gallows humour, ``the one where Erin Dies''. Myron took up her fallen glaive and split the Ulitharid in two with it, and her body was burned on a pyre alongside Burnie's a few days later.  In his report, Myron granted her posthumous awards: the Hardstone Cross for outstanding valour in the face of overwhelming adversity, the Garmond Cross for most conspicuous compassion in circumstances of extreme danger, and the Haremxe Cross for ``best gal pal during extreme bestiness 4eva.''
