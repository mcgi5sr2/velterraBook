\subsection*{Details} 

\noindent

Race: 	Goblin \\
Class: 	Artificer \\
Age: 	8 \\
Status: Erased from existence (ep 97)

\subsubsection{Background}

Exmerah ``Exme" Sliokzog was born on her feet two minutes before her brother, Kolo, in the barren wastelands of ``the North".  Growing up as a Goblin was not easy and the two young siblings spent their days being chased by wild animals, causing mischief, and being enslaved by evil overlords.  

Naturally ingenious from birth, Exme took the opportunity to learn all that she could from her captors, rising quickly up through the ranks until she became a full-time workshop assistant.  During this period Exme made several inexplicable advances in the field of electromagnetism, culminating in the production of Velterra's first electric gun.  

The two goblins were taken out of their death by Lazarus whilst launching an audacious escape attempt from their Northen stronghold.  

\subsubsection{Personality and Traits}

Exme was always the more softly spoken of the twins, usually happy to follow Kolo's lead and revel in their mischief.  Having seen all of their family and friends enslaved at a young age, both Goblins developed a burning passion for emancipation and dreamed of a free world.  Whilst Kolo took the stance that emancipation could only be gained by the sharp edge of a blade and that the only good human was a dead one, Exme always recognised that the races could work together towards the brighter future that they both craved.

What set Exme apart from every other member of the group was her inventive genius.  She built S.T.A.N.R.I from scrap metal and a leaking human brain, designed C.E.D.R.I.C, the ``Collaborative Experimental Destruction and Rapid Immolation Contraption,'' a sentient electric railgun with ``a funny voice that makes me go all trembly'', and unlocked dozens of functions within the Puzzle Die of Ruh'Breks that nobody else could fathom.  Her rifle worked entirely without gunpowder, which she dismissed as ``an inferior method of propulsion'', and she brewed a healing salve that temporarily bolstered those who consumed it.  She was always keen to talk to other tinkerers and scholars, forming quick friendships with Ruh'Breks Sensei, Konrad the robot-maker, Professor Jorge Myron, and Google Von Maccherstein's gnome engineers.

Following her maturity into a goblin woman and the loss of Kolo, Exme became more adult and took up casual smoking, though she still retained a sense of naivety due to her relative lack of world experience.  She discovered that dogs are men and cats are women, a finding that single-handedly advanced the field of anthropology in Velterra by decades.

Whilst confident in her abilities and place in the world, Exme was known to act with extreme jealousy in the presence of other women, especially those with any connection to Myron.  

\subsubsection{Relationships}

Exme's main bond has and always will be to her younger twin brother Kolo.  Prior to his death the two had never spent more than an hour apart, and the loss led Exme to re-examine her life and discover who she was by herself.  Her final note to Burnie when she left the group, ``I thought I could have both of you'', was addressed as much to Kolo's memory as to anyone living.  In the end, when the wish came and she had to choose between Kolo and Myron, she chose both, and paid for it with everything she was.

Myron and Exme have been involved in an ongoing ``will they, won't they'' relationship, perhaps best outlined in her self-published novelette ``Love Doesn't Have a Height Limit.''  The relationship survived Masuda, survived petrification, but nearly didn't survive the Hope's Rest sewers.  When Myron told her ``This is all your fault,'' it destroyed something between them.  Her unsent letters to him during her absence suggested the feelings never died, they just grew more complicated.  By the continuation, the two had found their way back far enough to adopt a son together, a teenage tailor called David, and to run the Delilah Hardstone School side by side.

S.T.A.N.R.I was more than a creation, he was Exme's constant companion, evolving from a small leaking robo-cub to a fully operational combat unit to a tiger-form battlesuit.  The tiger redesign honoured Toni, and in mecha-form Exme climbed inside STANRI through what can only be described as ``his vagina'' to become the fearsome ``Stanrexme.''  She was moved to tears upon examining Otoria's inner workings, recognising the exquisite craftsmanship of a fellow artificer's creation.

Exme cared deeply about her companions and mourned the loss of each one, keeping memories of their remains and honouring fallen comrades through the naming of her creations.  

\subsubsection{Exme's Story}

Starting off as a timid young goblin girl, Exme soon began to find her feet and became one of the most fearsome fighters the world had ever seen.  She helped clear the Temple of Unthala, survived the Die Hard heist at the Tower of Imota Kan, where she discovered that Lady Otoria was not human but an exquisitely crafted automaton, and served as the group's technical backbone through every campaign that followed.

In Masuda, Exme upgraded STANRI into a mecha-battlesuit that could transform around her, turning the pair into a devastating combat unit.  She built C.E.D.R.I.C, the electric railgun that would go on to vaporise Arbiter Khan's head in the final campaign, and mastered the Puzzle Die of Ruh'Breks, unlocking function after function of the ancient artefact that nobody else could begin to understand.

The catastrophe in the Hope's Rest sewers broke Exme in a way that nothing else had.  She stopped time with the Puzzle Die to rescue Delilah's twin babies, revived the fallen Myron with the healing shot she had intended for Delilah, and watched helplessly as Riphard strangled her brother.  When it was over, she tried to talk Riphard out of it, she told him it would not bring Delilah back, that Kolo was sick, that if he did this they had lost Kolo forever.  ``But he had already made his mind up.  I think he had made his mind up a long time ago.''

Exme left the group carrying Kolo's corpse, leaving Burnie the Puzzle Die with a note: ``I thought I could have both of you.''  During her absence, she founded the ``Delilah Hardstone Engineering School for Talented Yung Wimun'' and wrote unsent letters to Myron.  She rejoined the party at Linderdorf, quieter and harder than before.

It was Exme who unlocked the Puzzle Die's ability to locate the source of the seal, sitting alone in her cabin late at night, rotating the cube as objects in the room disappeared and time slowed around her, until the pulse came and she knew: Seven's Spire.  She later plugged the cube into the airship's ghost engine, achieving hyper-speed travel that allowed the party to cross Velterra in days rather than weeks.  For the Festival of Light infiltration, she disguised herself as Otoria, her actual face, eyebrows adjusted, and covered STANRI in a thin layer of meat and Burnie's hair to create a convincing tiger disguise, crawling inside through STANRI's entry point for travel.

Exme redesigned STANRI as a tiger in honour of Toni, and the gnome engineers of Linderdorf helped her incorporate Excalibrum into the build, turning him into ``Stanri 2: Electric Baloogaloo.''

Inside Seven's Spire, Exme was instrumental at every level, disguising herself as Lillith to bluff past guards, making STANRI invisible through a divine cloak of light refraction, and creating an interplanar pocket dimension to shelter the rescued prisoners.  When Arbiter Khan returned with his Inquisitor Ravagers, it was Exme's linear accelerator that vaporised his head, and STANRI's mechanical foot that finished the job.  As the party disguised themselves as Inquisitors and prepared to ascend toward the pinnacle, Exme stood ready to fulfil the promise she had carried since Kolo's death, to break the seal, return the gods to the world, and find a way to free her brother's soul from Kalimar's grip.

\subsubsection{The Continuation}

Four years after the Spire fell and the gods returned, Exme was a broken drunk.  She had poured everything into two obsessions: running the Delilah Hardstone School For Exceptionally Gifted Girls To Excel In Engineering And Emancipation, and finding a way to free Kolo's soul from Kalimar.  She and Myron had adopted David, a teenage tailor who made the school uniforms, and on the surface life had moved on.  Underneath, Exme was drinking herself to death and spending every waking moment researching the elder entity that held her brother.

When Lazarus regathered the party to deal with the Varg super-soldier threat, Exme went not for the mission but for Kolo.  Valkar Varg had made a pact with Kalimar, and where Kalimar was, Kolo's soul was.  She carried a vial of the Kalimel goo she hoped could cure her brother, and she read his mouldy old notebook aboard the airship like scripture.

In the Varg lands, Exme befriended Aracia and her spider Zarathrustra among the Adam-ants tribe, froze time to heal the group when slaads nearly killed them, and kept the mammoth eggs from a hunt she participated in with characteristic scientific curiosity.  But her focus never wavered.  When the party finally reached Kalimar's lair, a temple carved into a giant ice sheet descending through ratman hives, fungal forests, and flesh tunnels to a lava sanctum, Exme went invisible and rigged the elder god's spawn-egg hatcheries with dynamite.

In the battle that followed, she detonated the caverns.  Kalimar, the Elder God of Creation, turned his full fury on the party.  Riphard, Martin, Erin, and Exme all fell.  Burnie was swallowed whole.  Myron was stabbed through the chest and thrown dead from Kalimar's shoulder.  Mrs Ball delivered her biggest ever bonk, punching clean through the god and killing him.  But the cost was already paid.  Burnie, sustained only by the power of Synne, dragged the fallen back from the edge, but Myron was beyond revivify.  He was critically injured, paralysed, and dying.

The Puzzle Die of Ruh'Breks held one function that had never been used: Wish, with a ten-thousand-year cooldown.  The only wish in existence.  Exme could save Kolo, whose soul was now free among the tens of thousands that had been trapped inside Kalimar.  Or she could save Myron, who lay broken beside her.  She could not choose.

She wished for both.

The wish was accepted.  Myron lived.  Kolo was restored in a constructed body, walking and talking and alive.  But because the ask was so extreme, the price was absolute: Exmerah Sliokzog was erased from all reality.  Not killed.  Not sent to Hell or to the gods.  Erased.  She could never be resurrected, could never become a god, could never exist in any afterlife on any plane.  She was simply gone, as though she had never been, except that the things she had built and the people she had changed remained.

The party discovered she was gone when Kolo woke in his new metal body and Myron opened his eyes.  Bronwen, a devil sent by Lazarus, arrived to confirm what had happened.  Kolo found Exme's last gift to him: a mechanical bow with a cabled power pack, left where he would find it.  Her school endured, now headed by Professor Stanri, the mecha-bear she had built from scrap and a leaking brain, who had been gifted consciousness by the gods and now stood at the front of a classroom teaching the next generation of gifted girls.

\begin{DndSidebar}{The Rumour of Mrs Ball}
After Exme's erasure, her player Jonathan suggested that she may not have been destroyed entirely.  Mrs Ball and her kind each contain a universe within themselves, and Mrs Ball's stated purpose is the quiet restoration of broken realities.  It is whispered, in circles that have no business whispering such things, that Exme was not erased but moved, placed within the reality inside Mrs Ball, alive in a universe of one.  This has never been confirmed.  It may be a kindness.  It may be the truth.  The party never found out.
\end{DndSidebar}

\begin{DndSidebar}{Exme's Creations}
\textit{S.T.A.N.R.I}: From leaking robo-cub to mecha-tiger battlesuit. Named after Grumpy Stanri, the legendary goblin emancipator. Gifted consciousness by the gods after the seal was broken.  After Exme's erasure, Stanri took over as headmaster of the Delilah Hardstone School, teaching the next generation in her place.

\textit{C.E.D.R.I.C}: ``Collaborative Experimental Destruction and Rapid Immolation Contraption.''  Sentient electric railgun.  Improves perception, retargets shots, has a funny voice.  Vaporised Arbiter Khan's head.

\textit{The Detectomatic}: Portable magic detection device.  Invaluable for identifying artefacts, traps, and Martin's suspicious magical readings.
\end{DndSidebar}
