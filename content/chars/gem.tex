\section{Gem}

\subsection*{Details}

\noindent

Player: Natalie Southernden \\
Race: 	Silicate (spacefarer) \\
Class: 	Psychic archer \\
Age: 	Over 500 \\
Status: Active

\subsubsection{Background}

Gem was found in a holding cell aboard the githyanki Nautiloid, a prisoner of the same enemy the party had come to fight. She is a woman of living silicate, calm and faintly amused, who has spent more than five centuries travelling between the stars. Her people, she explained, were created at the very moment Canalon was cast out of the heavens, which made the elder dragon's return a matter of more than passing interest to her.

\subsubsection{Personality and Traits}

Gem carries herself with the unhurried confidence of someone who has seen far more than the world she has dropped into. She is, by her own description, an observer, bound by some protocol of her people not to interfere, and she joined the party precisely by breaking that rule. Her weapon is a bow that folds neatly into a violin, and she rounds out her marksmanship with psychic powers that the party found endlessly useful, not least when she polymorphed a lightning-wreathed bank Arcanist into a harmless clockwork bobbin.

\subsubsection{Relationships}

Coming aboard so late, Gem attached herself to the survivors as a fellow outsider with her own reasons for wanting Canalon dead. She took quickly to the party's stranger members, befriending a telepathic sewer octopod named Badunkadonk beneath Hope's Rest, an alliance nearly undone when Kolo, true to form, took a shot at the creature.

\subsubsection{Gem's Story}

Freed from her cell during the storming of the Nautiloid, Gem fought her way out alongside the party through githyanki and mind flayers, and chose to stay once the ship went down. She rode the Jecede railway west with the remnant of the group, helped see off the Inquisitors who attacked across the train roof, and was among the party that descended into the Black Rock vault to claim the Armoury of Sin for the coming battle with the god who had cast her makers adrift.
