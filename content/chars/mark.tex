\subsubsection{Background}

Mark O'Synne is a manifest avatar of divine power --- a fragment of the god Synne given form.  He first appeared as a golden-haired, small figure who emerged from Gary's chest after the party arrived on the jungle continent, though he had previously been bound to Pilcheur's forearm as the ``Mark of Synne''.

Mark claims to be working to prevent the ``Sundering'' --- a catastrophic divine collapse that threatens the world.  He was once far more powerful, but divine power has waned over the centuries since the Church sealed the world from the gods.

\subsubsection{Personality and Traits}

Mark is, by universal agreement of the party, a dickhead.  Meddlesome, condescending, and possessed of an infuriating tendency to appear at the worst possible moments with cryptic advice nobody asked for, Mark nonetheless provides genuinely useful luck-bending abilities that have saved the group on multiple occasions.

He can create multiple copies of himself --- Lark, Jark, Sark, and Shark --- each distinguished by different colours, though the practical benefit of this ability remains debatable.

\subsubsection{Relationships}

Mark was initially bound to Pilch, whom he described as ``the human I hated the least''.  After Pilch's death, he transferred to Gary, and later became trapped within Burnie's arm, where he spent much of his time trying to prevent the party from making catastrophically dangerous decisions --- with limited success.

The group's feelings towards Mark are best summarised by his character sheet notes: Background --- ``Fucking Mark''.  Personality --- ``Dickhead''.

\subsubsection{Mark's Story}

Mark's journey has been one of diminishing power and increasing desperation.  As a fragment of Synne, he represents the last active connection between the mortal world and the divine.  His time in Hell's management training programme revealed a more sympathetic side --- using his luck-bending abilities to help others rather than himself.

Despite being universally disliked by the party, Mark's powers and knowledge have proven indispensable in the quest to break the seal and return the gods to the world.
