\subsubsection{Background}

Mark O'Synne is a manifest avatar of divine power --- a fragment of the god Synne given form.  Trapped in a book for what seemed like an eternity, he was awoken by Pilcheur Gamont, and in his weakened state attached himself to Pilch as a tattoo --- the ``Mark of Synne'' on his forearm.  He first appeared in person as a golden-haired, small figure who emerged from Gary's chest after the party arrived on the jungle continent.

Mark claims to be working to prevent the ``Sundering'' --- a catastrophic divine collapse that threatens the world.  He was once far more powerful, but divine power has waned over the centuries since the Church sealed the world from the gods.

\subsubsection{Personality and Traits}

Mark is, by universal agreement of the party, a dickhead.  Meddlesome, condescending, and possessed of an infuriating tendency to appear at the worst possible moments with cryptic advice nobody asked for, Mark nonetheless provides genuinely useful luck-bending abilities that have saved the group on multiple occasions.

He can create multiple copies of himself --- Lark, Jark, Sark, and Shark --- each distinguished by different colours, though the practical benefit of this ability remains debatable.  He is also rather partial to trebuchet-enabled flight.

\subsubsection{Relationships}

Mark was initially bound to Pilch, whom he described as ``the human I hated the least''.  After Pilch's death, he transferred to Gary --- who acted as a father to help him through the hard times, to the point that the party records describe Mark as ``possibly the son of Gary (Peace be Upon Him)''.  For a time the monkey Vu Dong served as Mark's champion; unsatisfied, Mark orchestrated a bizarre scenario to trade in ``that filthy monkey, with his stinky arm'' for the more reliable Burnie Cinders.  He thus became trapped within Burnie's arm, where he spent much of his time trying to prevent the party from making catastrophically dangerous decisions --- with limited success.

His relationship with Meredith, Lazarus's elephant butler, began as that of a jilted love interest --- she stood him up on prom night --- but the two were eventually reconciled.

The group's feelings towards Mark are best summarised by his character sheet notes: Background --- ``Fucking Mark''.  Personality --- ``Dickhead''.

\subsubsection{Mark's Story}

Mark's journey has been one of diminishing power and increasing desperation.  As a fragment of Synne, he represents the last active connection between the mortal world and the divine.  His time in Hell's management training programme revealed a more sympathetic side --- using his luck-bending abilities to help others rather than himself.

Mark's most significant contribution was the construction of a magical VR storm machine aboard the airship, which allowed the party to inhabit the bodies of Book Burner recruits decades in the past.  These flashback episodes revealed the true history of the Book Burner purge and Pilch's role in it, while also introducing Burnie to the group when the time-travel process accidentally erased Vu Dong from the timeline.

Despite being universally disliked by the party, Mark's powers and knowledge have proven indispensable in the quest to break the seal and return the gods to the world.  He has a long and complicated history with Meredith, Lazarus's elephant butler, who once stood him up on prom night.
