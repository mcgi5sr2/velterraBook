\subsection{Episode 100: The game we played when the queen died.}

\DndDropCapLine{W}e begin on the airship. With Myron in the bowels of the ship with an apocalyptic hangover. Having drunk himself into a stupor in an attempt to contact Lazarus so he can see his father. He stumbles through cans of Bovril, lights a cigarette and realises its not him thats swaying its the ship. Its floating on water! Getting to the top deck he meets a puzzled David, who's obviously been crying. " I thought you'd left?" "I have, see ya" ignoring his grieving son and he stomping down the plank.\medskip

He walks the streets, observing the city. He's on his way to his favourite tobacconist, Jerode.He hears a mild cufuffel some streets away, shouting at first, then steel on steel. He chooses to keep on his way. A wordless exchange between himself and Jerode is completed with the precious tobacco acquired. Heading back to where the sounds came from earlier he sees the tail end of a mugging. And then carries on his wandering.\medskip

Meanwhile in le pearl minuit. Our party of misfit heros awaken in various states. The cabaret is quiet, but breakfast is on! A runner has left a message confirming a council meeting. We swap stories. Bronwen is hungover and deeply suspicious of whats going. Kolo has attempted to sample all the items on the buffet and is bereft that he's unable to eat.\medskip

Breakfast complete they set out towards the council chambers. Yesterday's festival decorations are being taken down to make way for today's festival. The are lots of street vendors. We bump into Myron.\medskip

Burnie notices a note tucked into his jacket. Its from Riphard, he's looking into somethings with the Jenny's and council meetings are shit anyway.\medskip

We take a meandering route to the council in order to take in the city. We go to a vantage point overlooking the bay, there's a little cafe. Kolo spots a dark elf fisherman and points this out to a nearby dark elf waiter. "Is he going to attack?" "..........no........"\medskip

Bronwen sidles up to Kolo "You go have fun" and casts waterwalk. Kolo bolts for the fisherman. He reacts in alarm as Kolo approaches and tries in vain to row away. Kolo does an impressive flip and lands in the boat. Casting zone of truth.\medskip

"What are you doing?"\medskip

"Its my day off, im relaxing. Can I help you Mr Robot?"\medskip

"It's suspicious"\medskip

"But there are dozens of boats"\medskip

"Yup. Er, what fish do you have?\medskip

""None yet. I just started"\medskip

There's some confusion over the presence of carp. Kolo steals a carp from another fisherman and runs back to Bronwen. He slips the carp into her bag.\medskip

"Sorted"\medskip

We head to the beautifully appointed council chambers. We are shown into the meeting of the 13 councillors.\medskip

We plead our case to the council who agree to mobilise the militia and and the printing presses to warn and equip the populace with knowledge of Canalon.\medskip

Bronwen has an idea on how to look in on Canalon. She plans to scry on her. We head back to the cabaret.Bronwen opens her mind and searches for Canalon, she homes in on Hope's Rest. She sees Canalon sat atop the hall of the guild master. Looking down on multitudes of people thronged in the streets. The eyes on Canalon's body suddenly shift to look at Bronwen. Then Bronwen is snapped back to her body.\medskip

Myron muses over becoming a postman. Bronwen asks Erin to help inspire the others as hope is becoming an issue. But she doesn't know what to say to help them.\medskip

we wallow in collective pity for a time. Bronwen tries to to drag them out of it and then walks away. The rest talk about going to kill the dragon.\medskip

A couple of Jenny's run through the door. "There's something in the sky!" it looks like a comet. Its coming down. It slows down at it gets above the city. It's a Nautiloid! Figures suddenly apparate into the street and it all kicks off. In blur of arrows, shells, sword strikes and hellish imolation they dispatch the assailants\medskip

"Come on we need to get up there!" We gather around Bronwen expectantly waiting for her to cast a helpful spell. "Erm, yeah, sorry. Can't do that." We start running for the airship instead.\medskip
