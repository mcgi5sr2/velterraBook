\subsection{Episode 103: Out of Luck}

\DndDropCapLine{W}e all watched, mouths agape as the goblin ran up the stairs. By himself. Burnie took a moment to celebrate his 400th bloody bowel movement. The sounds of air rushing past the ship provided some small comfort that we had not yet left the atmosphere. We followed him up the stairs. Myron took some time to give me a nickname. He enquired as to why I was here if not to provide some sense of leadership. I tried to explain to him that my my function in existence is meant to strictly be observer, and my presence here was against all protocols, only allowed due to the utmost emergency facing the universe. My god they need leadership though, the recent losses are taking their toll and morale is low.\medskip

Gem became invisible and went ahead to find out what had happened to the little goblin. Though invisible, the mentalist enemies were clearly aware of her presence. She spake into Burnie’s mind. He reported the psychic buggery to us and prompted us to run full speed up the right stairwell. With a knowing look, Myron and I took the other stairs.\medskip

We were greeted immediately by a knight who thrust his sword into Myron, drawing fresh blood. Myron attempted to strike back and managed to come up under his shield to deliver two blows of his own and a shot that raised him up in the air, landing on Erins’ glaive. THe knight exploded with psychic energy, making us all flinch. Burnie ran over and separated the knight from his life. The remaining knights attacked us all with their full fury. Gem focused her fabulous ruby eyes on the knights and spoke into their minds with an alluring and reasonable voice, “You’re all going to die here, why don’t you run away”. Two of the three froze with fear immediately, clearly suggested. Two more beasts ran down the stairs and frightened Burnie. I threw my sword at the one that cast a spell and channeled my healing powers into Myron. He needed to survive this. We all did.\medskip

Myron gritted his teeth and ran at the new assailant. Another psychic blast and Gem dropped to the ground. Burnie ran over and gave her what meager health he could and then writhed in pain from a creature of his fears attacking him from inside his brain. He dodged two attacks from an errant knight. Myron froze in place, unable to teleport. Gem rose to her feet, and finding her support against the wall she let out a psychic scream that exploded one of their brains immediately. In a rage, one hit Burnie with his bare fists, beating him to the ground. I moved past Myron and brought Burnie back to consciousness, but I could not land a strike on an enemy. Myron shrugged off his lack of movement. Another brain explosion and Gem was down again. Burnie was down again. The whole team like yo-yos with a deathwish. I walked past the flappy man and healed Gem. Myron took some pot shots but missed. The last enemy was defeated, although the cowering two knights still remained.\medskip

There was a giant brain in the room above. I tried to throw my sword at it, but it deftly caught it then tried to tentacle me but failed. Myron looked over at the sphere full of knives stuck in the end of the room. “That looks important”. The brain tried to dissuade him from doing anything foolish and asked to take our minds. Myron refused to bargain and started shooting into the brain. Burnie charged himself with lightning and became thicc. We decided the brain had to die. Another burst and Gem and I went down, Myron was stunned. It was up to Burnie. He considered his position. “What was this proposition you had for us?”. “You give up your free will to me and join me in defeating the gods once and for all, all of them”. “Who are you?”, Burnie asked. “I am Kannillon of course” said the voice controlling the brain. Burnie brought me back but not unnoticed. Burnie with no hope left, ran at the brain and attacked with all he could muster. He was furious, Wasabi racing into and across the brainy mass pumping in every single effect Burnie knew. I stood watching, still stunned by the events as the brain grappled Burnie with it’s tentacles. Burnie fought bravely slashing at the brain but ultimately succumbed to the tentacles, nearly dying so ferocious were the attacks. I fought off the stun whilst the brain attacked Burnie. We watched stunned as the brain tore apart Burnie’s body, tearing off his arms and legs, placing his head onto a tentacle. The tentacle head came down the stairs dripping blood, “I’m bored of you now, I’m going to crash the ship”. And with that the brain shut off.\medskip

I stood there staring into the open eyes of Burnie’s severed head as it lay on the floor for what seemed like an eternity. But within moments the gravity shifted and the Nautiloid started it’s rapid descent to earth. I took a breath and got on with the job. I raised Gem up and instructed her to get Kolo. Myron still stood as still as a statue staring at the knife ball with hatred in his eyes. I approached and stood in his line of sight. “You know what has happened. Come on, we have work to do”. We retrieved the head, the hook arm and the lighter that Burnie had treasured so dearly.\medskip

Gem had a plan and called for us all to head to the deck where we came upon the body of Erin. The ship was falling faster now and a fiery glow was starting to appear around the hull. Myron separated from us and built a small pyre onto which he lay Erin’s body and Burnie’s head. He poured out the last of his piece of shit. Over the remains. “It’s not how any of us would have wanted this. But it’s got gravitas hasn’t it.” He used Burnie’s lighter to ignite the pyre which caught quicker than anyone expected. A small red glow appeared and faded with a slow electronic death rattle “daaaisyyyy daaisss—-”.\medskip

I tried to reach Lazarus, to implore him assist us. But he had battles of his own and Cannilon is strong.\medskip

Gem enacted her plan, turning into a giant flying creature familiar to none of us. My own wings erupted from my back and together we left the ship to make our own controlled descent. However, the Nautiloid was on a direct collision course with Jecede. From many miles below, hundreds of thousands of desperate faces looked up to the sky and saw death coming for them in the form of a giant metal fireball. I used the communicator from Burnie’s ear to phone David. At first only static, but as we got closer we made contact. “David you need to get that ship off it’s current course.” Some protest but ultimately the boy knew what had to be done and we soon saw the ship flying in. As we flew down we saw the Good Ship Gary hit the Nautiloid at full throttle, enough to knock it clear off course. A brief moment of elation before the savage distress as we saw our trusty vessel explode from the damage sustained, reduced to nothing. It was just an airship I thought to myself, nothing compared to the lives below.\medskip

A single Jecede fisherman, who had only just got over the shock of being accosted by a strange robotic man had no option but to accept death as his body and boat accepted 20,000 tonnes of alien spaceship.\medskip

We landed in a street in the city just coming to terms with what had happened and the near disaster that could have befallen it. A mix of horror at the bodies of the Gith that lay all around, and elation at the pure joy of simply being alive. We took a breath and looked at each other. Myron looked stern and turned to address us after we had caught our breath. “I’ve done a lot of things in the course of this job that I did not want to do. But most of all, biggest insult to the excessive injury that we have faced seems like I have to lead us into all final stand. Cos it was bad enough before, but there is no way we can be allowed to lose. We;ve given too much. Unfortunately we’re going to have to find a way to win this or else it’s all for naught. And I know that probably in this moment that seems a bit unlikely but unfortunately it’s a certainty, it’s the only way this is going to go. Unfortunately we’re not going to be able to stop until we’re standing on the corpse of a space dragon. I don’t know how we’re going to do it. I’d have liked to lose. We gave it a good shot. Eternal life with the gods. Well not today.” I nodded my approval. At last, he had taken up the role that, although he would deny it with all his might, he was born to do. We looked at each other and knew that in that moment, we would defeat this evil that had taken so much from us. No more sorrow. No more indecision. Just results.\medskip

As we placed our hands into the centre of the circle, we all noticed we had been branded with what looked like a faded scar in the shape of the Mark of Synne. A memory we would all treasure for the rest of our fight. A memory of the greatest book burner who ever lived…\medskip

\textbf{Epilogue}\medskip

David could not believe what he was seeing. He and Captain Stick had sensed danger and had managed to get the ship into the air not long after it had started it’s ascent. But now it was falling and they were still so far away. Not for long, he thought to himself. Suddenly a burst of static and the demon woman’s voice in his ear. “Get that ship off course. Save the city”. David protested. He was scared. His mother was gone. His father distant. He was reminded in the moment of how he had never really lived up to either of their expectations. Well not today. Today it was time for David to be the hero they always wanted him to be. With gritted teeth he reached out towards the wheel but was stopped by a small branch. He looked down at Captain Stick and Captain stick looked up at him. “Captain, no”, he said simply. “You’ve already given this world so much. You don’t need to”. Captain Stick looked back at him. “But your family!? Mary-Sue, Jeremiah and Xavier.” Captain Stick looked back at him. David’s eyes were full of tears. “I don’t deserve you. None of us do.” Captain Stick looked back at him. David cried out aloud and jammed Captain Stick into the wheel. He picked up an exquisitely tailored parachute and headed for the door. “I’ll make sure they remember this. The children will sing songs of what you have done here for generations”. But Captain Stick did not listen. He just thought of the children as the view of the Nautiloid grew ever bigger in the windscreen. “I love you all” was the last thought that went through his mind before his body became pain and ash.\medskip
