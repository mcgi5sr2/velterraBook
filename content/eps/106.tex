\subsection{Episode 106: With friends like these, who needs therapy}

\DndDropCapLine{C}hoo Choo! We're on a train heading east. It's been a few days. There are rolling hills either side, with the Tom Ardy mountains in the distance.\medskip

Gem and Myron are playing ping pong. It's a clash of titans, having played almost continuously for 3 days. With Myron emerging victorious. He then goes to try sleep inside the folded up table.\medskip

The carriage rocks slightly, there are footsteps on the roof. Bucky deploys his cane hook to get onto the roof and have a look. There are two figures in crimson robes and two more with large broadswords making their way towards the rear of the train.\medskip

Myron pops out of the table and immediately starts shooting the ceiling. Unfortunately for Myron, but fortunately for Bucky, who is on the roof the bullets don't pass through. Myron then stabs the roof, successfully stabbing one of the interlopers in the foot. "Is that crow blood?"\medskip

Myron continues to fight with abandon to defeat his mortal enemy, the train roof. Eventually making a hole large enough to jump up. He lands beyond our enemies blocking their way, dead casual.\medskip

Bronwen flairs her wings and ascends to the roof. And produces an insect plague that swarms the knights. Then launches her sword at one of the inquisitors.\medskip

Bucky changes the cane hook into a sling and fires into a knight, knocking him off the train. Then at the second. Sending him towards Myron. Then charges into the insect cloud the face off against the inquisitors. Getting slowly eaten alive. An inquisitor then points a finger of death at Bucky.\medskip

Gem finds the ladder to the roof soooooooo last year but uses it anyway. And proceeds to swear at the inquisitor facing Bronwen.\medskip

The second inquisitor blasts Myron, Bucky and Bronwen with lightning.\medskip

The remaining knight accidently throws his sword away. Boo frikkin hoo muther fucka\medskip

Kolo parkours up to the roof and into the cloud. Firing his sister's gift into an inquisitor knocking him off the train, then wheels around and fires at the remaining inquisitor.\medskip

Myron dodges back through the train to get to the inquisitor. Bronwen summons her sword imbuing it with holy light. It stabs into the inquisitor, grievously wounding him.\medskip

Bucky dispatches the last knight then runs to the inquisitor, stunning him with a punch. Gem uselessly blasts the inquisitor with an eldritch blast, then Kolo annhiliates him with a shot.\medskip

Kolo, wishing to finish the job sends a series of sniper shots into inquisitor, destroying him.\medskip

We sit on the roof and discuss things. The train engineer is less than impressed at the hole in the roof.\medskip

A few microaggressions later and the rest of the journey passes in peace. With Bucky fixing all the damage he did to the train. Naughty.\medskip

Kolo pops on a bowler hat and assembles us in the dining car for a super secret meeting. He's concerned that Canalon knows we are coming. This actually could be in our favour. But Bronwen reminds us that no one actually knows the full plan, so the details aren't compromised.\medskip

Lazarus has been enjoying volauvents and apologises for not being able to help in the last battle. We tell him about the inquisitors, he's surprised there are still some around. The meeting progresses back and forth.\medskip

After a few more days we arrive a few miles out of Hope's Rest. The army disembarks and forms ranks.\medskip

Lazarus flies up and off to keep tabs on Canalon.\medskip

Hope's Rest is big, really big. You just won't believe how hugely, mind bogglingly big it is. Gem is overwhelmed.\medskip

Myron "Yeah, it's beautiful, lets go in this sewer."\medskip

Some of the team console Kolo against future trauma of entering the sewer by reminding him thats where the team killed him last time. Subtle\medskip

We advance into the sewers. Bucky speaks far too much. Gem thinks she should literally banish Bucky from this plane of existence.\medskip

Gem makes an otcopod friend called Badunkadonk, they're ace. She loves them. New favourite team member.\medskip

Seeing this, Kolo wonders if there is another way to the violence he knows. He doesn't wonder this for long as he decides to shoot Badunkadonk. Bucky tries to stop him, catching the arrow. A standoff ensues.\medskip
