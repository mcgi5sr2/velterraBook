\subsection{Episode 108: In for a penny, in for a pound.}

\DndDropCapLine{T}he lobby successfully bypassed in a caconphanous battle, we proceed into a corridor that leads to several back offices. It's eerily quiet, the place should be bustling.\medskip

Kolo takes off his shoes to be extra sneaky, he surmises the giant mind controlling dragon is the reason noone is around. Maybe only the automated defences are still active?\medskip

Kolo and Bucky stealth off to have a look whats ahead whilst Gem, Bronwen and Myron wait.\medskip

After trying to squash Bucky into a ventilation shaft, they find nothing but a set of stairs, we decide to go on. We come across the anti chamber to the vault.\medskip

There's 2 pillars emitting crackling energy between us and the door. We ponder how we may cross it. Bucky spots a reflection in the door of a set of buttons under the desk. Through a combo of thunder / misty step we bypass the barrier. There are 3 buttons under the desk. Green, blue, red. Kolo goes to push the buttons, but Bucky spots him before he can.\medskip

The desk looks like it was abandoned mid use, he fiinds a post it note after moving some papers.\medskip

blue = stop\medskip

red = help\medskip

green = goodbye\medskip

During a little speech by Bucky to try determine what the buttons do, Gem winks at Kolo then pushes the green switch. This turns off the lightning pillars.\medskip

Bucky resumes his speech. Gem pushes the blue button to stop this.\medskip

A robot emerges from the floor and moves forward, pausing before the desk. Holding one hand aloft. Bronwen notices a post it note stuck on its hand.\medskip

The robot then moves to strike. Holy crap! It slices visciously into Bucky. Oops!\medskip

It fires a sickly green beam at Kolo, he's in the process of giving Gem a thumbs up and ignores the effect.\medskip

Bronwen launches the flying sword and engages the robot. Then while she twists away the robot catches her with a strike from one its legs.\medskip

Bucky launches a series of hits, then rolls under the desk and hits the stop button again. The robot freezes. Then it moves to return to where it came from. We quickly grab the post it note.\medskip

The note says 51624, the vault combo perchance! Kolo enters the code and the door opens. Revealing all the money.\medskip

Gem asks why the room is full of metal disks, there's a brief lesson in currency.\medskip

Kolo checks out some of the lock boxes. One of them is unusual, the key hole is fake. There are no tumblers. Its lock box no. 8. Kolo pushes at the handle, it moves in with a clunk then a whole section of wall moves aside, revealing a very old staircase.\medskip

We enter a chamber thats contains many pots filled with a black ichor. There are 3 skull heads afixed to the wall with their mouthes open and a hole in the centre of their mouths. There is also a mural on the wall of a mountain with a woman trekking up, there is a village on the far side of the mountain near a waterfall.\medskip

We surmise the pots are filled with something toxic and acidic. Kolo checks out every pot to see if they are the same. They are.\medskip

Bronwen inspects the mural, she finds a depression in the mural. Pushing it causes the mural to slide opens to reveal some instructions:\medskip

\textit{In the deep village of the westerns valleys, wise Kimicho left her settlement and travelled north through the open fields above for four days she trekked until mountains blocked her way. To the east she found a winding path of five furlongs which revealed a hidden waterfall below her}.\medskip

We discuss things, Bucky is fairly sure he knows the answer and commits, plunging his hand into a pot. He's correct and he finds a key without being hurt.\medskip

During another speech by Bucky, Kolo takes the key and tries it in the skulls until he finds a fit.\medskip

Kolo "Is this how you assassinate people Bucky? bore them to death?"\medskip

We think about the words of the passage in different ways, to see if we can discern the other routes. Bronwen tries a pot, its acid. She screams in pain. She thinks again, then tries another pot. It's correct!\medskip

Gem spots thats there's only one punctuation mark in the passage. Bronwen and Bucky suggests it might be one of the braziers. Gem reaches in and grabs the key, wondering why she didn't take the time to douse the fire before trying.\medskip

The final key placed, the armoury of Sin is revealed. Helm, sword, armour, shield, pendant and 2 nets.\medskip

Bucky decides who gets what.\medskip

We decide to use the pendant, we can have a full nights rest in only an hour. We feel invigorated.\medskip

Well, we're off to fight a God.\medskip
