\subsection{Episode 15a: Pilch Journal - through the Sands}

[A sheaf of paper, burned at one corner and retrieved from a fire - the only tell-tale mark of the author - Pilch. The handwriting is a scrawl, and while it changes fonts and style frequently, appears to be more down to the writer’s worsening condition than an intentional affect]\medskip

First night in the expanse: The dreams are getting worse, ever since my powers awoke I’ve felt a slow diminishing of my strength, my vitality, occasionally substituted (bargained away?) by dark powers. When I fused the stone of Un’thala with my own shadow-blade I thought I had managed to stop this wellspring of dark energies, but I see now it was more like a dam - a dam which has sprung leaks and may soon burst, drowning me in darkness.\medskip

I will rest a while, the others I can trust to carry me through the desert - though this land of light is certainly not helping me. Thankfully Howlov and Quoth do not seem to be affected.\medskip

Second night:\medskip

Black Sabbath, my curse bringing blade now haunts my dreams. It has a voracious hunger it wants me to feed, and while I gladly would, I cannot sate it. It has turned its parasitic needs to me, and as the shadows grow the sword threatens to consume me.\medskip

But there is light, I can see it in my dreams, the Mark of Synne still glows, just about holding back the shadow, a small pool of refuge in an eternal night.\medskip

...Third night?:\medskip

I have slept for more hours than I have been awaken, the sword whispered to me in my sleep, and I have tried to throw it away, but it is always there in my dreams, and always there when I awaken. My pallour is pale, and my veins throb with dark energy. I will likely need the others soon.\medskip

Another night:\medskip

The mark of synne whispered to me tonight - well, not in any true sense. I feel it holds within its infinite curves a small fragment of fate itself - why I so often avoid disaster when I should have died long ago… Including that first day I wore it with that Arbiters bullet…\medskip

The whispers are a vision, a prophecy I feel showed me with 5 others, 4 living and one dead. A man of stone, a man from under stone, and two pariahs trying to do good for their people, as well as a being made of spinning gears that no longer spin. Otaria - I am surprised to see.\medskip

The dream goes on, where the men of stone each, wreathed in divine light, hold an edge of a sword I recognise as Black Sabbath, and pull it apart.\medskip

Shadow erupts out of it in great torrents, threatening to drown me, but then one of these outcasts, Kolo I believe, parts the waves for me. I reach down and grasp the stone of Un’Thala from this black tide. The other pariah, no doubt exme, hammers it into an exotic curved blade, connected by a single thread of light to the being of once-spun gears.\medskip

I feel this prophecy is clear, that in order to rid myself of the shadow I must shatter the Cursebringer with the aid of my allies, a pastor and a creature of old magic, then, protected by Kolo, I can aid Exme in forging the refined power into the Katana that felled Otaria - a link to an old friend.\medskip

I feel hope.\medskip

Night after the ritual:\medskip

Well fuck me. I don’t know whether I shouldn’t trust this band of jackasses or if the mark of Synne is on the blink… I’m free from the darkness, but the prophecy went awry.\medskip

But at least Kolo did his part, and Gary (accidentally) did his.\medskip

What actually happened: The others refused to help me, save Kolo who gave me protection from the shadow. The relief was instantaneous. Gary then, more out of want not to deal with me than intentional help put me to sleep with his old magic. This divine energy and the protection from the darkness let me stand against the sword in my dreams, and use the mark of Synne to purge the shadows from my body and melt the cursebringer to nothing.\medskip

When I awoke I found a tingling sensation in my… my minds grasp? It was the stone of Un’thala! Or at least its essence. This I was able to fuse with the Katana that felled my friend Otaria - and while I cannot feel a direct link or her presence as I had hoped, I shall wield the blade in her honor - naming it the Iron Maiden!\medskip

[later that day]\medskip

Welp Gary didn’t kill everyone, and I got knocked flat on my ass - Iron Maiden did good though!\medskip
