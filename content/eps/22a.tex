\subsection{Episode 22a: Pilch: Musings on Sorcery}

\DndDropCapLine{P}erhaps I’ve spent too much time researching this recently, but I’m starting to muse how our powers are such a strange collection.\medskip

Apparently given, or at the very least (in my and Riphards instances) accelerated, by the stone of Un’Thala (now fused into my blade Iron Maiden) - everyone’s appear to develop differently. Kolo has his marks of slaying, Gary his old magics - similar but different to Riphards, who’s a trained pastor - an Agitator. And Exme has a smattering she seems to be able to channel or harness through her tinkerings.\medskip

Other than proximety to the stone, the only thing we share is having some kind of divine intervention - Gary by being presumable made by the gods, and then discovered by us, and the rest by being saved by Laz.\medskip

I’ve even found old notes about colleges of arcanists and societies, and even accounts of Arbiters using magical artefacts, how long ago from, I do not know. But perhaps magic was once more prevalent? Perhaps it did not need weird stones? Does ours? Is it just some contraption that lets Lazarus keep tabs on us? Then how did it enrich my original blade, Black Sabath, and allow me to empower and bond with my new one?\medskip

Eurgh, I think all this academic reading is rubbing off on me. Or maybe it’s recent events…\medskip

I’ve always thought my powers came from the Mark of Synne, which I received when reading the otherwise innocuous “Apotheosis of Synne” book - I’m now pretty sure this was just a vessel of the power, given it was a textbook on Synne-worms, and not some grand apocryphal doctrine on Synnes rise to god-dom.\medskip

But I’m now not sure that my Weapon, my creatures and my shadow-powers are directly connected to the Mark. Despite their different effects they… feel different.\medskip

When Shadow Weaving (as I shall now on call it) I feel this sense of darkness all around me, as if I’m on an island surrounded by a sea of shadows. Or perhaps I am an island, with a wellspring that fills with the tides. I can deplete this wellspring to conjure beasts, create darkness or similar, but it is replenished easily. It isn’t like physical exhaustion, I’m the son of a butcher, I know what that feels like, but more a depleting of the soul. Likewise I know this dark tide can drown me, as I’ve experienced a strange shadow sickness twice before.\medskip

But the Mark… The mark is far more mysterious. I know it has saved my life on 3 occasions. Most recently I was gutted by that elven woman - Vathena? - that Kolo is so frightened of. I felt the blade pierce my flesh, saw the satisfaction in her eyes and the cold inevitability of death - but then the mark glowed warmly, and I saw her blade retreat - leaving no sucking wound, but healthy, clothed flesh. She attempted again, but this time missed carelessly.\medskip

The other two times… I drowned in Rivers Falls, after a clumsy fall - instead rising out of the water, and then making the leap I’d failed, moments before… Or I guess, moments forward? And the first time… An arbiters bullet had scattered my brain, but I felt my skull nit back together, the damp click of a missfire, and the eye-brow searing explosion as the bastard lost his hand.\medskip

Shame the bloody thing didn’t save me from falling into Laz’s clutches, quite literally. I guess even divine intervention has its limits.\medskip

This… Fate Spinning… It isn’t as easy to replenish, needing a full nights rest, and has let me “reroll the die of fate” - obviously no one else sees what I see, this running back of the stream of time for a second attempt at pretty much… anything. In a smaller way I can use it preemptively, giving myself or others a little “Guidance” with a touch. It is warm, and comforting - and I do not get the sense it comes from within.\medskip

Perhaps I should stop cursing the Mark of Synne. Perhaps I should stop thinking of myself as branded by a corrupting god, but instead recognise that if Synne is, as I believe, a god of fate itself - one that is neither good nor bad but allows events to happen, and then gets blamed for their inevitability…\medskip

I should look into learning to wield my Mark to its fullest extent - it might stave off my own darkness long enough to complete these challenges, and balance me into something more… Acceptable.\medskip
