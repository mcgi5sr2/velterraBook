\subsection{Episode 22b: Gary: Musings on Lunch}

\DndDropCapLine{P}erhaps I’ve spent too much time drinking out of puddles recently, but I’m starting to muse how sometimes things you eat can make you feel different ways.\medskip

Apparently, all that Gabrin junk Kolo was giving me was causing, or at least accelerating, some changes in me that I'm not a fan of. Its not just that though. Whenever we're in the city, I watch what everyone eats. I'm still not quite used to eating, so I find it interesting.\medskip

When Rocky has been at the pub for a while, sometimes he goes to a shop where a big cylinder of meat rotates in front of a fire. I think it must be the leg of some giant animal. A man takes a special knife, cuts him off some strips, and puts it in some bread with some vegetables and "hot sauce". Everything goes down his front and then he usually passes out in an alley.\medskip

Kolo doesn't seem to eat much food these days. He eats a lot of things he makes in his lab. He also spends a lot of his time selling these things to other people to eat. I do not think they should eat them. When Kolo does eat, he vomits it all back up a lot of the time. I'm worried about him. In both senses.\medskip

Once I saw Fishy eating a fish, and at first I was freaking out, but then I realised that fish eat each other all the time. So I think that's fine. I had to leave after that, because I was doing Police things. He looked so happy down there, on the banks of the Eldon, eating all the dead fish that wash up just past the tanneries. Maybe he knows what he's doing after all.\medskip

Esme has started going to these places on the other side of town where they make little food that isn't served on plates. Its called things like "Keenwaw" and "Advokado", and the man who sells it has a big beard, and hair that's tied up on top of his head. She spends lots of money on it, but no one in there looks happy. Also the dough for the bread is apparently sour, and I don't know how that can be good for you.\medskip

Also, Kolo explained to me recently that all meat comes from animals. Beef comes from horses, apparently. I don't want to have to take a bit off a horse, just to eat some beef. Then it would just look ridiculous.\medskip

Perhaps I should stop eating things that make me sad. Perhaps I should stop eating things that make anyone else sad. Perhaps I should do a diet - one that is neither good nor bad but makes me happy, and that I can blame when I'm inevitably sad…\medskip

I should look into getting some lunch - it might stave off my own hunger long enough to complete this important Police work, and power me forward into something less… boring.\medskip

I'm going to get spaghetti... but without beef.\medskip
