\subsection{Episode 24a: Musings on... Light...}

\DndDropCapLine{I}t worked. I'd say I'm cynical, but recent circumstances make me... optimistic? But yeah, it actually worked!\medskip

When last I spoke, I was musing on my powers, my Shadow Weaving and Fate Spinning - one, I'm now certain, is my own dark sorcerery, sapping my vitality and poisoning my body and mind, a twisted cancer that lets me do dark, twisted things. But the other is external - a divine fragment of the gods, maybe not a gift, but I have been gifted by it none-the-less. This amazing power is more subtle but no less awesome that my own dark sorcereries. I have meditated upon it, practiced its power, and now can feel it pulse with renewed strength.\medskip

A strength that recedes the darkness. A strength that fills my soul with light, my heart with happiness. It banishes the dark thoughts and pushes back the shadows.. For the first time I feel... Optimistic.\medskip

Hell, I even took up the harmonica! And... well... spent some time with some sailors...\medskip

I find myself thinking of my lost love and, similarly, of my old friend Lilith - zippy and the book burners, and my father... For the first time I don't resent the man, I have sympathy. The mark connects me to these memories as living segments of time - more vivid that before, and my senses feel clearer.\medskip

...I hope I live to see them all again...\medskip

This is not said with sadness, but an unrelenting optimism. I cannot believe I am the same man as before! The light has set me free, and I must share it!\medskip

We fight for all of them, and we fight together - The goblin twins, Gary, Riphard (though he could not join us on this expedition). Arbiter Keros and her new partner. Even Archibald. Life has such hope, and I hope so much!\medskip

It is a fact that the odds are against us, that it is most likely some of us will die in this expedition. But for the first time I hope this doesn't happen, and I know that even if it does, we are stronger together.\medskip

Still, I'll do everything in my power to make sure we get through it.\medskip

... Together.\medskip
