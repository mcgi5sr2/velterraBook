\subsection{Episode 40a: Church factions over the years}

[The following is a heavily burned excerpt from a larger academic writing. There is a visible church stamp half burned across one corner which reads [APO]CRYPHAL, and various segments have red underlining and numerous exclamation points, written past the original publication.]\medskip

...And what of the fourth faction of the church?\medskip

We’ve established that book burners were once scholars and ambassadors, what I believe to be a true third faction, increasing church knowledge through outreach with different cultures and study of ancient lore - recast as the churches bloodhounds to seek out anything deemed “apocryphal” - diminished in number and standing within the church as a whole, working only in the last bastion of free Velterra - Hopes Rest.\medskip

Pastors are much the same, tending their flock, though perhaps more likely to enforce church doctrine rather than sharing teaching. And Arbiters have moved from holy warriors, stalwart protectors of the realm to a religious police force, acting as judge, jury and executioners…. … But this fourth faction, known only in whispers and the most apocryphal of writings, who few believe in and fewer know by name… Tell me dear reader, when someone says “The church will find out” or “the church won’t like that” or “I don’t want to cross the church” - to whom do they refer? The Book burners - rarely. Pastors? No. Arbiters? Perhaps. But there’s a fourth nameless faction. I believe them to be the Inquisition - the shadowy left hand of the righteous church, who are the leaders and spymasters.\medskip

Ancient orchestrators of every major act the church has performed - whos reach is so long but hidden that few will believe this passage, and those that do will fear to speak of it.\medskip

You need not believe me, but please, keep it in mind.\medskip

\textasciitilde{} Dunsid Cranberry - Halfling Scholar. Pub 6620\medskip
