Proper metal shit.\medskip

Miraculously the gate has been blown to smithereens, assisted by the collapse of the tower.\medskip

The battlebots engage the lines of musketmen, who begin to retreat into Masuda. At the command of Myron, and with the aid of Riphards back-ballista piloting, Delilah swings the ship down low and Myron deploys a layer of smoke over the archers on the wall.\medskip

The musketmen fire up at the ship, chipping some more life off of Gary.\medskip

A third round of firing goes awry. This shit is harder than it looks. Gary gets ever weaker.\medskip

Delilah soars away from the battle into the sky. Myron reactivates the pollution. The mountain is shaking vigorously now, but only Burnie has noticed. Worrying. There's some meta-volcano-zilla discussion.\medskip

Gary arcs over the Masudan walls, penetrating the outer edge of the city. There's panic on the streets of Masuda. But relative to the battle field outside things are fairly peaceful and quiet.\medskip

Myron starts shouting ``Fuck it uuuuup!'' over and over. Burnie is reluctant for some reason. Myron wants fucking chaos. Kolo has tied Burnie to a ballista arrow. He notices in time and cuts himself free.\medskip

The group remembers they are actually on here to heist a macguffin from a place. Kolo spots the anticipated location of the mystical doodah. And a giant fucking mountain lizard. As in\ldots\ the lizard is the mountains. All of them. The group groups in the cargo hold, ready to drop down into the city. Belly of the beast. Not the lizard\ldots\ yet.\medskip

Everyone jumps out\ldots\ except Riphard. Riphard tries to convince Delilah to just ditch everyone and fly away. Delilah almost convinces herself\ldots\ but instead kicks Riphard out herself. A single word echoes on the wind, ``Divooooooorce''.\medskip

Exme guides the way to the gizmo with magic of some sort. Burnie ties up the balloon cows by ``some rubble''. Disguised as rocks\ldots. No way this could go wrong.\medskip

The group approaches a huge glass wall, holding back a large pool of water. Riphard shoots the glass. A crack appears. And grows\ldots. And stabilises. Looks like it's been well designed.\medskip

Riphard asks the group who thinks he should shoot again. Only Kolo is keen. Dejected he kicks a stone at the wall and walks away. An ominous trickle of water goes unnoticed.\medskip
