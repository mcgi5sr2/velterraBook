Episode 57 - A Miner Incident

Riphard is in a bizarre fugue state, after his good friend died just moments ago. He makes his way back out of the mine to see his pregnant wife. It’s a 3 day journey alone, but he’ll be fine, looking for “Grief Poon”.

We pull out the body, and Rip comes back to drag it out, leaving a shitty smear to help us out.

Then a tentacle porn monster drops from the ceiling and attacks our token Furry. It surprises us so there’s no much we can do. Tigerius gets angry, and then Burnie stabs it through the face - it’s super effective!

The room we find was some sort of storage room, been ransacked already and there’s not that much of interest there anymore. Remnants of boxes, decayed…

Useless.

There’s a door. A stone door. A stone door in a stone hallway, with a stone knocker.

We open, go down a hallway. There’s no traps. There’s stairs and mention something about a mining accident., but we go into a hallway with weird little armor and a bunch of chests. We open a chest and to no-ones surprise the armor comes to life.

I open a chest, there’s 4 thingies. Fist-sized stones, general rock-stones. They have runes on them. Burnie chucks one at a thing, it does a cool crystal thing - but no damage.

We slowly start taking them down, through a combination of kittyclaws, hooks, lightning and bear arms. Also a big fucking cannon.

Lilith remembers she can stealth in order to be more effective rather than plinking crossbow bolts.

Burnie has a strange fever dream in the heat of battle, to do with moons, and exme breaking it apart… and then him and Tigerius demolish the bastards. Tigerius proceeds to rage-out and demolish the last one attacking Stanri.

Exme discovers that the armor was probably enchanted with runic magic. We find 2 excalibur pickaxes, 3 runes of healing, 1 rune of explosion and a bunch of old gold (500g).

The group talks about forming a band over a short rest. We make our way down a mineshaft, and Exme tries out her new pickaxe.

We get attacked by a big mean ugly steam-punk nightmare. Stanri is in love. It farts acid at us, and stanri attempts to mount it. We collectively murderise it. Stanri humps the corpse. But is sad because of his lack of junk.

We make our way back to the earlier stairway, and downward into the condemned mine. We fight a big assed robot, using surprisingly good tacitcs (because it hits hard). Tigerius demolishes it at the end, though stanri eats it. And we find excalibrum!

Excalibrum all collected, Lilith gives us a big heart-to-heart about how we’re great, and gives us a key to a printing press under the sewers, suggesting we get the people on our side. She’s going to stay amongst the dwarves to act as a leader/mayor/diplomat, to protect the excalibrum and insert some solidity.

We go past the last gasp, and fix a guys leg, explaining that Dagernum died doing what he loved - swimming in shit. Exme decides to write historical fiction based on a fever dream of the moon destroying velterra.

Anthony’s carriage was ransacked, looks like he’s getting a lift with us!

Back at the airship, Myron gets destoned. He gets filled in, then has a big heart-to-heart with Exme, having engraved his shield with “kolo the wise” - thinking he’s a lost cause. Exme is convinced she can still get Kolo back.

Delilah forces us to go to Hope’s rest. We also know that the only place we can smelt the excalibrum is with the Lindedorf gnomes.
