\subsection{Episode 57a: The Adventures of Snark the Goblin (Part 1)}

\DndDropCapLine{P}erspectives of a New-World Goblin attempting to integrate into Modern Magnimar, as told by Snark and scribed by Zuriel\medskip

*Transcript Begins*\medskip

You want me to talk about what we been doing while you write Miss Zuriel Lady? Okay, sounds fun! You want me talk about the others too? You’re all so nice, I’m glad I found you!\medskip

Soooooo I’m Snark, I come from Goblin tribe faaaar away. You won’t have heard of them. They all dead now anyway. I try tell them, I say “No keep eating people and stealing things or we gonna all get in trouble!” but nobody listen so then they all get boom’d. I make new friends though! Met Moxie and Pillow and some other friends but living in woods is hard and sometimes I have to eat new friends. Never Moxie and Pillow though! I got to thinking when in woods that Goblins need be nicer in world, make people like them. I made lots of friends in woods so I gets to thinking why not make friends with people too? So here I am!\medskip

*Editor’s note: Moxie appears to be a young raptor, she seems very protective of Snark though the Gods know how she managed to find a raptor. Pillow is a rather large and impressive wolf, who acts as a riding mount, backpack and (as the name suggests) a place of comfort for Snark in the wild.*\medskip

So, first I met Bill, he is a lovely halfling man and his best friend Mac is such a friendly cow, though he is quite big and scary but it okays he’d never hurt me! Bill makes lots of food and loves nature even more than I love animals! He even makes Moxie and Pillow feel better when they get hurt, he so kind! We got to riding around on his biiiiiig cart and it’s so comfy and full of food. I so lucky making nice friends.\medskip

We met a very angry dwarf called Mr Krogan. He acts all grrrrr but he likes when I give him snacks so we friends even though he keeps saying nasty things about goblins. He has a big bushy red beard and a huuuuge axe. He looking for some old heirloom so me and Bill tagged along to see more world and do more good things.\medskip

We all travelled and ended up here, Magnimar! It’s so big! People are everywhere, even other goblins! Most people no like goblins here, but that just mean lots of people to make friends with!\medskip

We hired a bounty hunter called Jenny the Red to find more stuff about the heirloom, she is soooo cool! She wear red armour and is very sneaky, I once saw her cut someone’s legs out without them even seeing her coming! We weren’t very good friends at first, Krogan kept saying she would stab us in the back but I said she’d come around. It did! When Jenny got jumped in an alley by some bad men. The bad men didn’t like it so much when Pillow and Moxie tore their throats out, but Jenny seemed to be our best friend after!\medskip

We still not found heirloom so we got some jobs in Magnimar while Jenny searched. Bill worked in a soup kitchen, Krogan got a job as a bouncer at The Fisherman’s Friend and I made some new friends at a Donkey Sanctuary where I got paid to tell the Donkeys stories all day!\medskip

We got to drinking in the Fisherman’s Friend most nights, their milk is very nice and I get to talk to lots of people. We made friends with the barkeep Gwen Fiddich who is suuuper strong! She likes poking bad customers in the eye and threatening them with her big mace Betsy. She barkeep by night but fights by day, kind of sounds wrong way to me but hey.\medskip

That’s also where I met you! You like sitting in the pub gathering stories, you super smart and scholarly. And you can do all those fancy spells and stuff it so cool! Plus you are so pretty and have such nice dresses and things, goblins not have pretty dresses they set fire too easy.\medskip

\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\medskip

So, we were in the pub as normal after working, I don’t mind because Gwen serves some real good milk which has nice tang and sharpness, mmmmm. Sorry, anyway I was making some new friends with Jenny cos some people in Fisherman said I was stinky and shouldn’t be let in. I learned to play darts too, it seems quite easy to me I just throw and hit the things but I had to get help with the numbers. Goblins big on the tricks and the fun but momma always told me writing stole your soul so I never really did that.\medskip

Gwen used her famous poking finger to deal with some drunk men who were starting a fight, they tried to stay but when she went to get Betsy down they quickly decided to leave. I don’t blame them, Gwen is scaaaaaary.\medskip

The next day while we were working, you Zuriel went to see a fancy lady friend of yours up in her nice house and she asked you to put together a team because she had some shiny things stolen from her. It looked like it was nasty goblins who had done it and tunnelled into their vault. Such a shame that goblins are still doing such nasty things.\medskip

We went and had a looksie, we got taken to the rear entrance where all the most honoured guests go and led in to some servants’ quarters. So humble of her to call her dining room that! And she fed us pastries and cream! So nice, quick friend. She a very pretty lady, told us about an heirloom that been stolen by the peoples. They dug a tunnel right into the vault under house, so sneaky. She offered us 150 silver each if we could recover it, which is enough to buy 15 cream teas. I accepted on behalf of the team immediately.\medskip

We went to look at the room where stuff been taken, and sure enough goblin tracks. Such bad goblins. The tunnel went into the sewers and we found that there were people who lived there. Mostly bad people. Veeeeeery bad people.\medskip

Because of all the potential for nasty things we went and got our weapons and I fetched Moxie and Pillow because they always love adventures. Pillow didn’t seem to be too excited about sewers but he is a grumpy wolf sometimes you just have to ignore him. Bill brought big Mac as well so the whole gang was together!\medskip

We did some investigating, split up and talked to people in the poor part of town. Most of them were very nice when they realised I not gang goblin. Jenny said she had to pay some of them to talk but I just gave them some jerky and stroked their heads, I think I better friend-maker than her if I honest.\medskip

Turns out there a nasty clan of goblins in sewers and their old boss got killed to death recently, some big old evil hobgoblin had taken over and was bullying all the others into robbing rich peoples. This make me feel better, you know, goblins only doing bad because some big bully was making them. Goblins can be very loyal to bullies though which is shame because we will have to kill them then.\medskip

We also found a goblin girl had run away from the gang and they were trying to get her. Good goblin girl rebel I thought, we find her first and she tell us where they are and what to expect! A few more bits of friend making and one of Bill’s super-tasty ham hocks as a bribe and we found the goblin girl in a graveyard. Pretty spooky place to hide if you ask me but hey not everyone so clever eh?\medskip

We found her but goblin girl ran from us screaming. I tried to calm her down but she no listen, so Moxie had to chase her. Bill tangled her feet with nature magic (so cooool!) and we eventually managed to make her calm. Krogan didn’t help by insulting her, he said some nasty things about goblins. I will have to friend him more until he stops this silliness. Moxie didn’t help either, the goblin girl didn’t understand Moxie’s lovely smile. She told us where the base was, gave us a map and warned us of a nasty rockfall trap. The base wasn’t too far from the graveyard. She also warned us that boss fed bad goblins to a vampire! I not sure if real vampire or not, but we got to be careful whatever this is. Fortunately, we have Gwen who very strong, she should be able to poke vampire in eye and show him he bad.\medskip

Krogan and Jenny had good idea to make me look more like clan goblin so I could sneaky sneak and see what happening. You waited at the entrance as a lookout (but I think it more that you not like smell, eh Miss Zuriel? You did look a bit pale there)\medskip

*Editors note: this is incorrect, I stood watch for purely tactical reasons*\medskip

It was very dark in the lair, which no bother me but most of others could barely see. Bill made some shiny magic dancing lights to follow us. We go into big empty circle room with stinky water on floor and I go a bit ahead to looksie. Some nasty ooze monster slopped out which I thought was puddle and stung Pillow. It looked very burny, Pillow was not happy. We went into action and I set Moxie and Pillow on it, but when they bite it stung them badly. Lucky with others we managed to kill it fast because I don’t think Moxie or Pillow wanted to touch it again.\medskip

We think it hard to sneak up on goblins if we have big shiny lights that others need so Krogan and me went. We sneak forwards to biiiiig room with 5 big pillars and leave everyone else in the tunnel. We did a sneak up and got pretty close but Krogan not so good at the moving quiet and he make a clanky noise. We shout for help and Krogan manage to cut head off one goblin in one hit. Makes me think be nice to Krogan. I shoot one from behind pillar but no want go out against many with only lil old me. Friends come fast though, bad goblins try to shoot them full of arrows as they run up but Jenny super-fast and dodges a lot. Moxie is also real fast and gets in the face of one of the goblins as I get back on Pillow. I worry for Moxie, she young and brave but she ran into lot of goblins on her own. I need tell her be more careful, but she lucky this time and only get injured a bit.\medskip

Krogan is very strong and takes down another goblin in one hit. Jenny and Gwen manage to run in and kill goblins on left side. Only two goblins left when I get out round pillar and Moxie quickly eat one’s face. Last one had already dropped weapons when Moxie jumped at him so we pinned him down to have a nice little chat with about what was in cave. Bill was very nice to Moxie and healed her with magic. I must talk to Moxie about not being so rash next time though.\medskip
