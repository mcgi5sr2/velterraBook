Epilogue

Myron and Stanri sat on the beach enjoying the warmth of the sun and the gentle lapping of the ocean. The beach was relatively empty today, probably because of all the killings. Myron turned to his robot companion. “So….”. Stanri looked back at him with his giant glowing eyes. Myron continued. “Do you er.. Enjoy being a bear?”. Stanri appeared to consider this for a moment and then turned back to look out to the sea. ‘What must it be like to be him?’, thought Myron, ‘Does he even know what’s going on? I mean. He’s just metal n shit isn’t he? Is he even a he? It? Jeez. He’s probably the first thing that there’s ever been like him. Except for all them crazy robot women. What must that be like?’. He looked over at Stanri who was now lying flat on the sand. ‘Who am I even?’ thought Myron. ‘I used to think I knew. But then all this happened. Negotiations. Sleeping indoors. Friends. Feelings. Who knew?’. Stanri eased himself up onto his legs and padded down to the ocean, snapping at the incoming waves and pawing at the sand. ‘Is he happy?’. Myron looked out across to the horizon. ‘Am I?’. Stanri came back to Myron and dropped a still-flopping ocean fish at his feet then looked up at him, his big eyes close to Myron’s face. Myron could see his own reflection in them. He looked older, more tired. But there was something else in there too, something he hadn’t noticed before. In a way, it suited him he thought. He raised a hand and patted Stanri on the head. “Good boy”.
