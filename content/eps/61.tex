\section{Episode 61: Arbitroution}

\medskip

\DndDropCapLine{W}e got back to New Dabaghost to relax after some intense negotiations with the fishmen. We have 2 days to gather ourselves and steel the Dabbers for the tough discussions that are sure to follow.\medskip

Vuthelle says they trade with the mainland, but the fish troubles have put a real “spanner barb” in the works (look it up). Their community is close to collapse. I sense a business opportunity. After the discussions BlackRock can help them get their new economy going, for a healthy slice of the fish pie of course. I send Bobacious a raven to set the wheels in motion. This could be a prime candidate to road test our new currency.\medskip

Tigerius says he could eat the fishmen. He’s probably right. Do tigers eat fish? He wants to build bridges and canals to anywhere. For war, and trade! And cos he just seems to want to build shit and take over the world. Myron says he doesn’t want to piss in Tigerius’ trumpet, but he tells Tigger that that isn’t really the plan.\medskip

I suggest we replace religion after the fall of the church with the beliefs of the Guild of Gary, which seem overall more positive and beneficial to society. Myron and I get into the nitty gritty of theological debate and spiritual beliefs and their place in society at large. We realise our religion and beliefs don’t even have a name. Much ecumenical reform will be needed when we bring down the church.\medskip

Sidenote: Exme needs to invent light bulbs and the electrical grid.\medskip

The group discuss their arbitration role in the upcoming negotiations.\medskip

We sail out to the meeting point. The fish-king Oceany has a throne on the back of a whale. Sweet. The fish king boards our ship. He says his name is “Yourliege”. Yourliege Oceany, fair enough. Bernie audibly hears Lady Dabbaghast moisten at the impressive sight of Oceany.\medskip

There’s some regal back and forth pleasantries. Oceany accuses the Lady and her peoples of raping his … lands? She says obviously they didn’t know, they’re just trying to rebuild after the sea raped their old actual lands. There’s probably some irony in there somewhere.\medskip

The group starts popping off buzzwords in the hope it impresses the Fish King. The Fish King says we speak many words, but his people are a people of substance and direct meaning not verbosity. What do we want and what can we offer them. His helmet malfunctions slightly.\medskip

I wonder what exactly it is that we land folk CAN offer a perfectly content underwater population. I describe the wonder that is lemon trees and other exotic fruit. We stumble on alcohol. Turns out D’abberghast is an alcoholic. She aggressively steers the conversation back to the whole mass murder situation.\medskip

Tigerius brings it straight back to trade though with his keen business acumen identifying metal and weapons as a key missing element to under sea life.\medskip

We seem to be coming to a vague ending to the negotiations. Everyone’s roughly on board with the trade and free movement idea. But the King tells us he has other enemies and that us land folk are the lesser of his worries. He wants us to kill some other motherfuckers. Typical. I ask if they’ve attempted a trade agreement with these people. But it turns out they aren’t a people, but an… it. Akroneth The IMMORTAL. Should be easy enough to kill then.\medskip

We agree to this, though I worry about the efficacy of land weapons when used under water. Especially against AKRONETH THE FUCKING IMMORTAL.\medskip

The negotiation disbands and Dab says we can feast and stay at the palace. Tigerius mysteriously says he’s not coming as he needs to talk to his birds and fish… or something. Everyone’s very suspicious and weirded out. Exme says she’s not coming to fight the unkillable sea creature because her gun will kill us all if she does. She says she’ll make some stuff for us though.\medskip

Bernie offends Lady Dabbagonalley by pushing her about her apparent fish fetish, warning her that Oceany might try and ejaculate on her eggs. Tigerius, who apparently has come to the feast, take Bernie away. The Lady wishes us luck for the impossible suicide mission.\medskip

The group retire and Bernie’s pillow sprouts a mouth that whispers “biiiird fuckeerrrrrr” at him. He fucks the mouth.\medskip

The group inspect their fish weapons: tridents, bidents, unidents, harpoon guns. Exme has made us fancy goggles too so we can breathe.\medskip

We set out on our ocean quest. We come across the horrible poison water. Stanri dimly lights the way with his low-beams. I save my daylight spell for later.\medskip

We are set upon by some fishfuckers. Shark riders coming at us fast in their natural habitat. Tigerius lines up his bident to try and lance the oncoming sharky bois.\medskip

One of them tries to hump Bernie, but I shoot him dead. Like shooting fish in a barrel. But the barrel is the ocean.\medskip

Tigger claws the bigger shark boi badly, but he’s still standing. Swimming. Sitting?\medskip

Myron doesn’t know how to use his new harpoon gun and he accidentally lets go of it and it drifts away from him.\medskip

Bernie stabs the fuck out of a shark. Twice. The shark tries to bite him, but misses. He riposts. Good night sharky. He says something about his erections.\medskip

I shoot the other sharky boi. The big shark bites Tigger and the sharky boi double stabs him and also bites him. Bad day for a tiger to take up swimming. Tig gets his revenge, both parties badly bloodied.\medskip

Myron tries to use his gloves as a weapon ineffectively. But he tries again and gloves the sharky boi to death. Brutal. The shark bites him as he tries to swim away to grab him harpoon gun that has served him so well so far.\medskip

Burnie xbox’s the shark really hard. I shoot at it too. Stanri, who is also here, scratches at it. The shark bites Tig. The shark, fearing for its life, swims away and quickly vanishes. Phew, threat over. Now we only have immortal ancient sea monsters to worry about.\medskip

We press on and the water becomes a thick muck, sticky, cloying, oily, musky, and generally repulsive. We reach was appears to be the bottom of the sea and find a shipwreck. Surrounded by darkness, we spy an opening into the seabed with what feels like infinity beyond. We start to hear demonic voices in our heads.\medskip

“New play things, you will be mine. Cum. Cum.”\medskip
