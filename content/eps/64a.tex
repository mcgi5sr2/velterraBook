\subsection{Episode 64a: A Crumpled Bit of Scribbled Parchment Found in the Bin}

\textbf{Opshun I - Fings r wot thay seme}\medskip

We wennt tu Gultings Beuy, an Kirri woz thar deelin wiv them Essarays, an thay wonted to mez up ther vilidge so thay took uz lot too th wuds so wi wozn't in tarn an then thay kitniped aload ov peeple an trickt uz inta killin em. Den, wen Kiry sow us dooin it (evn tho we dint no we woz) she fort we woz bad an told thu polease bout it.\medskip

FINGS WOT DUNT MAIK SENSS IF DATZ TROO:\medskip

\begin{itemize}
  \item Ow did thaay no hoo we woz?
  \item Ow did tha pulice get der su kwik?
  \item Wy did Fraier wanna git us arested?
  \item Wey did Kiri lett Antny taik the wrap wen she new he wont ther?
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Opshun II - Kirys alwaze bin bad}\medskip

Keri tuld Fry an th puleace day wi woz cumin to Goldens Boy, an wurked wiv em to taik us done. Shi woz jus protendin to bi aginst thu Esserais, an agrid ti git us outin to ther wuds so wi wozn't in tarn an than thi kitnabed a bunsh o pilel and triked us inn too killin em, avin alredi told tha poles to cum ere.\medskip

PHINS WUT DUNOT MACE CENES IF TATS TRU:\medskip

\begin{itemize}
  \item Wi fraim uz iph shi cud juss sei wire bad anty church peple?
  \item \textit{Kirri}
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Opshun III - Esarys an Chirch alweze bad. Kris misled.}\medskip

Curch an Fraya new we wir cumin, maid plan wivute Keri. Crich giv spechul bad powas tu Frier n roturn fur frimin uz. Kier seez uz kilin all thums popal and beleaves it.\medskip

\begin{itemize}
  \item Woi dint Kori fink anyfin woz wird abut pilos bin thaer?
  \item Wy did Kiriy let Antny tace the rip wen she knew he wosnt thur?
\end{itemize}
