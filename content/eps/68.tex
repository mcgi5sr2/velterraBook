\subsection{Episode 68: Kill Kolo}

\DndDropCapLine{[}INTRO]\medskip

I wake up alone, and defeated. I lead everyone into danger, and potentially to our deaths. I was hoping the day would take a little longer to come, but it seems like The Eight had other plans. Today is the day that we will have to kill Kolo.\medskip

Outisde, I can hear the chattering of Kolo’s mutated Gabrin spawn.\medskip

I sit down and try and think real hard for a bit, trying to feel if I can work out if there are any monster type things, and I reckon there’s probably three in the room on the other side of this cell door, which are probably those nobheads I can hear down the way, and two others further off. Presumably one of them’s King Nobhead.\medskip

I can hear Rip talking to one of the guards, and he sounds like he might be trying to do something fancy. The guard goes off to fetch something.\medskip

Esme comes over the earpieces, which they somehow forgot to take away from us. Everyone seems to be alright, but just then, we hear Kolo come into the room, down the way. He goes up to the guard, who seems to be on his way to unlock Rips’ room, and puts a kibosh on that.\medskip

He does a bit of taunting.\medskip

I call Kolo over. He looks like a piece of shit. Like, he is a piece of shit, but he also looks like a piece of shit. Not only is he covered in fungus, like he was before. But he’s also got one big fucking arm and lumpy bits and big weird wings and shit.\medskip

I tell him a few unfortunate details, such as that I’m going to kill him before he can hurt Esme any more. He doesn’t seem to agree, and thinks that we’re gonna be sacrificed in the name of Kalimar, which doesn’t sound right.\medskip

Kolo leaves.\medskip

We try to take stock of what and realise it’s nothing. I mean, it’s not that surprising that they took our stuff off us, and obviously Esme doesn’t have Stanri. They’re not that stupid.\medskip

Delilah seems to be awake “Where are my children?”. I broke the bad news that the children may now be getting prepared to be sacrificed to an elder god, but hey, what could be better in a fight than a mother desperate to defend her children?\medskip

Riphard took some time to examine the drains, relieving himself into it through a healthy dual stream from his dwarven piping. He told her to make sure they sacrificed Dildo first. She wasn’t happy.\medskip

We continue to fail to do anything useful for a bit.\medskip

Exme told us all to check out bodies for signs of the infection. Burnie, a little too eagerly, inspected the inside of his anus, later telling me in confidence that he had found a lump, which had startled him because there were usually two.\medskip

Burnie took a break from fingering himself to try and goad the guards in his broken Goblin language. He started a ‘fake’ fight with Riphard about their respective Dwarven heritages.\medskip

They have their fight, and successfully attract the guards over to their cells, causing the perfect distraction. Then… it sort of peters out and nothing happens.\medskip

After that, Esme starts calling out for Kolo in the cell opposite mine. He eventually turns up, and start jabbering away at her in Gabrin, getting pretty riled up. At one point he punches a hole in the wall, and she doesn’t look impressed.\medskip

She seems to be trying to cause a distraction, but no one really has anything to do, and eventually this peters out too. Kolo turns to leave.\medskip

I yell out to him, calling him over to look at the bar that I’ve bent on the door, explaining to him that I’m going to bend the rest of them, break out of here and come and kill him, and that I’m not going to just wait in here to be killed by Kalimar after all.\medskip

He asks me if I’m saying that he’s going to have to kill me, and I tell him yes.\medskip

He goes to fetch his bow and comes back to shoot me with it. By the time he does, I’m another few bars down. He raises his bow and Esme cries out, as an arrow flies straight into my torso.\medskip

I carry on, as Kolo does the same. Esme is calling out to Kolo, trying to get him to stop, or else she says she’ll use Stanri to break out of her cell.\medskip

Wait…\medskip

I take another few arrows and get through the first door. From here, I can see Burnie trying the classic fake-escape-round-corner-psyche-out, which seems to be working.\medskip

With a yell from Esme, Stanri busts through the inner door like wet paper.\medskip

Burnies guard unlocks his door and goes in. There’s a thud.\medskip

Esme runs in between me and Kolo, as I start work on the second door. She attempts to appeal to a good nature, that bears very little evidence of existing. Behind her, Burnie hops out of his room, throwing a key to Rip as he does so.\medskip

Only a few bars are left, as Kolo and I trade threats above Esme’s head. A few more arrows thud into me, but it’s worth it. Kolo seems to think the babies are special in some way, and that Kalimar would appreciate them especially.\medskip

Esme tries to get Kolo to remember childhood memories, and appeals to his nature. On some level, I feel like she might be getting the picture, though.\medskip

Two of the knobheads have run up to join us, and seem to be avoiding too much damage to her, while holding her off.\medskip

As Rip runs out of his room, I manage to pop the last bar. I jump up into the opening, standing on the threshold. “Your move, Greeny”, I say, and I do feel bad about bringing skin colour into this, but I was under a lot of pressure to say something cool.\medskip

The two knobheads strike out at me with claws and fangs, my blood burns, and my muscles seize and the world goes black.\medskip

\textbf{-------------------}\medskip

Riphard and Burnie are barricaded in the room where the weapons are stored, collecting their belongings and anyone else’s they can carry. Riphard, clutching the Hammer of the Gods, shoots round the corner, felling an assistant, and taking a heavy swipe in the process.\medskip

As he does so, he sees Esme, unconscious, being dragged to a cell by an assistant, Stanri lying immobile, Delilah taking heavy swipes from two assistants, and Myron unconscious and paralysed. He ducks back, letting Burnie run out - shooting the assistant carrying Esme and hiding behind a table - before running round the corner, gun drawn, just in time to see Delilah downed by the assistants, paralysed and bleeding heavily.\medskip

“Must be her time of the month” he opines, entirely failing to give the situation the gravity it requires.\medskip

Delilah gasps on the ground, her maternal energy forcing the life back into her body.\medskip

Burnie runs up to Kolo, standing at the edge of the hole his assistants having been digging to summon the eldritch horror they worship as a god. He goes to grapple the distorted shadow of Kolo, but his pegleg slips on the rim of the hole, and topples backwards, 100ft down into the hole.\medskip

Riphard fills Esme with radiant healing energy, she obligingly plays dead, as she continues to be dragged into the cell at the back of the room. Just as he does so, the other two assistants round on him, and he falls to the ground, unconscious and paralysed.\medskip

In the hole, Burnie straps on Esme’s spiderclimb boots and starts hobbling up the slick walls.\medskip

Delilah stands to her feet, and sees her babies in Kolo’s hands on the other side of the room. Her maternal instincts propel her across the room, and she slaps Kolo square across the side of the face, causing him to drop both infants to the ground.\medskip

Esme, still playing dead, gets brought into Kolo’s room, and, cracking one eye, she spots a snowglobe on the side, and, twisting out of the grasp of the Assistant, bludgeons him to death with it.\medskip

Burnie makes it to the top of the hole, and, reaching over the edge of the pit, plunges his hook into Riphard, giving him a burst of healing electricity. Rip regains consciousness, but is still paralysed on the ground.\medskip

Esme runs out of the room, calling out to Kolo to stop, despite the fact that, apart from a few glimpses, very little of her brother seems to remain.\medskip

Burnie climbs up, and looks across the hole at Kolo, knife in hand. Kolo locks eyes with him, froth dripping from his toothy grin. Burnie’s hand twitches.\medskip

With a flick, Burnie activates the runes on the dagger, and the stone around the edge of the hole softens, and flows inward, sealing the pit, and even completing the surface with a small grate. Kolo cries out in frustration. “Silly Burnie, what have you done?”\medskip

Esme, still refusing to fire on her brother, calls out to him.\medskip

“Leave the babies on the ground, Kolo.”\medskip

“Okay Esme, I will.” says Kolo, and crushes Delilah’s head beneath his claw.\medskip

Silence.\medskip

Riphard, conscious and paralysed on the ground, lies, eyes seized wide open, watches.\medskip

Burnie kills the last remaining Assistant, as Esme steps up to Kolo, reprimanding him mildly, in what seems like a significant underreaction.\medskip

Kolo looks at sister, pleading for him to stop. He raises his still-bloodied lobster claw, brings it down, and his sister is lying in a heap at his feet.\medskip

Riphard overcomes his paralysis and forces himself to his feet. Eyes fixed to Kolo.\medskip

A golden light surrounds Esme, as Rip lays a gauntleted hand on her tiny, frail form.\medskip

Esme digs in her bag, laying discarded at Rip’s feet, drawing the Cube of Ruh’brex from it, twisting it into an array of complex shapes before she’s even finished taking it from the bag.\medskip

Time freezes.\medskip

Esme blinks, free to move in a frozen moment of time. Calmly, she picks up the frozen babies, walks into the cell where Myron was being kept, and stows the babies underneath the cot, before walking up to Myron, and jolting him with a bolt of energy, causing time to flow once more.\medskip
