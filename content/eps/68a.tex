\subsection{Episode 68a: Episode 68 Supplement - Exme's Thoughts}

\DndDropCapLine{I}'ve had bad days before. But this.... We had woken up in prison cells deep in the sewers. Of course it was Kolo, who else could it be. I'd lost all my tools but he'd left Stanri with me of all things. From between the bars I could make out some of the others, each one in their own cells. My first fear was that he had already infected them. From my research, that seems to be the easiest way. Slip it in the food or rub it onto the buttocks. Then just let time do its thing. They looked healthy enough from where I was but how could you know. My second fear was that they were going to kill Kolo. Even in his right mind, Kolo always underestimated them, and the things we've accomplished when we're together.... impossible. They weren't trapped in here with Kolo, he was trapped in here with them. I just prayed that I would be able to make them listen.\medskip

The note threw me. Two simple words written in a scrawled hand that I knew only too well. "Help me". I hadn't expected this, that there was still some awareness in there. From my research I knew that there was a very good chance that he could be saved, but I had thought that the way the fungus takes over the body, in particular the brain, would have rendered the subconciousness powerless and unaware. Yet here was the evidence. Kolo was fighting back against it. He was always strong.\medskip

We talked. I tried to persuade him to make this easy, that he should not underestimate them. That this could be fixed. I pleaded, trying to evoke memories of his past that would help the real Kolo grow stronger, take back some control and show the others that there was hope. But it was no use. Otoria, Gary, Pilch, Mother even. Sometimes I thought there was a sadness in those eyes, a look of recognition, but it was not strong enough to fight against this deformed monstrosity that stood laughing at us in its domination of my brother.\medskip

Myron started it. He was always as headstrong as Kolo if not more so. From my cell I could hear every arrow piercing him as he made his way towards Kolo. That damned fool. I think he would have rather died than sit there doing nothing. But that was too much for me to bear. I believed it so hard, that no-one had to die today. That I could persuade them all and we could subdue Kolo and fix him. Then everything would be back to normal, if anything ever was normal. What a fool.\medskip

Stanri opened the doors but was immediately floored by Kolo's assitants. Poor thing didn't stand a chance against those monstrous claws. But he had done his job. I had to get inbetween Kolo and Myron and stop them from destroying eachother. But what am I without my tricks and gadgets? Weak in body and mind. I tried, oh Mother I tried so hard. But every time I stood up to get in the way they threw me back. I just couldn't get through to either of them, the intensity of their rages. His assistants went easy on me for what it's worth but I still couldn't stand up to them. I knew the others were making their moves. When Myron went down and Kolo went for the children my heart broke. Delilah was there and the last words I ever said to her were to help Myron as I went after Kolo. Shortly after that the world turned black.\medskip

I woke up in what looked like his office, being carried by one of the assistants, bloody and hobbling. Dull sounds of battle came from the main room filling me with dread. If Riphard had got a weapon then that was that. Kolo needed the babies for his plan, whatever that was, but who knows what danger they could have been in. I murdered the assistant with the closest blunt object I could find, feeling the dark blood flow over my hands taking its life with it. After finding my bearings I looked back into the main chamber. Burnie and Myron were nowhere to be seen. Riphard stood facing Kolo, who himself stood over Delilah's unmoving form, hand outsretched towards her two babies who lay on the floor screaming. Out of the corner of my eye I saw the barrel of my gun poking out of the impossible bag, seemingly a thousand miles away. My first thought was to help Delilah so that she stood a chance of rescuing the children and lowering the stakes. But on my own what could I really do with Kolo right there? I needed that weapon to heal her properly if we were going to stand a chance. I made a choice and I ran for the gun, dodging past Kolo. I shouted at him not to hurt the children. I don't think I'll ever forget the grim smile that came across his face. Or the sound of Delilah's skull being destroyed underneath his terrible claw.\medskip

I felt sick as I dropped to my knees. There are no words. When I could bear to look over for a moment I didn't see Delilah. I saw that little girl in the church, with her pig tails and her best dress and that impossible bullet wound through her chest. As the immediate situation came back to me, with all its terrible sights and sounds and feelings, the worst thing is I still thought that he could be saved.\medskip

I found the cube and I stopped time. The children were the first priority. I managed to pick up their small timeless forms and get them out of the way to safety in Myron's room. In there I found Myron, down but breathing. I revived him with the shot that was meant for Delilah. He took a moment to wake up, then slowly stood up and went to look out at the scene playing out in the main chamber. When he turned back and looked at me I felt that time had stopped again. I've never seen a face convey such an intense mixture of rage and disappointment at the same time and I pray that I never see it again. I can't bear to remember that look. He walked away and I stood there, dull to the emotions that were starting to form inside me. Was I to lose both of them today?\medskip

As I ran back into the room I came face to face with my worst fear. I could feel the rage of the team and Kolo seemed to be drinking it in. The assistants were gone. Myron and Riphard were engaged in full-on warfare with Kolo. Burnie, less enthusiastically, was doing his part and was the only one who seemed to show any sign of an emotion other than cold hard hatred in the room. I went on auto-pilot trying desperately to shut out the image of Delilah's broken body, overcome with grief and the panic of the situation, coming closer and closer to the realisation that my happy ending was not coming. Kolo was never any match for them. Not when they are like this. Especially with all that Riphard had lost and stood to lose.\medskip

Kolo was forced into a cell. Riphard screamed for blood. Myron coldly made attacks, slow and determined. Burnie, ever the diplomat, and I did our best, eventually managing to subdue Kolo before the other two could outright kill him. Kolo was down, alive but unmoving. Finally, I thought to myself, they have listened. Surely, now the threat is over they will see that it wasn't Kolo's fault, he was sick. I wouldn't let myself think of Delilah, not yet. I think if I did then I would have died. There would be time for that after Kolo was fixed. We would make amends. Me and my brother.... The murderers together again at last...\medskip

Myron had cold fire in his eyes. He walked past me to go in for the kill. To kill my brother!??! Now, while he was down and vulnerable!? After all the death and misery of the day Myron dared to make one more corpse and deny me the only thing I have ever wanted?!?! He took a shot and missed. And I have never felt such anger. I tried my best to give him the same look he had given me and I poured all of the emotions I had into it. I trusted him. I thought I loved him and that he loved me. How could he not understand what this meant to me!??!?! He gave me that look again and with his next words he destroyed me. "This is all your fault". For a moment the anger welled up but then once again I saw that crushed skull in my mind, and the little girl, and all of them, the countless hundreds that I've put to death. The ones that my weapons and gadgets and gizmos have robbed of life or filled with misery. I saw it all in that moment and the worst thing is that I knew that in a way, Myron was right. He walked away from me and didn't look back.\medskip

As Riphard came towards us I knew that I had to make a decision now. One of them, Riphard or Kolo, was going to die. There was no way that he could forgive Kolo's crimes today I thought to myself. Fool. I considered it, I really did. I think Burnie would have helped me. He would have held me back short of killing him at least and given us that time. But where would we be after that? Riphard would never accept Kolo back after this. I tried to push him back but he kept coming, the madness burning through his eyes and an excited smile on his face. He was going to enjoy this I thought to myself with a grim sense of sickness filling my stomach. Burnie looked distraught. He tried, he really did, but Riphard would not listen. He stood over Kolo and put the whip around his throat, the horrible cold leather pushing into the flesh of Kolo's neck. When I looked at my brother then I saw him as he used to be. That little gabrin boy who would chase me through the woods and save me from the boars and steal muffins from old hag Figgis for us to share together. I saw him laughing with glee as a stables blew up and we ran away to hide in the shadows. I saw him jumping straight into a crocodile's mouth to try and kill it from the inside. I saw him secretly crying over the death's of our allies, when he thought no-one was looking... I saw him as a monster robbing two beautiful children of their mother and I took the finger off my trigger. I tried to talk to him Kolo, I really did. I told him it wouldn't bring her back, and that you were not well, and that if he did this then we have lost you forever. But he had already made his mind up. I think he had made his mind up a long time ago. Because it wasn't what happened today that made him kill you Kolo. I naively thought that losing his wife and the threats on his children would have been too much. But Riphard is not built like that. Riphard is driven by pride and you made a fool of him too many times. All those little slights and acts of one-upgoblinship. He wasn't thinking about Delilah. He was thinking about not getting away with robbing a family that one time. He wasn't thinking of his uncle. He was thinking of all the times you saved us from an impossible enemy robbing him of the glory of the last shot. He wasn't thinking about the children. He was thinking about every time you laughed at him and showed him that you were a better person, even as an inferior gabrin, than he could ever be. He shouted out his childhood friend's name as he killed you for goodness sake. I think you would have found that funny.\medskip

I want you to know that I did try to save you Kolo. I'm sorry that you are not you anymore and that we won't be seeing you again. You were the best of us and when you went the team lost something important and beautiful. I'll remember you as you were.\medskip

I think I need to spend some time away now Kolo. I need to try and make up for all the bad things that I have done. I need to learn to live without you. I'm sorry to leave you Burnie, but I know you'll be strong and look after the others. Myron, I hope you can forgive me one day. He meant too much to me... I'm sorry that this happened to Delilah. I just... I thought I could save everyone.\medskip

We were meant to be helping people. We were meant to be heroes...\medskip

I think I deserve this.\medskip
