\subsection{Episode 70: The Hunt for Google Von Maccherstein}

\DndDropCapLine{T}he airship drifts uneasily down from the sky, piloted inexpertly into the city of Linderdorf. Only a handful of steam vents and a set of huge brass doors belies the metropolis built into the mountain. Mr and Mrs Burnie’s dad promise to take care of the kids on the airship while the Shitshow lead Kevin into the city below.\medskip

In the city proper, the gang find themselves in a dense, hot, warren of foundries, mills and shops. Automatons are scattered throughout the populis, lithe, talkative models serving in shops, and hulking, heavily built models acting as workers and guards.\medskip

Kevin chirps up talking about history and shit.\medskip

Linderdorf acts as a bastion of civilisation and industry against The Varg, shipping weapons, inventions and steel all over Velterra.\medskip

No one listens to Kevin, though, because they’re trying to guess who wrote “Vergun” on his face in pencil. It was Myron, who is smashed on a cocktail made of:\medskip

One Part Sherry\medskip

One Part Grenadine\medskip

One Part Bovril\medskip

that he calls a Piece of Shit, and claims he named it after Rip. Myron does jokes now.\medskip

Myron smells meat, and tries to lead everyone to the meatwaft.\medskip

A dwarf man is roasting sausages. Myron buys 20 schnitzels. Riphard buys the business. The dwarf man seems fairly satisfied.\medskip

A passerby is asked for directions to the best blacksmith in the city, and replies that a team of Google Von Maccherstein and someone else (Märöck Grünhelvem and Jüppi Indelnacht) won the Blacksmith Cup this year, and that he is a Salumist. The party learn that sausages are actually just a type of face.\medskip

Suddenly, unicorns become untrustworthy with money. It is unclear how this will impact things going forward.\medskip

Schnitzels are revealed to just be sausages, and sausages are faces.\medskip

The gang leave the area, following Myron, and spend 10 minutes walking in circles around a building that seems to be a spa, ending up back where they started.\medskip

Burnie is unable to buy a schnitzel.\medskip

All in all, everyone seems to be enjoying this brief moment of non-dread.\medskip

Burnie puts on a disguise and tries again to buy a schnitzel. He succeeds.\medskip

They decide to leave in a way that wouldn’t just bring them back.\medskip

They end up in a pub, where a three-armed robot barman offers them a selection of the local delicacies. He does not offer to mix them all together in a big jug. He refuses to let the party mix them all together in a big jug.\medskip

They get one of each and promise not to mix them.\medskip

After drinking them, they ask the barman where they can find Google Von Maccherstein. He says to wander around where they might be and see if we bump into them. He asks why they want to see him, and hopes they don’t mind. They do, but what is conversation but polite prying?\medskip

They leave, smashed.\medskip

Rip gets fed up of hanging around, and starts harassing people in their homes to see if they’re Google Von Maccherstein. They say they aren’t, and Myron tries to do it better, but does it a bit the same.\medskip

Kevin gets back in contact with the local Thief Guild, and asks her if she knows where Mr McDonald and Google McKenzie are. After a brief bit of Thieves Cant natter, they find out the name of their workshop.\medskip

He comes back out, to find the others interrogating a lamp post. Myron takes the lead and Burnie directs him with strategically placed vomit.\medskip

They arrive at the workshop, and the small robot bird outside suggests that its the one they’re looking for. They knock on the door, and apparently the gnome who opens it isn’t Google. Which is inconvenient.\medskip

They ask if he can work the special metal. He says he doesn’t want to talk to these scary drunk people. Myron starts shovelling money through the crack in the door, and Burnie barges through.\medskip

Apparently, his masters are out exploring the Varg lands, looking for inspiration by touristing in a war zone, and he doesn’t know exactly where.\medskip

He gets solidly harrassed, threatened, and upset. He informs the moderately sensible ones among the party that the guards have already been called, and that they should probably get out of there, and come back in the morning.\medskip

Myron, already sleeping on the floor, gets taken to an alley and tucked in to a nice comfy pile of metal. Burnie instead decides to slumber in a nice pool of vomit.\medskip

The two conscious members go off to get clean.\medskip

Kevin and Riphard make small-talk as they spend an enjoyable evening getting to know each other while they bathe and receive many massages. Topics include Riphard’s Dead Wife, Riphard’s Cursed Children, and Riphard’s recent murders. Kevin remains fully clothed.\medskip

The next morning, they return to collect Burnie and Myron and meet with the dwarf they assaulted the night before. Burnie is slick with vomit. Myron sleeps serenely in his metal nest.\medskip

They collect themselves, and knock on the door of the workshop, and have a slightly more cordial conversation than the night before.\medskip

The dwarf they were talking to yesterday is apparently called something like, but not, Burnice. The gang present themselves as willing to find Google, and see if they need rescuing. To be honest, they just want them to do the metal, though.\medskip

Burnice explains they went to look at the Varg weapons, to get ideas for the big Blacksmith race, and provides documents and maps detailing the route they were planning to take.\medskip

Myron asks, purely hypothetically, if it would make Google more amenable to them, if he knew that he was the son of Professor Jorj Myron of Linderdorf University. Everyone is suddenly shocked to remember Myron has a first name.\medskip

Having looked at the stuff, and being given a picture of Google to look at, they say their goodbyes to Burnice, and head back out to the airship, to begin in earnest The Hunt for Google Von Maccherstein.\medskip
