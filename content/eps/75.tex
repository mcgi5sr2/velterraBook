Episode 75 - How to Kill Giant Robots and Influence People

So. The night before the big robo-show down. We headed back to the airship and I took some time to inspect the work that the gnomes had been doing for us. We now had new cannons and smoke screen and a reinforced hull. Very shiny and impressive all in all. Wanting to be fresh for the morning we decided to go to sleep. But the night had other plans for us.

We woke up in the waiting room. The squad took a bit of time to discuss the finer details of why our clothes follow us into hell. Meredith tried to explain to Myron unsuccesfully before leading us into the office. This time there was a large scale map of Velterra in the office instead of the usual kitsch furnishings. Lazarus was still butthurt about the battle simulator challenge but he explained that now that the end was coming into sight, maybe we should start putting together a plan to sort out whatever was keeping the gods out of the world and destroy it. Martin was very keen to ask about the murder of his previous employer. We were all a bit confused about the plan because we were sure that Lazarus had said friendship was the answer to everything. Burnie spends two hours every morning jerking off, presumably due to the difficulties of having a hook. Lazarus said that number one priority was bringing the gods back to the world. Myron was very adamant that the priority was to save Arbiter Kiros. Burnie claims that the greater the number of soldiers firing at you, the lower the chance of being shot. Burnie is the single most bullet ridden scarred man I have ever known. I asked if we knew what was keeping the gods away and Lazarus was a little shocked to find out that I had not used the cube to try and locate the source of the power. Egg on my face I suppose. Myron had many thoughts. While they debated I slapped myself to wake up and then got to work with the cube trying to locate the blockage. I focused really really really hard and the cube started rotating and clicking and glowing. Then suddenly there was a pulse. It was pretty clear that it was coming from The Seven’s Spire. I made myself a milky drink and drank it really quick and tried to go back to sleep.

In hell the boys were discussing 733t cryptography methods in order to trick the folk over at the spire and get them out of the way. There was a lot of talk about making use of the friends we have made along the way such as the Gary’s and the Tiger and our bird-based assassin friends. Friendship really is magic. Oh by the way, someone remembered that there was a festival of light coming up which is a celebration of all the gods in two weeks time. During it a lot of people pilgrimage to the Seven’s Spire. Mr Lazarus told us that he had been busy overseeing the torture of Mr Rubriks. He had not really found out more about kalimar or Kolo yet, only that Kalimar was a master of warping flesh. He also mentioned Kanalon. Burnie asked an excellent question which was if we free the gods then would we also be releasing all these mad hell beings as well. Laz was like probs no, who knows. Martin asked why the gods were separated in the first place. There are a lot of things being brought up here that we probably should have asked a long time ago. Gap. Mr Lazarus says we should take better notes. Or at least read them.

Martin offered us some help from the Varg friends that he has. Burnie decided that we should go and anger the Tiki-tuks then lure them along from the Temple of Unthala to the Spires. This was a pretty stupid plan. Everyone is going to send out messages for any contacts they have to get them to descend on the festival. Wake up time.

Burnie was kicked out of hell just in time to get a bucket of water thrown over him by Martin. A very bloodied Kevin walked in with a pittance of wax, an incredible feat for the area given their well established wax deficits. He collapsed and I took him to the infirmary.

We made our way over to the battle scene. A big oval with all the seats around it and the crowd gradually filling up. We took a bit of time to moan about the images they had put up of us on the banners. Riphard was not joining us preferring to spend time warming up his breast milk with his own elbow heat. As we entered we were met by a relatively young gnomish man called Grup who was the events co-ordinator. We asked about if there was any gambling about us. We were running at four to one against. We went to gamble. We bet 1500 gp eight times for us to win.

We made our way into the arena. There was so much music and shouting and excitement all around the arena. Difficult to hear really. Omg though this stone robot was so big. Not as big as the mountain lizard thing but still pretty big. Maybe like 30 ft. There was even smoke and stuff as it came up out of the ground on a platform. Bit much. Burnie took the lead in his excited state. He actually ran straight at it. Martin knelt down and Mr Mouse got out to take a moment to rub some dirt onto his face in two lines under his eyes. Then he got back in. Hardcore. The robot moved forwards to Burnie and hit him with its full force three times in a row. It was pretty devastating. Blood everywhere. Myron headed in. I sent a forcefield at Myron and then took a shot at the stone one. CEDRIC is AMAZING, he did such a good shot and he has such a funny voice that makes me go all trembly. But there was not time to enjoy, I pressed the button and STANRI went rigid, standing upright and opening up ready, a thousand small actuators rearranging his body into the perfect killing machine (but also the cutest!). Burnie came over and I heard Mark’s voice in my head which was upsetting.

Mr Mouse and Martin threw some kind of storm sphere at the robot which looked pretty cool. Some guy shouted in a high pitched voice for it to use its shocking burst but it did not actually do anything apart from spark a little. Myron tried to jump onto its leg but it was a lot slippier than expected and he couldn’t quite get on. He tried to hit it in the groinal area but it did not have the main bits that boys have that could have. I got into STANRI in a really cool like kawaii montage thing and I heard with satisfaction “All systems online”. We strafed and then shot CEDRIC at him which hit but some kind of energy field seemed to divert some of the damage. Burnie hit the stone monster with a stone dagger twice. The robot started firing missiles at Martin and Myron took the opportunity to attack.

The hand came off the monster and rained down fire all over Burnie and Myron. Myron started climbing it again and threw in a load of hits that resulted in more sparks. He was up to the knee. STANRI and CEDRIC and I moved around and gave it another blessed shot. We got a face full of magic missiles for our efforts. Burnie went nuts then Martin continued his lighting attack, the lightning striking aggressively against the side of the arena causing the crowd to cheer wildly. The robot did not look happy and we saw it turn towards us and fire off two massive cannon attacks which hit fully in the chest. Myron was enraged and brought out Godrbinger. He launched himself into the air giving the robot a brutal barrage finishing with a final shot that separated the robot in half. Myron screamed and after a few seconds the crowd joined in as well, the entire stadium being filled with noise. Burnie raised his arms and yelled to them “ARE YOU NOT ENTERTAINED”. Myron looked around desperately trying to find out who was doing the announcement. A speaker came out of Stanri and Myron took the mic. “Ladies and gentleman, we fight here today in a righteous cause. We are to take this cause to where it stands. The church have for too long suppressed the people of this land, keeping the technology locked into this city. We are planning an assault to retake hope’s rest for the free peoples of this land. We will take this robot and use it to free up a land that should be free. If you are interested in using your bodies to defend the cause then you are welcome to join us.” There was a few seconds of nervous silence before a good quarter of the crowd started to shout again and join in the chants. Martin joined in “This is your chance for freedom. Have you got still fear in your bones. You should be using technology to free your lives.”. I leaned over and gave him the word he was looking for. Oh Kolo, if you could have heard the three quarters of the crowd yelling “EMANCIPATION” I think you would have enjoyed that. Martin threw glitter into the air as he thunderstepped out.

Wrapping up, we won 48000 gp from the foolish gamblers. The robot creators kept their word and agreed to hand over the robot. I requested a small modification for a small room in the head about the same size as a mouse ;). They started to get working on making the repairs. As we rode back I spent a bit of time on the Die of Rubriks and think that I figured out a way to make all of us and the airship go a bit faster. On second thoughts, I had another crack at it and got a higher level function. I was gobsmacked at what it revealed to me. It can’t be. Surely not …….

We headed back to Lazarus’ war room, I couldn’t speak all the way. Lazarus wondered if we could get Lillith to infiltrate the spire. Myron was concerned about time but we can move fast now. The robot transportation shouldn’t be a problem. We can fit it in the cargo with a couple of engineers to help assemble on the other side. Martin had a good idea to drop a giant robot out of the airship. Surely that would create enough of a distraction to keep everyone busy while we raided the tower. Lazarus revealed that the inquisition probably know about the artefact and it would be well protected. Myron suggested we start listing our allies that we could call upon. Friendship. Fish people. Police. Gary’s. Myron was using big words and Burnie was feeling ‘wordiferous’. Turns out that Martin has been reading to them in their sleep in order to improve their vocabularies. Myron asked if it was possible to see into Lillith’s dreams. The debate went back and forwards for a long time. We decided that with the speed increase and the airship we could be anywhere before the enemy had a chance to act. We are fast now. Possibilities are endless. 9 days to get to Masuda and back to Seven Spires. We are going to go fetch the ninja assassins. You’re going down church. You’re going down.

Epilogue

It was very late. The others had long since gone to sleep. Exme was sat on her bed, a single candle lighting the room. The cube lay in front of her, cold and staring. Surely it wasn’t possible. It was too good to be true. She picked the cube up and started slowly rotating it, almost absent mindedly. As her fingers moved across its face objects in the room started disappearing, time slowed and re-started. A small door appeared, the smell of freshly baked bread wafting out and filling the room. Eventually Exme found the configuration she was looking for and the room became still and silent once again. The cube was softly glowing in her hands, almost offensively mundane. Exme stared at the cube. She could swear that it was looking back. Myron thought we could secretly arrive to the spire in advance and spend some precious time setting up the robot.
