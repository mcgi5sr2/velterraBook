\subsection{Episode 76: The Play's the Thing}

\DndDropCapLine{T}he party are up late, penning letters to their various allies and friends around the continent. They conjure a picture of a mounting popular uprising brewing in Hope’s Rest, a Church plot to quell it, an encroaching Varg attack organised by the church as a decoy. The printing press is to be used to publish large amounts of revolutionary literature, aided by the Garys the Jennies and Archibald.\medskip

Burnie sees Mr McDonald and Google Von Macherstein to see if they want to come. They do not.\medskip

Myron says goodbye to his dad, and it’s as awkward as you’d expect.\medskip

The big robot is stowed in the hold, disassembled, and the new cannons are primed, and they are ready to go.\medskip

Esme plugs the cube into the ghost engine, and tells everyone to strap in. She smacks a lever forward and the crew are pinned against their seats as they move literally quicker than anyone has moved before.\medskip

The crew entertain themselves. Esme and Myron play the travel version of a game where you spell words with little letter tiles. Esme spells “GABRIN”, Myron spells “AB”, Esme spells “MYRON”, Myron spells “BMO”, and Esme says he wins the game, and not just because she doesn’t want to play any more.\medskip

They suddenly realise they’ve massively overshot Tom’ardy and Burnie starts reversing back.\medskip

Esme goes to get Rip, who has been locked in his room with his weird corpse wife mannequin and two sacks of rotten pork he seems to think are his children. Esme for some reason thinks that Myron would be good at talking sense into Rip. She is, understandably, proven wrong.\medskip

Eventually, they sort everything out, and go out to see what’s happened to Tom’ardy. It’s a socialist utopia now, and they’re the heroes.\medskip

They go to find the council, in their shiny new collectivist council chamber, to try and see if they can work out who Lilith is this time. There’s a random elf with wobbly ears on the council, but she doesn’t seem to know where Lilith is.\medskip

The gang half-heartedly try and sell the concept of spreading the revolution to Hope’s Rest, but they’re starting to realise that it’s quite a sell. They like the concept, but most of them think\medskip

The wobbly-eared elf suggests they talk separately, in an anti-chamber, which is kept safely isolated from the chambers.\medskip

Riphard is eating his own shit.\medskip

Esme gives Delilah her special mission, which is clearly to help Riphard to get over the death of his wife by disguising herself as Delilah for a few months, being killed so that he can try to grieve better next time.\medskip

Lilith isn’t particularly wrapped about this, and so, instead, they begrudgingly tell Lilith the real plan, with the enormous, deadly decoy war, the clockwork ninjas, Lilith installing herself in the vipers nest, and then the invisible robot attack suicide mission. She’s not that thrilled about this either.\medskip

Myron asks if she could try disguising herself as Kolo instead.\medskip

Riphard is producing consommé.\medskip

Lilith says that she’s going to try and convince the council to help the Hope’s Rest part of the mission first, and then decide about her other bit. She says that the gang should start trying to drum up some support among the people while she talks to the council.\medskip

They decide to perform the greatest play ever played.\medskip

They perform the greatest play ever played.\medskip

The play is too pure to be recorded. True beauty is, by its nature, ephemeral.\medskip

It is performed several times throughout the day, with increasingly experimental ad-libs each time.\medskip

After the last performance, Lilith approaches the group, and announces that she has successfully convinced the council to commit to the party’s bizarre and immoral suicide mission, and moreover that she agrees to play her part in the plan.\medskip

Esme gets excited until Lilith assures her that she doesn’t mean she’ll pretend to be Delilah.\medskip

Lilith comes aboard the ship and they head off. Next stop, New Abbergast.\medskip
