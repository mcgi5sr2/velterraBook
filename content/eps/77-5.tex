\textbf{/u/magnificentjosh}\\
Myron gets out huge sheets of paper from somewhere, and spends days on his hands and knees, sketching and drawing arrows and diagrams, trying to remember everything he knows about Seven's Spire.\medskip

Looking over his shoulder, you can see various bits scribbled out, or torn off so completely. You can also see sections made illegible through having large quantities of Bovril spilled over them. A recurring theme seems to be trying to work out where the most likely place for Kiros (often represented by a stick figure with a sad face and a comically oversized gun) to be imprisoned is. Several areas of the spire have been circled in red pen, but most of them have been crossed out.\medskip

When not in his quarters with the papers, Myron is quiet. He works throughout the night, sleeping through most of the day. He occasionally paces around the deck, dismissing anyone who tries to strike up casual conversation.\medskip

The smells of burning oils and incense can be smelled from his room from time to time, as he observes various religious rituals, both those supported by the central church, and those confined to the uncivilised outposts far from prying eyes other than his. He has never been interested in fulfilling those Arbiter duties intended to tame the wild heart of his religion.\medskip

You also hear him training physically, getting used to his new armour, shield and weapon. You struggle to habituate to the gunshots coming from his room while you try and sleep, and the multiple new large holes in the exterior of the ship. The pile of broken training dummies outside his room grows gradually. They all seem to have crudely drawn smiley faces, all with an eye patch.\medskip

\textbf{/u/TheFailBus}\\
After the four days of travel you can see the shores of the Masudan continent. You fly towards the city of Masuda wanting to see if the giant lizard is still on its trail of destruction. As you get closer, you see no lizard, and the 'mountain' is laid in a similar, but not identical, position to where it was when you first saw it. The city of Masuda is chaotic - there was obviously a huge swathe of destruction across the city, many areas of the city are still complete rubble but there has been a lot of rebuilding since you were last here. Fresh buildings and those in construction scatter the city inbetween burnt husks and craters.\medskip

As you look further afield, you can see large troughs and burn marks across the landscape. The monster must have gone on a rampage further into the country before returning to its rest.\medskip
