Episode 79 - The Time Warp


A shakey alliance has been brought together by somewhat devious means thanks to the group's charm and guile. Representatives from Abberghast, including the royal king fish Oceanus himself, Hope's Rest commoners, the Guild of the Garys, assassins, Tom'ardy dwarves, and Linderdorf robots and robot cannons have all gathered on this fair field a days march south east of Hope's Rest. It is here the church will face their reckoning and a true test of their fate.

Tactics are hastily discussed in hushed tones for fear of spies. Fear and nervousness at the mysterious nature of the enemy about to be faced ripple through the rank and file. King Oceanus stands tall on his litter and gapes his maw to the group. A bellowing cry, loud yet soothing, falls upon the virgin ground, bolstering morale amongst the brave army brought together from across the entirety of Velterra.

The plan is to bring their army within range of our powerful barrages, but plans have to be flexible in the face of the unknown.

Ready positions are taken: the raged units of the Hope's Rest Guard and the Linderdorf cannons staying back atop two crests, with the Abberghast fishmen and a group of the Garys and assassins holding the left flank, and the central ground held primarily by the dwarves with the odd band from the others here and there to fill out what is sure to be the main battle ground.

A low continuous thunder is noticed as armour begins to rattle, gently at first, but ever building. A loud CRACK shocks the air and a plume of smoke rises in the distance. A warning shot. A brag. A threat. The enemy approaches.

God's own army presents itself across the expanse. A brief standoff seems to linger as each side eyes the enemy for the first time, prepared but wary. The riflemen steadily raise their arms. Another crack pierces eardrums and bullets rain down upon the infantry, arrows upon the fishmen. Shaken by the sudden commencement of battle the alliance infantry begins to retreat: the church must be lured into our limited range.

Priests upon horseback thunder down the left flank towards the fishmen, peppered with yet more arrows and hundreds lost already. The cavalry bear down upon the Abberghastians as the Hope's Rest Guard let loose a round of bolts straight at them from high.

Across the battle field the Linderdorf cannons begin their onslaught immediately crushing hundreds of church riflemen and archers. The bulk of the church infantry begin their death march toward the alliance stronghold.

Their numbers already weakened by cannonfire the riflemen and archers within range turn their attention to the robots in the hopes of spooking or even killing the limited number of these powerful units. But these steely beings show no signs of fear.
