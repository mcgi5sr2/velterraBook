Episode 80: The End is Night

Freshly unbound within the Seven Spire, Martin wonders who the hell this woman setting them free and talking to Myron like an old friend. I sneak a quick listen just in time to learn about the first few floors of the tower:

Floor One: Stores, servants quarters and guard training

Floor Two: More guards rooms

Floor Three: Officers quarters

Floor Four and upwards totally unknown

Well that's not great news but I guess it's more church scum for the grinder when the inevitable happens.

Our little gabrin creator is busy playing with her cube of delights, in many ways I would delight in connecting with such a device, but the price would be memories I have long kept hidden.

I introduce myself to this strange lady, as Myron interrogates her as to troop numbers and locations, I lose interest anyone who tries to stop us now is going to feel the full measure of the rage that dwells in our souls.

It’s time to get this circus on the road again, Myron leads the way straight into the break room for the guards who are as surprised as we are to see them. Myron is not terribly convincing about our reasons for appearing and not being in the chains like the last time they saw us. I nip behind the door to disguise myself as Lillith and start a ruse about us grabbing some food and that they should leave my prisoners the fuck alone! It still amazes me that people fall for this kind of shit. Spurred on by our lack of success so far Myron opens another door right next to the previous one, it really is a wonder this man is still alive. Lo and behold its the kitchen.. This time he meets like minded souls, or what one could described as intellectually challenged cooks, who are easily convinced that we should be given some vol au vents.

Having successfully negotiated the kitchens we take the only remaining door up the stairs to a maze of small private rooms, arenas, archery ranges and other related martial training areas. Myron comes at us with the good idea of finding some book nerds he is convinced reside around here. Meanwhile Riphard tugs off a statues penis.

We open a door that oddly says “something” above it in old dwarvish. Its disguise time and as a low energy alternative to the magical shifting I am used to we just dress up in an old grey cossack kinda robe popular in foothills below the Varg mountain ranges. Riphard wears his golden robe over the cossack one as “king of the peasants” he now ranks up there with almost all kings. Cryptically he also describes how he and Exmerah have already done the death thing. I feel a pang of jealousy, but push it away. That was who I was, I have changed many times, and this latest incarnation is far better suited than its predecessor. Burnie gets a bit serious about how we should approach the coming levels with more guile and subterfuge. He is probably right, but Myron whose ken is often beyond that of the mortal man, lets us know it is still the hi-jinx levels, and that the serious ones will come later. Before we leave, someone notices that Stanri is not going to make a good book nerd, time to flex again. I disconnect and reconnect as many areas of stanris mechanical systems as I dare, doing what must look like a drastic overhaul of his systems before I enfuse some divine eminence into him, allowing for the refraction of light around his physical form. The game is good, but Exmeh is very concerned over her mecha suit, and starts using the detectomatic to check of magic elements. She casts it quickly over my own body and it kicks up a few notches….

I try and distract from the issue and once again engage Myron in the lets go and kill everyone speech. The more I its inevitable the more I begin to believe it, this group is so careless I fear that any and all encounters will always end in bloodshed. For now we agree to stop arguing, collaborate and listen, at least until we recover Kyross.

The next level is swarming with guards, a large corridor looms before us, the walls trim and formal are lined with notices and posters. As we wander down posing as just another group of book nerds we make good progress. That is until a group stops us next to a familiar group of posters. A few double and triple takes occur between us the guards and the posters before I try and pretend that we aren’t the adventurers they are looking for. Even with a sprinkle of the divine gnu image manipulation program on the posters they are having none of it.

Riphard takes the only recourse of action and blows a number of them to kingdom come, Burnie electrocutes almost all of the rest of them who do a fish like flop on the floor as electricity pulses through them. He hacks a few of the remainers to pieces who now no doubt had wished they had voted to leave instead. Exmerah breaks out of the divine cloak of darkness and unless another hugh cacophonous roar of energy on the guards. The noise is as terrible as the destruction, leaving only a handful for Myron to put out of their misery. The bloodshed has begun in earnest.

The next level is as fancy as the mansion back home, but a lot less populated than the guard quarters on the level below. We take the time to peer out the window and see our giant robot wreaking havoc outside. Not far above it and circling its head is a giant mechanical eagle mounted by female ninjas. These are not foes I would wish upon anyone.

For reasons that I would later find hard to explain, I take off my head and place it in Stanris mouth. With our powers combined, I push the divine cloak of obscuration over us again and set out down the corridors. What we find is rich living, book reading, sherry swilling ?paedos?.

Not surprisingly when we recount what we saw back to the group, Burnie starts to have a mental break, the number of books is just too damn high. Despite the break of what I would consider reality we manage to coax Burnie to the floor exit, which is guarded by four piety guards dressed in what looks like very thick steel plate. The are guarding the exit which is also covered by an even thicker looking steel portcullis. What purpose this would serve in the case of a fire in the building is briefly discussed before Burnie gets the final piece of his puzzle. Exmerah and Myron mutter in the background about whether this is still alpha Burnie or we are now closer to omega Burnie, something about it is really worrying. There is little time to consider it further as Burnie starts a mad dash towards the room with the old sherry drinker and a mountain of books. It takes him seconds after bursting through the door to unload some hook lit crossbow bolts into the bookcases either side of the room, which understandably incenses the old sherry drinking toff. The toff promptly rolls backwards off his chair and pulls a gun from his hip holster in the process. He marks Burnie well, landing a piece lead firmly in his chest before Burnie manages to vault the toffs desk and discharge his filthy electrical energy into the toffs bowels. The hook rips the old gentleman open, and charred entrails start covering the floor. Burnies big day out is not over though, as he is bellowing “That’s what you get for reading books!” a few more guards come bursting in to see what the commotion is. Burnie dashes past them, and then seals the doorway behind him with the stone dagger. Good job that has multiple uses in a day really….

Elsewhere with the rest of the party, Myron starts into the fire warden role to convince the purity guardsmen that its evacuation time. Sadly Myron is less convincing than we all wished he was, and the guards are taking none of his BS this time. Combat of the mortal variety begins.

I rush past the guards and and blend into the weave to bypass the portcullis behind them, associating myself with reality on the other side. Sadly there is no lever here, so I dust them in fey wild magic markers. 18s later and Myron and Exmerah are standing over the dead bodies of the guards, I do wonder if we should give these people more of a chance to flee before we murder them, but then I recall the churces persecution….

Good thing I do as we meet another three guards past the portcullis, turns out the lever was on our side all along. As an invisible bear blocks there escape path Myron rushes in and starts the dismembering process, its takes a few seconds more than we are used to, and Riphard has to lay in some serious heat, but they all die in the end.

Stanri starts moaning and whining, and Exmerah says he smells food, we have vol au vents so end up ignoring and exploring a whole host of very fine and posh rooms. We figure they must be set up for dignitaries, but they seem to be unused of recent. We wander pretty aimlessly for a few more minutes before Exmerah finds a secret door next to a balcony we all assume will be important at some point, but never is.

At the top of the stairs is a large grand hallway, there is no decoration as it is cold stone and stark as Burnies knackers. Standing either side of two heavily armoured doors are four piety guards, an inquisitor, and a marshall. No use hiding anymore we stumble in like fools, and are received as creepily as the others have come to expect from an inquisitor. The inquisitor turns out to be only slightly softer than I had heard, the fight quickly descends into a bloodbath. Myron even gets dirty fingered to death by the inquisitor at one point. Thankfully it's not real death, as he is really starting to grow on me. We take an absolute beating off them all, before we are able to give them the long kiss goodnight and start looting their bodies. Seems like the thing to do really… we find a massive set of keys, and go exploring the two doors. The first leads to a guards restroom, plenty of those in here but at least this one has a set of stairs to the next level. Myron has a flash of brilliance and realises the key set is probably a jailors keys set. So the other door must be the prison. He is not wrong, but dam is it depressing when we get through the door.

The corridors reak of death and decay, the walls are damp and the air is musky, foul and fetid. We must have killed the cards as we find no one inside. The corridor leads off in a curved arc doors on either side for as far as the eyes can see. We split the key bunch and start crashing through doors. Most cells are empty, the few that aren’t either have rotting remains or full on desiccated bodies. Near the end of the first corridor we find almost dead human who can barely speak and a very psychologically scarred dwarf. Interrogation is impossible with the state they are in so we do what we can with them, and carry them on with us. As we push through more corridors we find a few more people hanging on, signs of torture and abuse are rife on all of them. The church's good image clearly does not extend to this level of the spire.

As we are tending to the wounded, and working up a plan of how to get them to safety without compromising our own objectives, Myron gets ahead of us all. He finds another large steel door slightly ajar, opening it slowly and carefully we finds the body of a naked lady strung up on a rack. The room is full of metal instruments glinting in the light as they hang from chains, or slowly rotate on spindles not long spun. He takes his time, treading carefully as he approaches, then on recognising Kyros pulls out a fist full of cured beef jerky and stuffs it into her mouth. She is alive, barely and the beef jerky almost chokes the last of her life force from her body. Somehow the pure essence of Myrons will is infused into the jerky and seeps some strength back into Kyros.

As she splutters back to the realm of the living, she starts demanding if we have seen Arbiter Kahn, I say we as by now the have dragged what is left of the living with us to the same room. Seeing a member of the Church stood in front of me, I try the only thing that is left for me. I try to kill her, and avenge all those I have just released from succour and imprisonment. Myron pins me to the grounds screaming all shades of obscenities before I relent. Things are clearly not as they seem here. Exmerah and I go for a walk, during which time she explains how barring her all the members of our party worked for the church at some point! Flabbergasted I can barely believe I had been taken in by such charlatans. Exmerah goes on to explain a lot of things, that I really feel could have, no, should have been out in the open from the get go! Before I can get to riled up we come across the prison stores. We take the lot and return whats owed to all the captives, this time including Kyros.

With no real plan of what to do with our new found ?friends? Exmerah puts up an interplanar pocket dimension with a very nice door within one of the prison rooms. We setup the prisoners in the 16th Cell on the left. Once safely installed we start making our way out, it feels high time we got to the pinnacle of this tower and finished our business once and for all. Unfortunately when we get close to the prison door, we hear a key in the lock. Arbiter Khan has returned, and has brought two of his pets with him. The Inquisitor Ravagers are massive hulking beings with rough hewn metal spikes inserted within their arms where hands should be. It can’t be a pleasant existence for them, which would explain their anger somewhat.

I barely last 6 seconds before Khan has danced fluidly through all of us and for a brief moment I am smelling the sweet scent of deaths release. Not this time, not on this day of all days. Kyros unloads a full set of chambered rounds into his as he advances towards her, only for his head to be vaporised by the sudden impact of a metal slug from Exmerahs linear accelerator gun. Myron and Burnie go to town on the ravagers, but without their leader they soon suffer a similar fate. As the ravagers are being spread liberally up and down the walls and corridor, Stanri moves in for some of the actions, and in the processes steps on Khans head, exploding like a melon under his mechanical foot. Riphard opens the gates on his holy weapon and lets rip a stream of pure energy, igniting one of the Ravages. Burnie finally frees his hook that was stuck in the ground, and uses the momentum to uppercut straight into a ravages chin, as life bleeds out of the ravages, I can’t help but feel a pang of guilt.s

Its short lived, as Myron starts a speech about how Pope Ezekiel the IIIrd is going to have to pay for this, I couldn’t agree more. Instead of riling Myron up further we take the time to disguise Stanri and I as inquisitors using the masks we have ripped off the inquisitors.
