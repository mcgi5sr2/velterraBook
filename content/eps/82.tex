\subsection{Episode 82}

\DndDropCapLine{M}ission report: Sgt E. Devereux\medskip

After the battle at the pit entrance my new colleagues Myron and Martin discuss the appearance and nature of an object they refer to as "Mrs Ball" They seem to be unsure about the equipment they use as a squad, however the spherical helper did not cause them any alarm so I decided not to question it further. As we proceeded further into the dig Riphard natural affinity for worked stone showed him the path most recently used out of the options available and this area had at one time been a granite mine. The party proceed forwards, bickering all the while.\medskip

The tunnel led to a narrow ledge skirting a steep drop into the pit below. There was another ledge above us that tracked along our path. Myron halted and signalled for quiet whilst he took in the way ahead. He looked back and drew our attention to a large pile of skeletal remains in the shape of a ball further down the path, also on the ledge above was an undulating mound of flesh.\medskip

As we planeed our next move if felt something touch the back of my armour. I looked down to see Exme placing something there "Protection. Many shields, maybe one hundred thousand." I reached out and patted her on the shoulder. "I'm not a child Erin" "I mean't it only in thanks, apologies"\medskip

I've only been with them a short time and still need to get to know them. If we are to work together I don't want trouble. I took a few steps forward and stretched out with divine senses, picking up nothing further. During this time Myron began to climb to the upper ledge. The fleshy mass began to move and Riphard let fly with a thunderous shot. I charged forwards, the divinely blessed senses revealing hidden creatures along the pathway. Myron finished his ascent only to be ambushed by a shadow, its cut draining some of Myron's strength. The rest of the party threw themselves into battle. In a flurry of polymorphed pony, wicked strikes, magical flair, a ball with deadly purpose and flying bullets the assailants are readily dispatched.\medskip

Myron's balletic barrage slammed into the shadow, knocking it back. Then another bullet passed cleanly through its head and it dissapated. Exme levitating nearby having fired the shot.\medskip

"I don't need help Exme!"\medskip

"You're welcome!"\medskip

To my alarm Exme then shot Myron, explaining that she was healing him. It's going to take a while to get used to this. They all then discussed the possibility that Myron is cursed. This arguement continued to escalate as we went further in. Clearly stealth isnt a tactic this team readily employs.\medskip

Finally the mine gave way to ancient stonework. Still arguing, loudly, we approached the only visible door. Inside we found a hunched up wrinkled old man. He said his name was Jeremiah. That he's very busy, lost, can't remember anything and is 24 years old. He made several references to a master that he is apprentice to. He's forgotten everything since coming out of the stasis tube. Martin gently pointed out that whatever this stasis tube is, something has gone wrong as Jeremiah is now an old man. He crumples up in shock and Martin comforts him whilst the rest of us look over the room. Exme notices lettering on the wall that are definitley red paint, yes. Not blood. Riphard, Burnie, Myron and Exme look over the script while I kept back to watch. They worked together quickly and impressively rapidly decoding the letters. Riphard stood back uttering the phrase "I will have the last laugh" there was a slight click and then a jolt as the floor gave way and we all tumbled into void below.......\medskip

Addendum: I have completed writing my letters to the families of the recon team deployed to the Varg. As their acting CO it is my duty and sad honour to inform them of their bravery and sacrifice. Those events still weigh heavily upon me, but for now there are more pressing things to deal with. I will send them for delivery at my next opportunity. I have also sent a note to the Company Captain informing them of my return to the empire and my current status.\medskip
