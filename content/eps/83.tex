\subsection{Episode 83: Cape of Good Pope}

\DndDropCapLine{T}he party fall down.\medskip

Their fall is hard and fast, as if it were a metaphor for their chances of saving the world.\medskip

They fall through a dark pit, a stygian ocean swimming with cackling laughter in a manner similar to the way mackerel or other similar fish swim in the water ocean. You know, the main one.\medskip

They eventually stop falling and crash into the ground, where the old man crumples into a broken heap and dies instantly. Everyone else is fine.\medskip

They find themselves in a high-ceilinged, ornately carved room, very different in vibe to the scratty old mine above.\medskip

The cackling voice starts talking instead of cackling and welcomes everyone to its games room. Everyone stands about bitching for a bit, until the voice announces the first game, which is just “answer this question”.\medskip

The voice says the question is “family friends or honour” and asks them all to pick which they prefer, but then has to awkwardly admit that it meant “family, comma friends or honour” and then actually “love, family or honour”.\medskip

Everyone answers, and then they have to fight some shit.\medskip

With a puff of dark light, the party are surrounded by strange creatures, unknown to this world. There’s some pale ladies with sharp-looking teeth, some see-through people and a stinky rotten dude covered in dirty rags. Everyone gets confused, because they instinctively assume that last one’s Burnie, but he’s got an enigmatic swagger to him they aren’t expecting.\medskip

One of the pale ladies runs in to give Burnie a hickey, but he and Myron manage to keep her away from the soft bits. The grotey dude shambles towards Exme, who shoots her big gun thing at him, but then freezes in existential terror.\medskip

Rip fires out at one of the ladies and summons forth motes of holy fire that reath the party in good fire, bolstering them against all this bloody damage.\medskip

A see-through person tries to kill Martin, but is repelled by BAD.\medskip

Burnie throws the weird lady at Paralysed Exme. It’s apparently supposed to be a good thing. Then he runs over and throws Paralysed Exme across the room, where she is immediately set upon by one of the see-through people. Again, he expects to be thanked for this.\medskip

Martin thanks him by turning him into a sperm whale.\medskip

Everyone agrees this is fair.\medskip

Dev says a prayer and everyone is suddenly surrounded by spectral glimpses of the gods, wielding spectral weapons against the unholy hoard.\medskip

Some fighting happens.\medskip

The sperm whale dodges in place and then tries to eat a ghost. It fails to do so, then flails around, bashing into shit.\medskip

Some more fighting happens.\medskip

Martin fires lighting out of his hands and then moves slightly to his right in a puff of smoke.\medskip

Dev decapitates two of the pale ladies with one swing. She gets dizzy and makes a fool of herself. Exme blasts the last of the skinny ladies with the big gun, before the lady slams hard into Mrs. Ball, but just manages to not die.\medskip

The whale thrashes.\medskip

Martin runs back to exactly where he was before he magically transported himself, blasts a lady into dust and then goes to try and ride the whale.\medskip

Dev uses her polearm to slide over the whale like the bonnet of a car, then lands on the other side, just as the images of the gods rip the gross guy apart.\medskip

Exme, hovering in the air dispatches one of the see-through people, before the last remaining guy rushes towards Mrs. Ball, narrowly avoiding colliding with her. Myron lays in wait, and thrusts his bayonet through the chest of the see-through guy, doing no damage and fires off two shots while clipped through the guy. Rip does a rhythmic gymnastic routine while the see-through guy claps excitedly before being dusted by Martin.\medskip

They try to levitate the whale out the room, but it is too big, so they force Burnie to do some Spiderman and Elsa shit to make themselves feel better.\medskip

They walk down the stairs out of the room which lead them down into a small, very odd chamber with an aquarium full of goo standing in the middle, with some undead giants standing around waiting to be killed, and then steps down into a cube space with a gross little guy hanging out attempting to monologue.\medskip

We don’t let him, and start the killing right away.\medskip

Or at least, Rip tries to, but his gun is full of ambergris. This weird guy fires a thing at Rip.\medskip

Martin tries to, but Myron makes him second guess casting whirlwind and he just doesn’t do anything. Nothing to do with an anti-magic field.\medskip

Exme tries to, but spends too long fiddling round for stuff in her pocket.\medskip

Mrs. Ball succeeds in fighting.\medskip

One of the skeleton giants is fed up of waiting, and walks over to the party. He introduces himself as the Pope of Skeleton Giants, and everyone is very impressed, apart from Dev, who pokes him, and gets a taste of the Papal Scimitar, which tastes like a communion wafer.\medskip

And that’s Canon.\medskip

Then she pokes the pontiff with the point of her pike again, in an act of blatant heresy.\medskip

One of the two skeleton cardinals attacks Mrs. Ball, recognising her as an affront to a world created by a loving god.\medskip

Myron runs up to engage the Cardinal in theological debate, surrounded by protective white flames, while the other one fails to hit Mrs. Ball, ontologically.\medskip

Valklondar monologues a bit and there’s a smelly cloud.\medskip

Rip stumbles around trying to get out of the smelly cloud, and nearly walks straight into the Papal Scimitar. Dev’s polearm thrusts out of the smelly cloud to defend him, and seizing the moment, Rip uses his whip to decapitate the Pope of Skeleton Giants. In doing so, he has feels a transfer of religious authority, as he becomes the new Pope of Skeleton Giants.\medskip

The cube wizard (not the other cube wizard, but the one who’s in this room) cackles maniacally as spectral hands rise out of the ground, clawing at Myron, who’s not really bothered.\medskip

One of the Skeleton Cardinals has an accident with Mrs. Ball.\medskip

Erin stabs a Skeleton Cardinal, aware of the danger that one of these two might see this as an opportunity to manipulate voting in the Conclave, if they don’t recognise the rules that make Riphard the one true Pope of Skeleton Giants. The church is officially divided on the matter of whether the Pope works on the same rules as Santa does in the Tim Allen film “The Santa Clause”.\medskip

The film is typically considered to be deuterocanonical.\medskip

Myron kills one of the skeletons.\medskip

The Cube guy fireballs everyone. Martin tells him not to, but he does anyway.\medskip

The sects which recognise Tim Allen films are typically known as Redbelts, after the 2008 martial arts film set in a prison.\medskip

Erin falls to the floor, a charred mess.\medskip

Mike Russell of the Portland Oregonian named Redbelt the 9th best film of 2008.\medskip
