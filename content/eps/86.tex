\subsection{Episode 86: Cat Scratch Fever}

\DndDropCapLine{L}ast time: went to the Varg. Lots of bonding on the airship. Invaded the Varg and made friends with Chesny Hawks and a veritable array/cornucopia of others, who were generally nice except that one that exploded and killed Martin. And that rude woman, Vesh. Martin had spent time here before. There were less than there used to be and they had been taken over by a giant who demanded we show deference, which we did but reluctantly. We then had drinks and a chat, before Myron challenged a snow leopard tiger man to an unarmed sparring match.\medskip

Martin is still dead in pieces and Esme starts attempting to reassemble him. Bernie wants to try and help but is shooed away. Myron warms up for his cat fight.\medskip

They agree to fight to first unconscious. There’s some passive aggressive banter about other rules. Zalat gets two hits in quick and using fancy monk shit does a 360 spin kick to Myron’s head. He taunts Myron who immediately goes to punch him in the balls, followed up by a swift elbow and headbutt, also to the balls. Myron’s all about ball play. Zalat coughs, clearly thinking this has become a medical check up.\medskip

Zalat connects with a stunning strike which Myron shrugs off then misses with a flurry, clearly shaken by Myron’s raw power. Myron goes for the back of the knee leg sweep, but it’s weak, but connects with the second. Zalat again misses with his attacks and adopts a defensive stance. Myron just goes for his face with a fist and a shoulder and connects hard with both.\medskip

Zalat manages to focus to connect with a strike, but Myron’s rock hard constitution means he barely feels it. Myron swings wildly for his kidneys connecting with only one. Zalat twats Myron squarely in the side of the head thrice. Myron elbows his legs and upper cuts his balls (for old times sake). Zalat gets two more slaps in. Myron’s not too bothered and attacks the inside of his bicep with a knuckle, an elbow, and a hammerfist.\medskip

Zalat, severely bloodied, still believes in himself and goes for a leg sweep, but misses, but does land one strike. Myron body slams onto the outstretched leg from the sweep, goes for another ball strike, but misses with a final strike. Zalat is wobbling on his feet, blood dripping into his eyes. He swings wildly missing badly, before connecting with two hard strikes. Myron still looks fine though and takes a step back, almost taunting him allowing him to take a swing, but Zalat misses. Myron charges him down and goes for a flying elbow leap, but misses, he twists in the air and connects with a knee instead and grabs his head and slams him into the dirt. RKO!\medskip

Zalat, still with Ki points left, hauls himself off the ground and tries to hit Myron in the face. Myron dodges these pathetic attempts and hits him square in the face, finally crumpling him into a heap.\medskip

There’s a bit of back and forth about foightin’. Myron reckons he could take everyone right here right now.\medskip

Ragnar asks Rip about his clothes. Rip is dressed in tight jeans and an open silk shirt. There is much talk about the various tribes and beliefs of the cultures around.\medskip

We are awoken early by the sounds of the camp at dawn. We are fed yak’s milk gruel for breakfast. There’s some banter with Krum regarding this. Esme checks that Martin has rebooted successfully. Martin seems grumpy about being turned off and on again. Martin wanted to go out in a blaze of glory.\medskip

We discuss our plans for the day and beyond. We think we have to kill the giant apparently. Riphard reckons we can poison him, possibly non-lethally, to achieve something similar. We have an intense discussion about potential approaches but get nowhere. Esme talks to her goat boyfriend. She presses him on the issues plaguing their tribe and pressure points we can exploit to challenge the giant leader.\medskip

We agree to go and help with a hunt to ingratiate ourselves more with the tribe and begin bonding ourselves more with the peoples. Myron bigs up his hunting skills and tries to narrow down what exactly we’re meant to be hunting. There’s a bit of banter with the giant leader who threatens to rip Myron's head off. Myron threatens the same. The giant says he couldn’t reach. Burnie says he didn’t specify which one. The giant says he still couldn’t reach. Riphard suggests this just means he has a small penis. Esme contacts Stanri to try and cheat on the hunt by finding out the best local game to hunt.\medskip

The group decide whether food or glory is more important in terms of what to hunt. Myron says lets go for one of each. Stanri gets back to us and tells us about boreworms (glory/food), cave mammoths (food), and the ashen rider (pure glory). Stanri doesn’t know anything about plants unfortunately to aid in the poisoning plot.\medskip

One of the tribe suggests hunting for elk and we all viciously mock him, which is a good start to ingratiating ourselves into the clan. Myron, the ranger, begins his hunt for a cave mammoth.\medskip

Dev starts having intrusive thoughts that the group is holding her back, but she has some doubts that these are her actual thoughts. She has had several of these before. She brushes these away yet again.\medskip

As the group drifts off to sleep they collectively dream of moldy flesh sloughing off of bones and decaying ruins. Their thoughts turn skyward as they see masses of wings and eyes, as a gargantuan purple dragonlike being thunders through space…\medskip
