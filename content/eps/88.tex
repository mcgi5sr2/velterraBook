\subsection{Episode 88: Movers and shakers}

\DndDropCapLine{W}e move to link back up with the rest of the tribe, hauling mammoth meat, hide and tusks with us.\medskip

We find them after six hours in a stand off over hunting rights with "The Adam-ants" (who are super badass looking, the Old Prophet has a tiny bonce though but live and let live).\medskip

Exme throws down some mammoth "Nice to MEAT you! How about some barbeque and vodka?"\medskip

We have an evening socialising with the Adam-ants. Exme makes friends with Aracia and her spider Zarathrustra. Burnie tries to talk to the Old Prophet but he's having none of it. We set up the mammoth egg incubator and ponder cave mammoth biology.\medskip

The tribe decides to head home, Martin goes with them to look afte the eggs. We decide to segue to hunt a bore worm. Magrat lends us Eric and Krum to help us hunt one. We travel to an ice shelf to set up an ambush. Eric begins to jump up and down to summon a worm. Myron senses one aproaching. Eric gives a thumbs up as the colossal worm breaks out of the ice, devouring him.\medskip

Battle ensues against the worm. Riphard getting the killing shot. We spend a few hours butchering it and comforting Krum over the loss of Eric before returning to the tribe. Burnie asking if Eric's furs and women are now going spare on account of him being eaten.\medskip

Magrat begins to speak about the possibility of us joining the tribe when he is interupted by the sound of horns blowing the summoning song of emmerdale farm. The Varg is to give a speech. We head out to hear what he has to say. Exme knocking Krum into the friendzone enroute.\medskip

We see Valkar Varg flanked by Pact warriors, hellish creatures. Valkar calls to march on the south, to seize what is theirs and to crush the weak southern gods. So that's that , the time for poisoning and overthrowing the varg has passed.\medskip

We bicker a lot. Erin sends a warning to Stanri that the Varg are moving to invade. Then we carry on bickering.\medskip
