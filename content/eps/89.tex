\subsection{Episode 89: Swamping the Weekend}

\DndDropCapLine{E}sme kneels on the ground and eats a handful of soil.\medskip

Everyone is weirded out by this.\medskip

The camp is packing up, preparing for war. Burnie writhes in a pile of furs. Myron talks to Magrat about going South and the war and stuff, and then apologises for the coup they were attempting to foment against him.\medskip

Magrat says that he doesn’t really care what they do, and Myron explains that they have a super secret mission for the war effort and won’t be coming South with them. Magrat says that he doesn’t really care what they do.\medskip

Everyone thinks about last night, when they were talking about their plan. Their plan is to find the hole in the ground where Kalimar lives and slither right down inside it. Esme’s plan is to eat soil.\medskip

Esme tries to convince Magrat to slither into the hole with them and help save the world, and, while he isn’t convinced of that, he is intrigued enough that he brings all the named characters on the journey to go and see Crankle, who they hope will be of some help with the plan to slither into the hole.\medskip

Esme goes dirt crazy and starts talking about “Swamping the Weekend”.\medskip

They go and see Crankle.\medskip

Crankle had a party and killed a load of kids.\medskip

Esme eats some goblin viscera, mixed in with dirt. Riphard falls into a pit of bloody meat.\medskip

Esme explains that goblins are weak to having their blood removed or their body parts separated, and that’s probably what’s happened here.\medskip

Esme then says that the weekend has been swamped and everyone forgets their worries for a minute, because of the funny clever thing.\medskip

Then everyone starts to think about the fact they’re standing in a big field full of corpses, and probably they should do something about this. Everyone goes about having a look for things. They surmise that maybe Crankle didn’t have a party and was stolen away by forces slightly darker than him, one of which might be the big winged dude who’s mates with Valkar.\medskip

Martin helps out by making one of the goblins not a corpse any more, and he’s a valued member of the group now who is very helpful and alive. Riphard is very helpful and provides lots of spare parts for the new friend to use because goblins don’t do very well without all their bits, apparently.\medskip

Together with their new friend, they discover the path, which is the one with the tracks, which just goes to show how good a friend he is to have.\medskip

They think about the fact that they’re heading towards where that weird temple was, and that’s probably where the hole is. Esme reckons Crankle was researching Kalimar goo, and that’s probably why he’s been taken to the hole.\medskip

They walk on down the road, and have a nice chat about pornography and sewing machines and stuff like that and how good parents Myron and Esme are because they know all about the parts of a sewing machine. Just some good friends swamping the weekend.\medskip

The Adamants are listening in and become jealous of the camaraderie on display, and come to get involved in a violent way. The spider lady is a fucking bitch, I guess.\medskip

They try and kill us, and Riphard gets hurt, which is nothing new. The spider gets turned into a duck, I guess.\medskip

Esme goes into her bag and gets out her latest project; a collaboration with David, who has been using his own alchemical methods to try and imbue life into a clay lady named Julie, who looks very surprised at the onslaught she unleashes.\medskip

Myron informs everyone about “Kalimel”, which is a cool thing they could call the Kalimar goo. Then he remembers he’s supposed to be fighting but doesn’t remember he’s using some dodgy shells he found in Esme’s bag, and is surprised when it explodes in a huge cone, which kills that spider lady bitch.\medskip

One of them blows some stuff up with her crazy blood, totally swamping the weekend. The spider gets blown out of the duck, and then Rip sends it into another dimension. Classic duck stuff.\medskip

The Lizard King yells and people hate it.\medskip

Martin calls forth the darkness at the centre of all things and the light is pulled from one of the bad ladies, ageing her nearly to death. Magrat cuts the Lizard King into pieces.\medskip

Myron blows a hole in some dude, Rip fires some guns, you know how it goes.\medskip

Some randos get involved, seeking the oblivion of death.\medskip

The bitch with the longbow tries to run away, as a result of which, Martin turns her into a duck at long range, and Esme uses the cube to set the lads on her before shooting her to pieces with a gun.\medskip

Magrat consumes a man whole.\medskip

Riphard alone witnesses this.\medskip

Everyone else finishes off the lady and Magrat takes her to pieces.\medskip

Everyone wanders off.\medskip
