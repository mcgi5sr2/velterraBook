\subsection{Episode 91: Sankes Don't Have Lips}

\DndDropCapLine{M}yron and Erin push open the heavy iron gates and greet the lipless giant snake-people.\medskip

Myron inquires about the goo going for the classic inspector play. He tries to deceive the snake-people. Riphard inquires who they are and who should be here after being told we’re not meant to be here. The snake people evade the question and immediately commence a fight.\medskip

Riphard, ever wary of snakes of all types, springs to action first. He just shoots one in its human-snake face. The first naga responds with a flame strike dealing damage to everyone. The naga Rip shot responds with a poisonous spit-take. Thankfully Rip’s hardy constitution and dwarven resilience greatly mitigate this grossness.\medskip

Myron readies his guknife and gets a read on their nature. They’re immune to poison and charm. Very standoffish. Myron runs up the hurt naga and shoots into it’s head, hitting once, but struggling to get a proper footing with his second and knife attacks.\medskip

Exme eats a soilwich.\medskip

Burnie attempts to sneak attack and gets a slash in.\medskip

Martin tries to polymorph one of them, but the nagas are very wise to his antics and it remains an ugly snake-person.\medskip

Erin gets in there with her glaive and does some major damage, carving up the first naga deeply before following through with her butt.\medskip

Rip gets another good shot in and takes cover behind the corner by the entrance.\medskip

The injured naga cures some of it’s severe wounds while the second naga slinkies up and allows Erin to swipe it, but she misses badly. It then dives in to eat Erin. Erin is swallowed whole and immediately succumbs to the immense attack before the naga vomits up her semi-corpse . Myron, seeing this, wants to try and eat the snake. He angrily shoots it twice more in the head before plunging his bayonet all the way through and out the other side. The naga collapses to the floor.\medskip

Exme continues eating her dirtway.\medskip

Burnie heals Erin on the way to running up and trying to stab the remaining snakunt, but he’s predictably rubbish at this and ends up impaling the floor instead.\medskip

Martin fingers the naga with his deadly finger. The naga visibly withers and weakens at the snake knees.\medskip

Rip once again reliably shoots the naga for solid damage.\medskip

The naga, writhing in pain, brings down a giant column of flames upon the clumped group of attackers around it.\medskip

Myron goes for his trusty weapon, and tries to stab it first, but the fire wards off his attack from the intense heat. He instead double taps it in the belly dealing it massive damage.\medskip

Exme is starting to feel unwell having nearly finished her mulch-kebab.\medskip

Burnie goes to stab the naga with a stone dagger and manages to plunge it deeply into the wounds from Myrons strikes. The naga looks close to death.\medskip

Martin tells the naga it sucks and calls it a dingus, attempting to mock it to death. The naga looks confused, then horrified, and immediately dies of shame.\medskip

The dead nagas melt into a viscous puddle of goo, which drains through the floor.\medskip

Erin calls for aid and Myron force feeds her his beef jerky. It’s incredibly chewing and this occupies Erin for quite a while.\medskip

—-------\medskip

The group search for any sign of further drags and scuffs, but there’s only rotting scraps and bones lying around giving no clue as to a good next direction to head in. Myron attempts to sniff for goo to no avail. Martin just picks a door and opens it with confidence. Martin notices the lofty rafters in the next room and spies a large monkey-like figure perched above. Its eyes being to glow. Burnie notices ants have started to pour into the room from every nook and cranny. The group realises this is one of Valkar’s animalistic hype men from the announcement ceremony.\medskip

“You will not leave here… MALKIOR WILL TAKE YOUR HEADS!” the monkeyman bellows with burning rage.\medskip

Burnie gives everyone an emboldening bond and blesses us all.\medskip

Fleshy wings suddenly sprout out of the sides of Martin’s head and he feels poison coursing through his body. Erin begins to feel her skin burning. The millions of ants have begun their chaos.\medskip

Erin glows with her aura of vitality and feels a little better.\medskip

Martin attempts to counterspell the mad ape man who is trying to freeze Burnie to the spot, but he fails and Burnie is paralyzed.\medskip

Myron gets burned, and immediately runs into the room towards the mean monkey menace. He leaps up to the rafters and manages to grab on. Myron’s gun begins to drip with ethereal white fire, radiating holy power. He stabs his now glorious bayonet into the monkey's torso.\medskip

Riphard just misses with a shot, but quickly reloads and gets a second shot in. The monke begins to dimly glow.\medskip

Malkior swipes out at Burnie and slashes him with its long claws. The monkey then squirts fire at Rip, but Rip ducks behind the corner deftly.\medskip

Burnie stabs the great ape with his spicy sword in an apparently sneaky way.\medskip

Martin begins to fly and swirls his hands. A wind picks up along the floor, sweeping up bones and debris before engulfing the monkey in a whirlwind. Burnie and Erin feel inspired by this show of magical force.\medskip

Malkior, an absolute legend, attempts to warp the flesh of Rip and Erin, but their mighty constitutions fend off the attack.\medskip

Burnie shrugs off the writhing mass of ants, and heals himself minorly. He then tries to get in another couple of spicy stabs, connecting with both.\medskip

Martin’s face suddenly drops and he appears intensely stupid and empty, completely enfeebled of mind. He is completely mentally shattered, seemingly permanently.\medskip

Erin heals Burnie a bit and strikes out at the evil before us, wounding him deeply.\medskip

Myron shoots twice and stabs at the monkey, tearing away significant chunks of flesh as he falls back into the roaring whirlwind via Myrons outstretched bayonet, which flays him with the help of the powerful wind. He disappears in a red mist of viscera into the vortex.\medskip

Martin’s ear wings suddenly detach and flutter into the distance and he falls a short distance to the dungeon floor.\medskip

The group gang together to buff Martin as much as possible while Rip lets rip and begins popping off bullets right next to Martin’s head; bellowing him to snap out of it. From the length of the buffs Martin has 10 attempts to regain his sanity. ONE! TWO! THREE! … nothing. FOUR! FIVE!\medskip

Martin’s dull eyes suddenly light up and spark to life. “For a second there I felt… like a mouse.”\medskip

Riphard zones out for a while.\medskip

Erin awards Myron a field promotion.\medskip

Myron suggests we do some “prayer shit” to call for aid from the Gods. We form a octagon of power, as octagons are the strongest shape as everybody knows, and we all feel a deep connection with the Gods.\medskip

The Gods tell us that they know we face harder challenges ahead and warn us they will intervene only when they deem it necessary, and now is not that moment.\medskip

Erin senses a voice in her head, oddly familiar by this point. This time she is convinced that these are her own thoughts emanating from somewhere deep inside her brain and definitely not an external psychic malice. She feels intense disdain for her now-colleagues and even the Gods themselves.\medskip
