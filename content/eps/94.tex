\subsection{Episode 94: Sometimes fights just happen. Just like that episode of the simpsons.}

\DndDropCapLine{B}uilt in the side of a giant ice sheet lies the uppermost level of what we believe is a temple Kalimar. Our party have just defeated Valkar's lieutenants and now look to moving further in.\medskip

Exme goes over to the symbol of Kalimar on the ground, holding the vial she hopes will cure Kolo "Let's fucking kill Kalimar"\medskip

We head down the tunnel, it winds and fractures before the floor drops away and we slide into darkness. Picking ourselves up after the drop we realise we've been split up. Exme, Myron, Burnie and Erin are together. Martin and Riphard can hear us close by.\medskip

Myron tests the walls by shooting them. they are indeed walls. Patches of blue fungus start to become prevalent as we move through the tunnel. We talk nonsense ploughing ahead not paying attention for 4 hours until we walk straight into a group of ratmen looking puzzled as to how we didnt notice them. The picture of competence.\medskip

We clash in a whirlwind of blades, bullets, bites, big bonks, the ripping of face meat and a cheeky hook to someones anus.\medskip

We carry on chit chatting, contemplating if the now deceased Mrs Ball was an egg.\medskip

Myron and Exme interogate some regular rats. Many rat heads are slapped clean off as the over powered duo quickly go through all the rats they can find. Leaving behind a sad, terrifying pile of rat corpses.\medskip

Martin give Exme a call, they are being followed by something large. Its keeping its distance. They've tried to lose it but it keeps finding them.\medskip

We eventually link up and confront the creature. It can move with unatural speed. It takes hits as it approaches but it sinks it's teeth into Erin, paralysing her with its poison.\medskip

Burnie switches places with her and piles into the creature with a flurry of attacks. The fight continues with the monster flinging poisoned darts from its tail into the party. A new, more confident Mrs Ball smashes the monster. We continue to trade blows, Myron getting the killing blow. The creature flails in its death throes while we watch.\medskip
