\subsection{Episode 97: The Destruction of The Creator}

\DndDropCapLine{T}hey had made their way through the fleshy, tunnelous bowels of Kalimar's lair. Their last sleep showed them visions of the great battle starting between the forces of Gazhikstan and Kalimar. And here they now stood, before the fleshy door where our enemy lay.\medskip

They looked around at each other and made their last minute preparations.\medskip

Burnie raised his welted hands and beckoned us pray to the gods and Synne.There was a deep retelling of the lore of many people who had been connected to Synne, all dead by the hands of the group.\medskip

Exme, clearly flustered got up from the ground where she had been fashioning a low quality tin foil hat that she thrust onto her head muttering "It's not ready but it will have to do". She gave Dev a Ruby ring which was meant for her birthday but times must. Exme and Myron took a moment to discuss Kolo.\medskip

They were in “complete agreement” about the need to bring him back. Myron sought council in Erin to ask about whether or not it would be bad for Kalimar if he stopped having powers. Erin was able to offer her wisdom to the situation.\medskip

Myron cracks his knuckles and a shockwave of prismatic fire passes across him. They’ve put it off for as long as they can. They walk through the fleshy door.\medskip

They enter Kalimar’s domain, a forsaken cave, miles below the light of the sun, a pocket of corruption built into the roots of the world, leeching out its poison. A cathedral of hate. Clots of flesh, with half-formed limbs, twitch and grasp their way out of cracks in the stone walls. Lava bubbles and spurts up through the cracked, broken floor.\medskip

From out of the lava rises a mountain of flesh and chitin. Kalimar ascendant. Mutated rat creatures scurry up from hidden holes to behold their lord. The air is thick with spores, seeking to corrupt anything that breathes.\medskip

Kalimar, dripping lava, stands gloating as he watches them enter.\medskip

A moment’s pause, before Esme flies up and shots the guy. With a gun.\medskip

As she does, tendrils rip out of the wound and grab for her. She dodges, and then shoots one of the ratmen and they explode into confetti, as the power of The Eight sees off the abominations.\medskip

Kalimar roars, and ratmen swarm the group. Myron ignores them and, still sticky, starts running around the lava pool, firing his gun into the hot air, achieving nothing.\medskip

Martin calls forth his magic to blast apart the swarm of ratmen, and everyone generally fails to do much to Kalimar.\medskip

Rip’s sense of smell heightens. This doesn’t have a huge impact on anything.\medskip

Kalimar hauls himself out of the lava, in the opposite direction to where Myron was running, which is annoying. With one enormous claw, he grabs Martin and crushes him, before trying to chomp him down. Esme flies in to save Martin, bending space to move safety to him.\medskip

She tries to fly away, but is grabbed out of the air and chomped. Things aren’t going great.\medskip

The ghost of Reggie, crushed out of Martin’s body by the big claw, reenters Dev’s dogtags, which glows as she calls forth her dead comrades, who obliterate a bunch more ratmen and greebs, who just keep running and being burst into confetti\medskip

Burnie runs up underneath the towering gooch of Kalimar, and attempts to do some hook damage. Kalimar’s form resists almost all of it.\medskip

Erin gets grabbed and chomped, Myron sighs from the opposite side of the room, destroys a few ratmen and starts jumping from pillar to pillar across the lava, feeling left out. A few ratmen try to chase him, but he bats one into the lava.\medskip

Kalimar stomps a foot the size of a building, and gouts of lava spurt out. Myron gets hit on his little pedestal, and the shimmering light protecting him dissipates.\medskip

Burnie gets grabbed, and waved around like a little action figure of an old, gross man.\medskip

Martin makes a turtle-themed quip and reaches out to Kalimar, willing his form to bend to his will. He hits a brick wall. The Elder God of Creation cannot be turned into a turtle.\medskip

Esme, being crushed in Kalimar’s claw, is held up to the face of the god. Each jagged tooth in his cavernous mouth is longer than she is tall. She stands defiant, and once again demands Kalimar return her brother. When she is refused, she presses a button on a device from her pocket and, in another place, a cavern full of Kalimar’s pupal spawn is collapsed.\medskip

As Kalimar roars in anger, Esme ejects a barrage of flares from one of her many mechanisms, and flies free of Kalimar’s hold.\medskip

The skin of Kalimar, now visibly pockmarked with wounds, flexes and pulses, and tendrils of flesh burst forth, snaking and writhing around everyone in the room.\medskip

Martin turns Burnie, who is currently grabbed in Kalimar’s claw, into an enormous crab. Kalimar eats the crab in one gulp.\medskip

The tendrils writhing out of Kalimar’s wounds tighten around everyone, Martin lets out a squeak of twisting metal, and inside a nearby god, a crab turns into a doomed old man.\medskip

Esme, standing shoulder to shoulder with Rip, falls\medskip

Rip surrounding by very aggressive minions, pulls out both his whips and starts to cut slices across the lion, goat and rat people. The press continues in on him, and he screams out for his children, and their children to be “ No others shall inherit the bank, the dynasty will last forever!” the numbers are too great and he is overcome and then mercilessly stabbed by the malicious malcontents. Glory to Calimar they bray as the golden gown of our hero is streaked and stained with his own viscera.\medskip

Martin falls still hovering 10ft up on a gust of fresh air, Martin is also assailed by goat and lion men, they jump and hack away at his already heavily mangled form. It’s more than the butler can handle, each cut cleaves away more of his fragile mechanical body, until there is nothing but a pile of trash on the floor.\medskip

Myron, bloodied and bent, carries on up the arm. From the wounds covering Kalimar’s body emerge two more monstrosities, who block his path. From behind, the lion man from the elbow region walks up behind Myron, who looks down with surprise as the tip of a blade emerges from his chest. He falls, and the two ratmen plunge their blades in and kick his corpse off Kalimar’s shoulder. Arbiter Smord Myron’s corpse falls from Kalimar’s shoulder and is scattered across the ground.\medskip

Erin falls\medskip

Inside Kalimar, Burnie is narrowly avoiding being digested. It is only a matter of time.\medskip

Kalimar stands triumphant. Of the creatures that walked into his sanctum to challenge his divine might, the only one that remained conscious was being digested in his stomach, and would surely become part of his being before long. He was brought close to death, but now it was certain, he would be reunited with Canillon, and the world would be brought back under their control.\medskip

But not all that opposed him walked into the sanctum. He had not just earned the ire of mere creatures.\medskip

The room was around the perfect sphere of infinitely dense metallic material, topped with a pretty pink bow, that many knew as Mrs. Ball. Mrs. Ball was not accustomed to feeling any particular way about any of the things she observed, but Kalimar has shown himself to be different. Kalimar must die.\medskip

Moving Kalimar towards her was not going to be enough. Mrs. Balls prepared for her biggest ever bonk. Mrs. Ball moved. She and Kalimar came together and the God of Creation came apart.\medskip

A volcano of viscera erupted from Kalimar’s back, as Mrs. Ball punched a hole through his entire midsection. He stumbled back, collapsing into the lava pool, as Burnie schlorped out of the whole in his torso in a cascade of bile.\medskip

Now sustained by nothing other than the divine power of Synne, Burnie gets to his feet. The Vessel of the God of Fate is moved by forces older and more powerful than the actions of muscles and bones, and he leaves a trail of deep black light as he moves from one of his fallen comrades to the next.\medskip

Slowly, groggily, Erin, Riphard, Esme and Martin are all dragged back from the brink of death. The battle is won.\medskip

Erin rubs the ruby ring Esme gave her. Running towards Myron she casts revivify, a burst of divine energy erupts around her, the close proximity bathed in and singed by the holy light. Myron remains lifeless, neck broken, body broken he is beyond even the powers of the divine.\medskip
