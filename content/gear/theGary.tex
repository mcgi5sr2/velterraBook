\subsubsection{Background}

The Good Airship Gary is the primary base of operations for the team.  The group discovered the airship within the Tower of Ruh'Breks, an ancient wizard's hidden stronghold buried deep underground.  Ruh'Breks himself, a dying sorcerer who had sealed himself away centuries ago, gifted the vessel to the party along with the legendary Puzzle Die.  The ship is powered by spectral energy batteries, sentient ghost-like entities captured and contained within S.T.A.N.R.I's storage compartments.

Named after the group's beloved stone companion Gary, who sacrificed himself protecting the party in the jungles of South Africa, the airship became the symbol of the newly formed Gary Guild and the team's most prized possession.

The team have made a number of modifications to the ship over their time together, outfitting it with cannons, bomb bays, a smoke screen system, a reinforced hull, and an on-board workshop for Exme.  Battle upgrades were completed during the Masuda campaign, and further modifications were made in Linderdorf.

The most significant upgrade came when Exme plugged the Puzzle Die of Ruh'Breks directly into the ship's ghost engine, achieving hyper-speed travel that pinned the crew against their seats and allowed the party to cross Velterra in days rather than weeks, fast enough to recruit allies from Masuda, New Abbergast, and Logarsk before the Church could react.  The ship's cargo hold was modified to carry the thirty-foot Stonecast Mech, which was dropped from the airship as a siege weapon during the assault on Seven's Spire.

\subsubsection{Functions}

The ship serves as mobile headquarters, offering sleeping quarters, Exme's engineering workshop, storage for the party's arsenal, and a command deck.  Its arsenal includes multiple cannons and a heavy forward-mounted weapon.  Captain Stick, a sentient wooden figurehead, has been entrusted with command of the vessel since its discovery.  S.T.A.N.R.I's on-board systems provide communications, navigation, and analytical support from the air.

\subsubsection{Destruction}

The Airship Gary was destroyed in Episode 103 during the Nautiloid attack on Jecede.  When the githyanki vessel was crippled and began falling toward the city, Captain Stick took the helm and steered the burning airship directly into the Nautiloid, deflecting it from the populated harbour.  Both vessels were destroyed on impact.  Captain Stick went down with the ship.

The Gary had served the party from the jungles of South Africa to the skies above Jecede, across two campaigns and more miles than anyone cared to count.  Its loss, alongside Burnie and Erin in the same battle, marked the end of the party as it had been.
