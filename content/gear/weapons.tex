``Collaborative Experimental Destruction and Rapid Immolation Contraption.'' \subsection*{Godbringer}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=80mm]{./content/img/godbringer.png}
\begin{figure}[h]
\end{figure}
\end{center}

\noindent

A weapon of immense divine power, Godbringer earned its name through its role in the party's quest to break the seal and return the gods to the world.  Forged from Excalibrum, the legendary star-metal capable of harming those blessed by the Inthun, by Google Von Maccherstein's team using knowledge recovered from the Bright Bart Forge, Godbringer represents the culmination of the party's long search for the materials and craftsmen needed to create a weapon that could stand against the Church's most powerful agents.

Wielded by Myron, Godbringer's defining moment came in the Clawtech Arena in Linderdorf, where Myron launched himself into the air and brought the weapon down in a final strike that bisected the thirty-foot Stonecast Mech entirely, a blow that silenced the stadium before three-quarters of the crowd erupted in chants of ``Emancipation.''

\subsection*{Hammer of the Gods}

%\begin{center}
%\includegraphics[width=80mm]{./content/img/xxx.png}
%\begin{figure}[h]
%\end{figure}
%\end{center}

\noindent

Used in ancient Crusades by the Zealot Arbiter Elron, when he travelled east to Masuda, said to defeat an Inthun infused Varg in a single blow (so may be made of starmetal).

\begin{DndComment}{Extract from ''The Lands of the Godless"}
Excursions to the East have met with mixed success over the years. There has always been a strong desire amongst the faithful to bring the light of the gods to the heathen lands. The question of how best to approach this has led to many missteps and failings. I write this treatise in the hopes that future generations will learn from the mistakes of the past in future endeavours.

Firstly, knowledge of the culture of the lands is essential to the success of any conversion. The first voyagers failed when they approached the peoples of Masuda in the same way they approached those of Nanduan. While they may look similar, the cultures vary in insurmountable ways. The people of Nanduan have been resistant to all forms of preaching, while a foothold has eventually been made in Masuda albeit in small ways.

More success has been found with peaceful approaches to the eastern lands. Much can be learned from the failed crusade of the zealot arbiter Elron. He bore the Hammer of the Gods, a weapon so powerful it struck down even the Inthun-infused terror of Hunthar Varg and ended the third Varghold incursion in a single fiery blow. With such power behind him and an army of arbiters, Elron sailed East to deliver ‘holy justice’ to the unbelievers.

The Masudan response to such an invasion was, by all accounts, as brutal as it was swift. Few survivors returned, and those who did told tale of a dawn assault by incredibly skilled swordsmen with lightning fast blades. As they tried to react it soon became clear that Elron and all the commanding officers had been slain during the night, their throats slit by assassins that slipped past even the most perceptive guards.

The Hammer of the Gods itself was lost during that fateful voyage, a lesson in humility to all our followers. Let it be known that when we approach with brutality the Gods will show us no clemency. We must spread our message with the light and fairness in which it was intended.
\end{DndComment}

\subsection*{The Stone Knife}

Named not because it's made of stone, but because of its utility. Resembling more a chisel than a knife, the handle is flared slightly at the base to support a hammer, and the tip is a flat edge making it easy to carve off slabs of stone. The guard is asymmetrical, one side ending in a flat edge and the other a sharp cylindrical point, to allow tapping for precise stonework. The blade itself has that classic patterned look that shows it contains Excalibrum or its alloy, and ancient dwarven runes are faintly etched along the blade's length. In spite of these it is exquisitely beautiful and very, very sharp.

In addition to being a powerful weapon, the Stone Knife can be used to magically shape stone once per day. Recovered from beneath the Tom'ardy Mountains, it became Burnie's signature tool, he used it to seal the Kalimar summoning pit beneath Hope's Rest and, later, doorways inside the Seven's Spire itself.

\subsection*{Kolo's Mechanical Bow}

\noindent

Exmerah's posthumous gift to her twin brother.  A cabled bow with a power pack, found waiting for Kolo when he was restored in his construct body after Exme's erasure.  She had built it for him before the descent into Kalimar's lair, knowing that one way or another, he would need a weapon when he woke.

The bow debuted during the Inquisitor fight on the Jecede railway roof and served Kolo through the Black Rock heist and beyond.  Like everything Exme built, it worked beautifully.
