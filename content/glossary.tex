\section{Glossary of Terms}

\noindent
A guide to the terminology, slang, and proper nouns of Velterra for readers navigating the episodes.

\vspace{3mm}

\subsection*{A}

\noindent
\textbf{Adam-ants.} A rival Varg tribe encountered in the northern wastes. Defused via barbecue and vodka; later wiped out when they ambushed the party.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Arbiters.} The Church's warrior-enforcers. Plate-armoured, gun-carrying soldiers trained in martial combat and divine justice. Myron served as one for decades before turning against the institution.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Armoury of Sin.} The divine war gear worn by the god Sinn when he last fought Canalon: a helm, sword, armour, shield, a pendant that grants rest, and two nets. Hidden beneath the Black Rock bank vault.

\subsection*{B}

\noindent
\textbf{Balloon Cow.} Inflatable bovines found in Nanduan. Exactly what they sound like.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Bertrands Stand.} The last defensive wall in Hope's Rest. The city's final line against attack.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Black Rock.} The party's bank in Hope's Rest, operated by Riphard. In the continuation, the vault beneath it held the Armoury of Sin.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Black Sabbath.} A two-handed pronged shadow-blade forged from shadow sorcery and the Stone of Unthala. Pilcheur's primary weapon, later reforged into the katana Iron Maiden.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Book Burners.} The Church's now-defunct literary enforcement arm, tasked with hunting and destroying heretical texts. Pilcheur and Burnie both served. A hidden purge ordered by the Inquisition destroyed the order from within.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Bright Bart Forge.} An ancient dwarven forge high in the mountains above Linderdorf. The party fought the Aspect of the Storm to claim it and used it to forge Excalibrum weapons.

\subsection*{C}

\noindent
\textbf{C.E.D.R.I.C.} Collaborative Experimental Destruction and Rapid Immolation Contraption. Exme's masterwork: a sentient electric railgun and linear accelerator with ``a funny voice that makes me go all trembly.''

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Canalon.} Elder-god space dragon and mate of Kalimar. Descends from the void in the continuation to mind-control populations across Velterra. Connected to the ``Canilon'' tattoos found on dead Inquisitors.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{CC.} Church Calendar. The campaign takes place in 6653 CC.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Chesney Hawks.} A Varg tribe led by the giant chieftain Magrat. The party infiltrated them to reach Valkar Varg in the continuation.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Church of the Seven.} The dominant religious and political institution of Velterra. Built around worship of the Seven Gods, though it was the Church itself that sealed the gods away from the world during the Sundering.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Craftwerk.} A construct or automaton. Term used in Linderdorf, the mountain city of foundries and automatons. Martin Andleberger is one.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Cult of Synne.} The Church's label for the party, used to frame them as criminals and heretics. In truth, several party members did bear the Mark of Synne.

\subsection*{D}

\noindent
\textbf{Delvers.} Dwarven miners sent on one-way expeditions into the deep mines of the Tom'ardy Mountains. A slave class in all but name, sustained by the mountain dynasties' appetite for rare minerals.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Die of Ruh'Brex.} See \textit{Puzzle Die}.

\subsection*{E}

\noindent
\textbf{Emancipation.} The rallying cry for goblin freedom from human and Church oppression. A central theme of the campaign, shouted by three-quarters of the Linderdorf arena after Myron bisected the Stonecast Mech.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Empire of the Eight.} Hell under the rule of the devil Lazarus in the continuation. Lazarus rebranded the domain after the party returned the gods to the world.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Excalibrum.} A rare divine metal found in meteorites, also called Starmetal. The only material that can harm those blessed by the Inthun, making it the key to defeating the Church's Inquisitors. Requires a master smith to forge.

\subsection*{F}

\noindent
\textbf{Festival of Light.} A Church celebration and pilgrimage to Seven's Spire. The party used the festival's masked crowds as cover for their infiltration of the Spire.

\subsection*{G}

\noindent
\textbf{Gabrin Kokaine.} A substance Gary briefly became addicted to during the party's time in Hope's Rest.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Gabrins.} The in-world term for goblins, used by both the players and the peoples of Velterra. The twin protagonists Kolo and Exmerah are Gabrins.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Gary Guild.} ``Garys Against Religion, Yo.'' The party's guild in Hope's Rest, founded after Gary's rousing speech to the guild council. Later transformed into a pseudo-religious peace movement under Garbigail's leadership.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Godbringer.} Myron's Excalibrum-forged weapon. Earned its name through the party's quest to break the seal and return the gods to the world. Bisected a thirty-foot Stonecast Mech in its defining moment.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Great Expanse.} A vast desert separating Hope's Rest from Port Averdale. Home to the Spancers and various hostile creatures.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Great Stone.} The artefact used to create the Sundering, located at the pinnacle of Seven's Spire. Originally called the ``Stone of the Eight,'' acknowledging Synne as a god before the Church struck her from the record.

\subsection*{H}

\noindent
\textbf{Hearthrust Society.} A noble alliance descended from Lady Otoria's family, based in Masuda. Allied with the party during the civil war campaign.

\subsection*{I}

\noindent
\textbf{Inquisition, The.} The Church's elite secret enforcement arm and its true leaders. Inquisitors are immune to non-Excalibrum weapons, have tentacle-filled maws behind their masks, and bear Canilon tattoos. The ``fourth faction'' of the Church, hidden from the public.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Inthun, The.} An enchanted stone slab of immense and terrible power. Kills most who are strapped to it, but imbues survivors with awesome strength, innate magical abilities, and nigh-on invulnerability. Works better with children, a fact the Church exploited.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{IRIS.} An artificial intelligence built into Burnie's eye. Jive-talking personality. Shoots lasers and provides targeting data.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Iron Maiden.} A one-handed katana reforged from Black Sabbath. The blade that killed Lady Otoria, later claimed by the party.

\subsection*{J}

\noindent
\textbf{Jennies.} Merfolk, or fish-people. Some live peacefully alongside humans; others have territorial disputes with land-dwellers. King Oceani committed his Jenny army to the Battle of Hope's Rest.

\subsection*{K}

\noindent
\textbf{Kalimar.} An elder entity and god of creation. Corrupted Kolo through psychic whispers in the Tom'ardy mines, warped his flesh, and trapped his soul. Killed in the continuation by Mrs Ball, who punched clean through him.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Kalimel.} Kalimar's flesh-warping goo, used by Valkar Varg to breed Pact warrior super-soldiers. Ninety-nine in every hundred subjects die during the Trials that create them.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Killepitsch.} A liquor used during Pilcheur's exorcism in Riverfall, alongside pastoral chanting and a great deal of vomiting.

\subsection*{M}

\noindent
\textbf{Mark of Synne.} A protective brand connected to the eighth god. Burns into the skin and grants ``fate-spinning'' powers. Pilcheur, Burnie, and later others bore the mark.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Mrs Ball.} A sentient sphere of impossibly dense metal. Each Ball contains a universe, and her people restore broken ones. Delivered the killing blow to Kalimar. May hold the reality into which Exmerah was erased.

\subsection*{N}

\noindent
\textbf{Nautiloid.} A githyanki spelljammer vessel, crewed by mind flayers and gith warriors. One attacked Jecede in the continuation, killing Erin and Burnie before the Airship Gary rammed it from the sky.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Northern Rock.} Riphard's first bank, established in Derek Bobacious's house in Riverfall. The precursor to the Black Rock operation in Hope's Rest.

\subsection*{P}

\noindent
\textbf{Pact Warriors.} Varg super-soldiers created through Kalimar's goo. Enhanced with immense strength and hellish power, but the process kills ninety-nine in every hundred subjects.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Petite Ferret, The.} A pub in Hope's Rest, run by Horatio BrambleThrust.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Piece of Shit.} Myron's cocktail: one part sherry, one part grenadine, one part Bovril. Named after Riphard.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Puzzle Die.} A legendary magical cube created by the ancient sorcerer Ruh'Breks and recovered from his hidden tower. Contains dozens of functions accessible by manipulating its faces, including teleportation, invisibility, time stop, and a Wish with a ten-thousand-year cooldown. Also called the Die of Ruh'Brex.

\subsection*{R}

\noindent
\textbf{Ronin.} An honoured status in Masudan culture. The party achieved it after the Gabrin Coming of Age ritual, a cocoon metamorphosis that left them physically transformed.

\subsection*{S}

\noindent
\textbf{S.T.A.N.R.I.} Exme's mecha-bear companion. Evolved from a leaking robo-cub (Mk 0) to a full combat unit (Mk I) to a tiger-shaped battlesuit. Later gifted consciousness by the gods and became headmaster of a school for gifted girls.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Salumist.} Someone from Linderdorf. In their culture, sausages are ``a type of face.''

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Sand Dagger.} Dangerous sneaky creatures found in Nanduan.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Seven's Spire.} The Church's holiest site and seat of power, a towering structure containing the Great Stone that sustains the Sundering. The party assaulted it in the campaign finale to break the seal and return the gods.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Spancers.} An anti-Church group operating in the Great Expanse. Desert bandits who became allies of the party.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{SRA.} Small Races Alliance. A gnome and halfling terrorist organisation, initially hostile to the party. After the party killed their leader Razzle Backshine and installed Buttons as his replacement, the SRA became reluctant allies.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Starmetal.} See \textit{Excalibrum}.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Stonecast Mech.} A thirty-foot stone fighting robot with flamethrowers and laser cannons. Won by the party in the Clawtech Arena in Linderdorf. Dropped from the airship as a siege weapon during the assault on Seven's Spire.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Stone of Unthala.} A large stone, about the size of two fists, retrieved from the skull-shaped ice Temple of Unthala during the party's first mission. Served as their primary means of communication with Lazarus across dimensional boundaries.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Sundering, The.} The event in which the Church used the Great Stone to sever the world from the gods. Intended as protection against divine conflict, it became a prison that allowed the Church to consolidate unchecked power.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Synne.} The eighth god, struck from the Church's records. God of fate, luck, and balance. Not evil, as the Church claimed, but a neutral force. Several party members bore her mark. See also \textit{Mark of Synne}.

\subsection*{T}

\noindent
\textbf{The Gary.} The party's airship, discovered in the Tower of Ruh'Breks and named after Gary following his sacrifice. Powered by spectral energy batteries. Destroyed in Episode 103 when Captain Stick steered it into the falling Nautiloid to save Jecede.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{TikkiTukks.} Small bear-like creatures with masks and sharp teeth. Some cannibalistic. The party fought them at the Temple of Unthala and later in the jungles of South Africa, where they hacked Gary down piece by piece.

\subsection*{U}

\noindent
\textbf{University of Assassins.} Estebaun's institution in Hope's Rest. Trained Kevin, the party's young assassin recruit.

\subsection*{V}

\noindent
\textbf{Valeria.} The main continent of Velterra, where most of the campaign takes place.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Varg.} The northern warlord and barbarian alliance. Enslaved goblins for generations. In the continuation, Valkar Varg united the tribes and made a pact with Kalimar to breed super-soldiers.

\smallskip \noindent
\textbf{Velterra.} The world in which the campaign takes place.

\subsection*{W}

\noindent
\textbf{Wish, The.} The Puzzle Die's most devastating function: the only wish in existence, with a ten-thousand-year cooldown. Used by Exmerah in Kalimar's lava sanctum to save both Myron and Kolo. The cost was absolute: she was erased from all reality.
