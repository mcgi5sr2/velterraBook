\section{The Velterran Archives}

Alongside their session write-ups, the players maintained a campaign wiki, a working archive of quests, item research, monster sightings and recovered in-world documents. The records below are preserved from that archive as they were written at the time: a snapshot of what the party knew (and what they thought they knew) midway through their war against the Church.

\subsection*{Recovered Documents}

The Book Burners' cache and the party's own research turned up a number of texts whose meaning only became clear much later, and some whose meaning never did. Two extracts are reproduced here; others appear alongside the items they describe in the Gear chapter, and the apocryphal \textit{Church Factions Over the Years} appears among the episode interludes.

\begin{DndComment}{Extract from ``Chronicles of Rhu'Brex''}
In the centuries since Rhu'Brex walked the earth many of his works have been used or destroyed, his reputation for creating items that pushed the boundaries of magical knowledge created great desire and great fear.

One creation of his which has enraptured the wizarding world has remained elusive, despite the efforts of treasure hunters and practitioners of the arcane to uncover it. The Puzzle Die was a result of the great arcanists experiments into multi-functional magic. Most artefacts are known for a single, powerful effect and are purpose built for this. Rhu'Brex was obsessed with the concept of magic being adaptable, his concept being that it would be superior to have a single, portable device with countless possibilities instead of needing to fill a lair.

The Puzzle Die is known to have many properties that can be accessed, though knowledge of how to access its powers are mostly as lost as the Die itself. It is known to be able to grant its bearer the ability to move vast distances, destroy your enemies and transmute items -- though Rhu'Brex created the Die to only respond to need, a precaution believed to prevent his apprentices from toying with such a dangerous tool.

One of the basic functions of the Puzzle Die is the ability to detect the presence and location of other magical items over great distance -- a utility of such value that Rhu'Brex unlocked this function for use by any bearer.

The location of the Puzzle Die has remained a mystery for many years since the mysterious vanishing of Rhu'Brex himself. My own theorem is that Rhu'Brex held it within one of his secret lairs within the jungles of X far to the west -- the increase in dangerous creatures and the lethality of the area certainly suggest some form of magical influence.
\end{DndComment}
