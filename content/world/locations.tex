\subsection*{Temple of Unthala}

A skull-shaped ice temple in the frozen wilderness south of Vathos Boundary, accessible via a perilous bridge.  The temple was home to a colony of TikkiTukks and contained the Stone of Unthala --- an ancient relic that served as the party's primary means of communication with Lazarus.  The party fought their way through rooms of masked bear-creatures, a giant spider, and dart traps to claim the stone from the chief's quarters.

\subsection*{Riverfall} 

\vspace{5mm}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=80mm]{./content/img/riverfall.png}
\begin{figure}[h]
\end{figure}
\end{center}

\noindent 

Riverfall is a fair-to-middling sized town, blessed with a beautiful set of rivers and waterfalls that give it its name.  The town serves as an important stop on the road north and is home to a significant Church presence, centred around the Sanctuary of Novetta.

The town had four pubs --- the Sad Crusader, Club and Cask, Open Flask, and Minstrels --- one of which unfortunately and mysteriously burnt down after Pilch's possession caused him to vomit magical darkness and Kolo responded by setting fire to the building.

Riverfall is also home to a very expensive blacksmith, the Tower of Imota Kan (which houses a vault of secret artefacts and a lovely collection of non-religious but Church-safe books), and the house of Derek Bobacious --- site of Riphard's first bank, Northern Rock.

A new addition to the town is a nicely sized ditch on the outskirts.

\begin{DndSidebar}{The Tower of Imota Kan}
The Tower of Imota Kan --- Nakatomi spelt backwards --- was the site of a daring heist that played out like a scene from a famous pre-Velterran action scroll.  The robbery was led by ``Hans'' and his band of mercenaries, and was thwarted by the party in a running battle across multiple floors.  It was here that Otoria met her end at the blade of the singing swordswoman Lark.
\end{DndSidebar}

\section{Nanduan} 

Nanduan is a large kingdom on the North-Eastern coast of the continent of Erassa. The capital city is located on the banks of a tidal river estuary, and maritime trade makes up a significant portion of the economy.

Nanduan does not trade significantly with Velarian cities, but has some relations with the elven city of A'kul'theok. There is limited migration from Nanduan to Church-controlled cities, and so not much is known about this land by commoners in these areas. Traders and travellers may have some patchy information. It is known that relations between Nanduan and Masuda, to the south, are strained, at best.
Known Inhabitants

    The Hearthrust Dynasty - A large, wealthy and prominent noble family. They recognise the danger of the isolation their social class gives them, and therefore the children of each generation are sent out into the world for a few years at the age of 18 to experience what life is like for the people below them. Some run small businesses, some join religious orders, but most use the opportunity to become adventurers or travelers. As most marriages within the family are arranged during the teenage years, some of the Hearthrust children are already betrothed by the time they begin their travels. In this case, the face of the Hearthrust daughters are covered, to save the world the pain of seeing, but not being able to attain, such beauty. Hearthrust girls are renowned in Naduan as being exceptionally beautiful.

    They are also seen as beautiful in their own country of Nanduan.

    An Unnamed Shipping Magnate - Known to be betrothed to Lady Otoria Hearthrust.

Flora and Fauna

    Sand Dagger - Dangerous and sneaky creatures known to hide underneath and behind things.

    Balloon Cow - Like cows, but inflatable.
