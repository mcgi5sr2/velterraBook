\begin{center}

\begin{figure}[h]

\end{center}

One of the dimensions of the afterlife.  Hell is managed by the devil Lazarus from a plush office accessible through portal doors created by his elephantine butler, Meredith.  The office furnishings rotate between kitsch decor and a large-scale war room map of Velterra depending on Lazarus's mood.  Demons handle the physical torments of the damned while devils manage the administrative burden of processing and categorising souls.

The party visited Hell regularly through their dreams, using the Stone of Unthala to communicate with Lazarus.  Kevin, upon looking out of Lazarus's office window, saw the infinite abyss of the damned --- and, being wise beyond his years, retained his sanity by waving hello to the various murderers, thieves, and scallywags he recognised having assassinated recently.

It's hot.

\subsection*{Hope's Rest}

\vspace{5mm}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=80mm]{./content/img/hopesRest.png}
\begin{figure}[h]
\end{figure}
\end{center}

\noindent

The largest city in Velterra and the commercial heart of the Church-controlled territories.  Hope's Rest is a multi-cultural trading mecca, bisected by the River Eldon, and governed by a complex guild system rooted in five founding families.

Each family selects a board that appoints a GEO (Guild Executive Officer) to rule their district.  The current GEO, Parchet Green --- a half-elf male, quite stern and ruthless --- holds power largely because all who stood against him in elections have vanished.

The five guild families and their domains:

\textbf{Life} --- Taverns, gambling houses; headed by Zelfie Higgins, a halfling woman. \textbf{Might} --- Archers, mercenaries, the Hunt (bounty hunters), and the Masters, the guild's elite unit; headed by the elf Tolon Eversong. \textbf{Coin} --- Traders, stores, market, and mint; the Mint is run by Wellington Hunter-Smyth and the Traders by Montigue Hartwell. \textbf{Service} --- Cleaners, valets, plumbers, Jennies (semi-official), Thieves Guild, Assassins Guild; headed by Meke Longpaw, a Tabaxi. \textbf{Making} --- Carpenters, builders, blacksmiths, clothiers; headed by the dwarf Dalvona Amethyst.

The city's politics district clusters around the Guild Hall itself.

Notable landmarks include Bertrand's Stand (the last defensive wall), the Petite Ferret (pub, run by Horatio BrambleThrust), the old Book Burner headquarters (now the Gary Guild base), and Riphard's Guild of Banking.  The city's sewer system, vast and unmapped, became the site of Kolo's lair during his corruption and the scene of the group's most devastating loss.

The party spent more time in Hope's Rest than anywhere else, founding guilds, establishing businesses, fighting the SRA, and earning enemies and allies in equal measure.  During the final campaign, the Church sent 8,000 troops to Hope's Rest, and the Battle of Hope's Rest saw the alliance army --- Garys, Jennies, assassins, dwarves, fishmen, and commoners --- fight to hold the city while the party assaulted Seven's Spire

The former headquarters of the Book Burners, now repurposed as the base of operations for the Gary Guild.  The building features firepoles between floors (a source of endless entertainment for Exme), a vault in the basement, a third-floor workshop converted for Exme's engineering work, and a library still tended by the elderly Archibald. \smallskip

\subsection*{Vathos Boundary}

A small settlement on the edge of the civilised world --- the last stop before the southern wilderness.  Vathos Boundary is where the party first set foot after their resurrection, and from here they ventured south to retrieve the Stone of Unthala from the ice temple.

The town's modest amenities include the Copper Flagon pub, the Bronze Hammer tavern (run by Ledrun Harkness), and Marcy's --- the establishment politely recorded in the party's notes as the ``Titty bar,'' run by the busty barkeep Marcy Davis.  Frederick the blacksmith, Pastor Gunther Dunkhill's Church, a city guard house (under Captain Urnok, a half-orc never seen without Gumshoe the troll), a jewellers, and a stable round out the town, all under the watch of Mayor Bertrum Salvador.  The surrounding countryside is home to the Dutty Hoes Camp, an RWA cave hideout, and the Forest of Big Pig


\smallskip

\subsection*{Tom'ardy Mountains}

Home to an exclusive and reclusive enclave of high-status dwarven families, each dynasty residing in their own private mountain.  It can take days just to say hello to your neighbours --- not that anyone does.

Beneath the surface, the mountains hide a darker truth: a classist, slave-dependent economy built on the backs of ``Delvers'' --- miners sent on one-way trips into the deep.  The ancient Hardstone (or Hardthrust) family vault lies in the deepest levels, alongside the Excalibrum deposits that the party spent weeks searching for.  The mines are a treacherous labyrinth of traps, animated statues, Gorgons, and worse --- it was here that Kolo began his descent into corruption, and that both he and Myron were turned to stone.

After the party's intervention, Tom'ardy was transformed into something approaching a socialist utopia, with the Delver class system abolished and the mountain dwarves joining the alliance army for the final battle.

\smallskip

\subsection*{Temple of Unthala}

A skull-shaped ice temple in the frozen wilderness south of Vathos Boundary, accessible via a perilous bridge.  The temple was home to a colony of TikkiTukks and contained the Stone of Unthala --- an ancient relic that served as the party's primary means of communication with Lazarus.  The party fought their way through rooms of masked bear-creatures, a giant spider, and dart traps to claim the stone from the chief's quarters.

\subsection*{Riverfall} 

\vspace{5mm}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=80mm]{./content/img/riverfall.png}
\begin{figure}[h]
\end{figure}
\end{center}

\noindent 

Riverfall is a fair-to-middling sized town, blessed with a beautiful set of rivers and waterfalls that give it its name.  The town serves as an important stop on the road north and is home to a significant Church presence, centred around the Sanctuary of Novetta and its head priest, Priest Granger.  The keeper of the Tower of Imota Kan, Hollie Granger, shares his name --- as the party's notes put it, ``possibly someone important's daughter.  Nothing like a bit of nepotism to keep the town running well.''

The town had four pubs --- the Sad Crusader, Club and Cask, Open Flask, and Minstrels --- one of which unfortunately and mysteriously burnt down after Pilch's possession caused him to vomit magical darkness and Kolo responded by setting fire to the building.

Riverfall is also home to a very expensive blacksmith, the Tower of Imota Kan (which houses a vault of secret artefacts and a lovely collection of non-religious but Church-safe books), and the house of Derek Bobacious --- site of Riphard's first bank, Northern Rock.

A new addition to the town is a nicely sized ditch on the outskirts.

\begin{DndSidebar}{The Tower of Imota Kan}
The Tower of Imota Kan --- Nakatomi spelt backwards --- was the site of a daring heist that played out like a scene from a famous pre-Velterran action scroll.  The robbery was led by ``Hans'' and his band of mercenaries, and was thwarted by the party in a running battle across multiple floors.  It was here that Otoria met her end at the blade of the singing swordswoman Lark.
\end{DndSidebar}

\subsection*{The Seven's Spire}

\vspace{5mm}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=80mm]{./content/img/7spire.jpg}
\begin{figure}[h]
\end{figure}
\end{center}

\noindent

A moderate-sized city dominated by a massive spire dedicated to the Church of the Seven --- and the location of the Great Stone that maintains the seal keeping the gods from the world.  The district surrounding the spire is given over entirely to religion: tat shops hawking religiosity, heavily plated guards sporting god symbology, and moneyed citizens decked out in serious god swag.  Commoners wear potato sacks, as befitting their station.  Making money is considered vulgar and is kept to the outskirts.

The tower itself is a fortress.  The lower floors house stores, servants, and guard barracks.  Above that: officer quarters, training arenas, and archery ranges.  Higher still: clergy levels filled with books and sherry-drinking clerics.  A prison level holds cells stretching in curved arcs for as far as the eye can see, most containing either rotting remains or desiccated bodies --- and, in one steel-doored room, the torture rack where Arbiter Kiros was found barely alive.  The pinnacle, above all of this, houses the Great Stone and the seal itself.

A massive sewer network runs into the tower's foundations.  The annual Festival of Light draws thousands of pilgrims, providing the perfect cover for infiltration --- especially if one is disguised as a lemon.

The party's infiltration of Seven's Spire was the climax of the entire campaign.  Lillith posed as a pastor to gain entry weeks in advance, the Stonecast Mech and ninja eagles provided a distraction outside, and the party fought their way floor by floor toward the pinnacle to break the seal and return the gods to the world.

\subsection*{Logarsk}

\vspace{5mm}

\begin{center}
\includegraphics[width=80mm]{./content/img/largosk.png}
\begin{figure}[h]
\end{figure}
\end{center}

\noindent

A sleepy town nestled over the mountains from Seven's Spire, where the Church puts children through a trial at the age of five.  The party arrived here on the run and discovered something far worse than a harsh coming-of-age ceremony: the local Marshall was abusing children in the church basement using the Slab of Inthun, an ancient artefact that either kills its subject or imbues them with immense power.  It works better on children, a fact that made the Marshall's crimes all the more horrifying.

The party's intervention brought them into direct confrontation with the Inquisition for the first time --- an ambush, by the party's account, in which the Inquisition wanted to take the surviving child as their own.  The encounter went badly.  It also introduced Arbiter Kiros, who tried to blame the group for the ensuing mess but gradually came around to their cause.

The party returned to Logarsk much later in the campaign to collect the Stonecast Mech for the final assault.

\subsection*{The Great Expanse}

A vast, burning desert where travellers lose their way and their lives with equal regularity.  The Expanse is plagued by ``bandits'' called Spancers --- an anti-Church group who prey upon travellers but proved to be valuable allies when the party crossed their territory.  The Spancers and their Tengu allies became part of the broader resistance against the Church.

\smallskip

\subsection*{Port Averdale}

One of the major ports on the main continent, full of colourful people and shady goings-on.  What it really needed was a show from a circus troupe, but the party failed them.  They do have a lovely square made up of two triangles that hosts all kinds of cultural events.  It also had a lovely customs expo, which was mysteriously burned down.  Otoria was killed in a harbour tavern here --- choosing honour over surrender when outnumbered by scores of thugs --- before being resurrected by Lazarus and joining the party.

\smallskip

\subsection*{Linderdorf}

A mountain city built into the rock itself --- a dense warren of foundries, mills, and shops where automatons walk the streets.  Lithe mechanical models staff the shops, hulking constructs serve as guards, and the city ships weapons and steel across Velterra.  Linderdorf is the bastion of civilisation against the Varg, and its university --- where Professor Jorge Myron teaches --- is one of the finest centres of learning in the world.

The party arrived here seeking master blacksmiths to forge Excalibrum weapons.  Their drunken search for Google Von Maccherstein through the city's labyrinthine streets became legendary.  Myron invented the ``Piece of Shit'' cocktail here, Riphard bought a schnitzel business, and Martin was found to have been a construct originally built in the city.  The Clawtech Arena hosted the party's battle against the thirty-foot Stonecast Mech, and the city served as the staging ground for the alliance army's final assault.

\smallskip

\subsection*{New Abbergast}

An island settlement ruled by Lady Gharbighast, built into a fortress-town on a beautiful coast still under construction.  The party arrived to find the settlers under attack from merfolk who resented the overfishing of their waters.  Through Toni's diplomatic efforts, a trade agreement was brokered with King Oceani of the merfolk, and the underwater terror Acroneth the Immortal --- an Aboleth --- was slain to secure lasting peace.

Lady Gharbighast later bore a child with King Oceani and, despite Church pressure, remained loyal to the party's alliance.  The fishmen of New Abbergast joined the final battle.

\smallskip

\subsection*{Golding's Bay}

A town controlled by Freyer Thistlebloom, an SRA leader with magical abilities.  The party was ensorcelled within the local temple into slaughtering an entire village of innocent people.  The horror of this event drove Toni to surrender to the Arbiters in protest and ultimately to leave the group entirely.

\smallskip

\subsection*{The Varg Lands}

The frozen northern territory beyond Linderdorf.  A bitter, snowbound wasteland of roving goblin bands, ogres, and bearkin warriors.  The Varg Hold --- a massive tent city stretching for miles --- lies somewhere in the interior.  Children watch travellers from the hills, and giants roam near the mountains.

The party ventured into the Varg lands to rescue the gnome inventors and find the Bright Bart Forge.  It was here that Martin was found beaten and chained by bearkin, Kevin collapsed in the sub-zero temperatures, and a lightning bolt struck the airship.

\smallskip

\subsection*{Bright Bart Forge}

An ancient dwarven forge at the summit of a mountain in Varg territory, sealed for centuries by the Aspect of the Storm --- an air elemental wielding a lightning sword that had possessed the mountain.  The forge was the source of legendary weapons in ages past, and its rediscovery provided the party with the forging knowledge, rune-carving books, and tools needed to create their Excalibrum weapons.

Inside, the party found dwarven skeletons --- the last smiths had sealed themselves in when the Aspect took the mountain, and died of causes best not discussed in polite company.

\section{Nanduan} 

Nanduan is a large kingdom on the North-Eastern coast of the continent of Erassa. The capital city is located on the banks of a tidal river estuary, and maritime trade makes up a significant portion of the economy.

Nanduan does not trade significantly with Velarian cities, but has some relations with the elven city of A'kul'theok. There is limited migration from Nanduan to Church-controlled cities, and so not much is known about this land by commoners in these areas. Traders and travellers may have some patchy information. It is known that relations between Nanduan and Masuda, to the south, are strained, at best.
Known Inhabitants

    The Hearthrust Dynasty - A large, wealthy and prominent noble family. They recognise the danger of the isolation their social class gives them, and therefore the children of each generation are sent out into the world for a few years at the age of 18 to experience what life is like for the people below them. Some run small businesses, some join religious orders, but most use the opportunity to become adventurers or travelers. As most marriages within the family are arranged during the teenage years, some of the Hearthrust children are already betrothed by the time they begin their travels. In this case, the face of the Hearthrust daughters are covered, to save the world the pain of seeing, but not being able to attain, such beauty. Hearthrust girls are renowned in Naduan as being exceptionally beautiful.

    They are also seen as beautiful in their own country of Nanduan.

    An Unnamed Shipping Magnate - Known to be betrothed to Lady Otoria Hearthrust.

Flora and Fauna

    Sand Dagger - Dangerous and sneaky creatures known to hide underneath and behind things.

    Balloon Cow - Like cows, but inflatable.

\section{Masuda}

A kingdom to the east with a samurai culture, strained relations with Nanduan, and an ongoing civil war.  The party spent a significant portion of the campaign here, arriving by airship and hiring Daisuke as their interpreter and fixer.

Masuda was the site of the hunt for the Lord of Lightning (where Myron proved himself), the castle infiltration to rescue Romjiro (where Myron threw Julieto out of a window), the discovery of the Hammer of the Gods in the Golden Hall, and the alliance with the Hearthrust Society.  The civil war provided ample opportunity for the group to make friends, enemies, and a considerable amount of money.  Ruh'Breks Sensei sacrificed himself here, holding back a giant mountain creature to protect the airship as the party fled back to Velterra.

\subsection*{Akultheok}

The main port on South Africa, relatively friendly and very much like a Caribbean beach resort complete with great cocktails.  The surrounding jungles, however, are as dangerous as they come.

\smallskip

\subsection*{The Jungles}

Super-dangerous jungles full of tribes, trebuchets, and cannibal Tikki Tukks.  Hidden somewhere within was the Tower of Ruh'Breks, containing the Puzzle Die, the airship that would become The Gary, and the tower's caretaker --- Codie, a sentient stone servant, presiding over a load of dead servants and snakes.  It was in these jungles that Pilcheur met his end at the hands of a creature of the deep, Gary sacrificed himself protecting Kolo at the Tikituk Temple, and the party's course was changed forever.
